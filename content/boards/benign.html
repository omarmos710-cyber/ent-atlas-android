2Hoarse voice: what and when

  • Hoarse > three weeks (US guideline: four), or sooner with smoking, pain, dysphagia, otalgia, stridor, neck mass → laryngoscopy · urgent if over 45, unexplained (NICE)

3Phonomicrosurgery

  • Suspension microlaryngoscopy, cold steel or laser
  • Microflap: small upper-surface incision, dissect beneath, lay epithelium back · keep superficial lamina propria + ligament · voice therapy before + after
Never strip both sides of the anterior commissure at once → web

6Nodules

  • Singers', screamers', teachers' · free edge at anterior third / posterior two-thirds = midpoint of membranous fold, most collision
  • Oedema + bleed organise, hyalinise · soft red early → firm grey-white
  • Talkative children (boys), adult women · breathy tiring voice, lost upper range, neck ache
  • Strobe: hourglass closure, wave kept
  • Voice therapy first (reviews 2020: shrinks nodules) · children's mostly go · not shrinking after good therapy = not nodules · excise large fibrotic ones, spare ligament · steroid may help

7Polyp and Reinke's

  • Polyp: men thirty to fifty · shout → haemorrhage · smoking, allergy · haemorrhagic, gelatinous or fibrous · diplophonia · pedunculated: choking, stridor · excise + therapy
  • Reinke's: bilateral pale translucent membranous folds · smoking, abuse, reflux, hypothyroid · deep rough voice (women mistaken for men on the phone) · stop smoking, treat reflux + thyroid, therapy → decortication, one side at a time

8Granulomas

  • Contact: vocal processes hammer · hard attacks, low forceful speech, clearing · reflux, intubation
  • Hoarse, pain to the ear, lump, clearing · red arytenoids
  • Intubation: rough, large or long tube · often bilateral, posterior · many drop off · treat as contact
StepTreatment
First linePPI twice daily (omeprazole 20–40 mg) even without heartburn + alginate after meals, bedtime · 3 months → review
Allvoice therapy: stop clearing, hard attack
Persistentbotox to adductors (low dose) · inhaled/injected steroid
Surgeryonly airway, doubt, failure · recurs · histology (carcinoma, granular cell)

9The white patch

  • Leukoplakia/keratosis in smokers: keratosis → dysplasia → CIS → cancer = precancerous
  • Biopsy or excisional microflap · stop smoking, treat reflux · follow up · no repeated deep stripping
Keratotic or red, reduced wave, irregular, poor mobility → biopsy, don't watch
Amyloid: men fifty to seventy · yellow-orange submucosal mass · Congo red apple-green · exclude myeloma, systemic · endoscopic removal

10Cysts, laryngocele, sulcus

  • Ductal (vallecula, AE fold, ventricle) · epidermoid (white, deep, against ligament) · mucous · microflap, ideally intact
  • Saccular cyst (mucus) · laryngocele (air): internal, external (through thyrohyoid membrane, swells with Valsalva), combined · wind players, glass blowers · CT · endoscopic for internal, external excision
  • Adult: look for a ventricular carcinoma blocking the saccule
  • Sulcus: lamina propria missing · breathy, spindle gap · palpate at MLS · therapy first · injection, fat/fascia, slicing, medialisation: limited

11Papillomatosis

  • ~80% of benign laryngeal neoplasms · HPV 6 + 11
  • Juvenile: from maternal warts at birth (first-born, young mother, vaginal; caesarean no guarantee) · two to five years · hoarse, weak cry → stridor · called asthma or croup · squamo-ciliary junctions → trachea, bronchi
  • Adult: men thirty to fifty, anterior fold · rare malignancy (HPV 16 + 18, smoking, RT)
StepTreatment
Primarydebulk, don't cure: microdebrider, CO₂ or KTP · spare ligament + commissure · repeat · office KTP
Frequent recurrenceintralesional cidofovir (off-label) · indole-3-carbinol, interferon
Severe, distalsystemic bevacizumab IV ~5–10 mg/kg every 2–3 weeks · relapses on stopping · blood pressure, urine protein
PreventionHPV vaccine (9-valent) · Australia: juvenile cases falling
Avoid tracheostomy: spreads it down the trachea
Bevacizumab (2021): all improved, more than half no further surgery · no adjuvant licensed

12Other neoplasms

  • Chondroma: posterior cricoid, men forty to sixty · calcified on CT · biopsy vs chondrosarcoma · laryngofissure, keep cricoid
  • Haemangioma: infant subglottic (propranolol) · adult cavernous
  • Granular cell: Schwann · pseudoepitheliomatous hyperplasia mimics carcinoma

13Exam traps ✗

  • Operating on nodules before proper therapy
  • Stripping both sides of the anterior commissure
  • Watching a smoker's white patch, no biopsy
  • Child with papillomas called asthmatic
