2Paraganglioma: origin

  • Neural-crest glomus bodies on Jacobson's (IX tympanic), Arnold's (X auricular), dome of the jugular bulb · benign, slow, very vascular
  • Fourth–fifth decades · women ~5 : 1
  • Zellballen: chief cells + sustentacular cells
  • SDHD: multiple tumours, only when inherited from the father · SDHB: higher malignancy risk · ~a third hereditary → test every patient, screen relatives

3The glomus in clinic

Red mass rising from the floor of the middle ear behind the drum · Brown's sign: pneumatic pressure → pulsates, then blanches · bruit over mastoid · late: bleeding polyp in canal
  • Pulsatile tinnitus ~80% · conductive loss ~60% · fullness · later bleeding
  • Jugulare: IX–XII palsies (hoarseness, dysphagia, tongue + trapezius wasting), facial weakness
  • Pain points to carcinoma more than glomus
Never biopsy in clinic → catastrophic bleeding · hypertension, headache, palpitations, sweating → metanephrines before any anaesthetic

4Imaging & staging

  • CT: erosion of the caroticojugular spine = jugulare · excludes aberrant carotid, high bulb
  • MRI: salt-and-pepper (flow voids), intracranial + neck extent
  • Angiography: feeders (mainly ascending pharyngeal), multiple tumours, embolisation · balloon occlusion test if carotid at risk

6Treating the glomus

  • Surgery for most: embolise 24–48 h before · alpha-blockade if secreting
  • Small tympanicum, whole rim visible → transcanal · larger → hypotympanic, extended facial recess, mastoid-neck · jugulare → Fisch infratemporal fossa
  • Radiotherapy / SRS: growth arrested ~90–94% (control ~95% for jugular in meta-analysis), fewer new nerve palsies → elderly, unfit, residual, or when surgery would cost lower nerves
  • Observe slow tumours in the elderly

7Squamous cell carcinoma

Age 40–60 · ~three-quarters have long chronic discharge
  • Bloodstained foul discharge, deep boring night pain, friable granulation / polyp that bleeds · later facial palsy, vertigo, trismus, neck / parotid mass
  • Mistaken for COM or OE → biopsy any non-healing granulation
  • CT temporal bone + neck · MRI soft tissue + dura · chest imaging · modified Pittsburgh staging

9Exam traps ✗

  • Biopsying a red mass behind the drum
  • Months of "COM" for a bleeding, painful ear
  • No catecholamines before surgery
