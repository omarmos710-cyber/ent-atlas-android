CHOLESTEATOMA

skin in the wrong place ✎

1What it is

  • Keratinising stratified squamous epithelium in the middle ear cleft, keratin in its centre (3-D)
  • Matrix = living wall on inflamed perimatrix
  • No cholesterol, not a tumour (keratoma)
= the unsafe ear: ossicles, labyrinth, facial canal, dural & sinus plates

2Where it comes from

TypeHow
Congenitalwhite mass behind intact drum, usually anterosuperior, child, no OM · epithelial rests (also petrous apex, CPA)
Primary acquired (commonest)pars flaccida retraction → attic → keratin builds, neck narrows → self-perpetuating sac
Secondary acquiredthrough marginal / posterosuperior perforation, or implanted (trauma, surgery)
Theories: cell rests (congenital) · Wittmaack invagination · Ruedi basal cell hyperplasia · Habermann migration · Sade metaplasia

Attic ventilation

via narrow anterior + posterior tympanic isthmus · blocked → attic + Prussak's isolated → pars flaccida retracts while mesotympanum is normal

3Pars tensa retraction

I not touching incus→II touches incus→III on promontory, lifts with suction→IV adherent, no space
III = atelectasis · IV = adhesive otitis

4How it destroys and spreads

Erosion: collagenases from matrix + perimatrix, cytokine-driven osteoclasts, infection · mastoid usually sclerotic · pressure: Lee yes, Dhingra no
Prussak's→attic→aditus→antrum, mastoid
or posteriorly→sinus tympani, facial recess
Residual hides in: sinus tympani · facial recess · under the scutum · anterior epitympanic recess

5The scanty, foul ear

  • Discharge scanty, foul, ± blood
  • Conductive or mixed · cholesteatoma hearer: bridges a broken chain → warn hearing may worsen after surgery
  • Signs: attic / posterosuperior marginal perforation · white flakes / pearl · eroded scutum or canal wall · red fleshy polyp · fistula test + in some
  • Blue drum + dark fluid → cholesterol granuloma, not cholesteatoma
Discharge suddenly stops = pus may track inwards · ask headache, fever, vertigo, facial weakness · complications: earache, vomiting, neck stiffness, diplopia, unsteadiness, listless child

6Map it before you operate

  • Microscope + suction: keratin, defect, polyps
  • Fistula test (tragus / Siegle): vertigo + nystagmus · specific but positive in only 30–50% · every case
  • Audiogram: conductive + SN parts
  • HRCT: extent; scutum, ossicles, tegmen, lateral canal, facial canal, sigmoid plate
  • Non-EPI DWI MRI: keratin bright · ~90% sens + spec · misses pearls <~3 mm · residual after CWU; can replace a low-suspicion second look (not a high one)
  • 0° endoscope in clinic: depth of a pocket + photos over time

8Newer options

  • Obliteration (bone pâté, muscle, bioactive glass + cartilage wall): ~40% of open cavities discharge / can't take water · avoids cavity, ↓ recidivism · single-stage series: recurrent 4.6%, residual 5.4%
  • Endoscopic: limited attic / mesotympanic disease, sees sinus tympani + anterior attic · antrum/mastoid → combined · one-handed, bleeding, heat · finds residual in 17–38% after "clear" microscopy

9Lateral canal fistula

7–10% of cholesteatomas, 90% lateral canal, only ~60% seen on CT → assume one in every case
  • Small: matrix off at the end, seal it
  • Large / only hearing ear: leave matrix, convert to CWD
  • Endosteum tears → cover at once
  • Never strip matrix off a cochlear fistula

10Staging & follow-up

EAONO/JOS: I one site · II ≥2 sites · III extracranial complication · IV intracranial · difficult sites: sinus tympani, protympanum
Residual (left behind) vs recurrent (new retraction) → follow ≥ five years, longer in children · ossiculoplasty at first stage or second look

11Complications

Intratemporal: incus first · fistula, labyrinthitis · facial palsy (tympanic segment) · mastoiditis, abscess
Intracranial: extradural, subdural, meningitis, brain abscess, lateral sinus thrombosis, otitic hydrocephalus
Chronic ear + new vertigo, facial weakness, headache, fever, neck stiffness = complication until proven otherwise → VII + fistula test → CT (± MRI) → IV antibiotics, senior
Traps: drops on an attic crust never lifted · no fistula test · CWU in a patient who won't attend
