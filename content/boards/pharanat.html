APPLIED ANATOMY OF THE PHARYNX

a shared corridor for air and food · three parts, three constrictors, one weak spot ✎

1A fibromuscular funnel

  • 12–14 cm, skull base (basisphenoid, basiocciput) → lower border of cricoid at the sixth cervical vertebra
  • 3.5 cm wide above → 1.5 cm at the pharyngo-oesophageal junction: narrowest gut after the appendix → foreign bodies lodge

Layers, in → out

mucosa (ciliated in nasopharynx, squamous below) → pharyngobasilar fascia (thick at skull base, fills gap above superior constrictor) → muscle → buccopharyngeal fascia

2Three constrictors, three elevators

  • Into midline raphe, lower over upper like stacked cups · pharyngeal plexus (X)
  • Superior: hamulus, raphe, mandible · middle: hyoid, stylohyoid ligament
  • Inferior: thyroid + cricoid → oblique thyropharyngeus + transverse cricopharyngeus = upper oesophageal sphincter, closed at rest
  • Stylopharyngeus: only muscle by IX · salpingopharyngeus: tube cartilage · palatopharyngeus: posterior pillar + Passavant's ridge · all elevate pharynx + larynx

4Three parts

schematic · each part gives rise to its own cancers

5Waldeyer's ring

  • Adenoids (roof–posterior wall) · palatine (between pillars) · lingual (base of tongue) · tubal · lateral bands · posterior wall nodules
  • B cells (germinal centres) → secretory IgA · T cells (parafollicular) · plasma cells IgM, IgG · most active in childhood
  • No lasting immune deficit after tonsils + adenoids removed

6Nasopharynx

  • Roof + back: basisphenoid, basiocciput → atlas · floor: soft palate + isthmus · front: choanae
  • Lateral: tube opening ~1.25 cm behind inferior turbinate, torus tubarius · behind it fossa of Rosenmüller: commonest NPC site · salpingopharyngeal fold
  • Adenoids grow to about six, then atrophy · bursa (notochord): Thornwaldt abscess or cyst · Rathke's pouch: craniopharyngioma
  • Lymph: node of Rouvière (retropharyngeal), parapharyngeal → upper deep cervical + spinal accessory, often both sides
  • Airway, middle-ear ventilation, velopharyngeal closure, resonance (hyponasal if blocked, hypernasal with VPI), mucus drainage

7Oropharynx

  • Hard palate → hyoid · opens through the oropharyngeal isthmus
  • Front: base of tongue, lingual tonsil, valleculae (retention cysts) · side: tonsil between pillars · back: C2, C3 · roof: soft palate undersurface
  • Epiglottis tip + pharyngoepiglottic folds separate it from hypopharynx

8Hypopharynx

  • Hyoid → lower cricoid, in front of C3–C6 · three subsites: piriform, postcricoid, posterior wall
  • Piriform sinus: lateral food channel where fish bones lodge · thyrohyoid membrane + thyroid lateral, aryepiglottic fold + arytenoid medial
  • Internal laryngeal nerve under its mucosa: anaesthetise there · cancer → earache
  • Postcricoid: cancer in women with Plummer–Vinson · posterior wall: hyoid → cricoarytenoid joints
  • Upper epiglottis + pharyngoepiglottic folds = its upper border with the oropharynx
  • Piriform lymph pierces thyrohyoid membrane → upper jugular: presents with neck nodes · posterior wall → retro- + parapharyngeal · postcricoid → paratracheal, supraclavicular

9Nerves, vessels, spaces

  • Motor: pharyngeal plexus (X) except stylopharyngeus (IX) + tensor palati (V3)
  • Sensory: naso V2 · oro IX (gag afferent) · hypo internal laryngeal (X)
  • Referred otalgia: IX → Jacobson's, X → Arnold's · tonsillitis, quinsy, post-tonsillectomy, tonsil, base of tongue, piriform cancer
  • Ascending pharyngeal, facial (tonsillar, ascending palatine), maxillary, lingual, inferior thyroid · veins → pterygoid plexus, IJV
  • Retropharyngeal: skull base → superior mediastinum (≈T2; tracheal bifurcation in some texts), nodes regress after childhood · parapharyngeal: inverted pyramid, post-styloid = carotid, IJV, last four cranial nerves, sympathetic · pre/post-styloid predicts tumour type

10Swallowing

oral→pharyngeal→oesophageal
  • Oral voluntary · pharyngeal reflex, under a second · oesophageal wave three to six seconds
  • Bolus touches anterior pillars (IX, X) → reflex · breathing stops
  • Palate meets Passavant's ridge · glottis + false folds close, epiglottis tilts · larynx + hyoid up and forward · tongue base drives back, constrictors top-down
  • Cricopharyngeus relaxes + is pulled open by laryngeal elevation: the key factor
  • Sensation V, IX, X · muscles V, VII, IX, X, XII
  • Incoordination → pouch · HPV cancer: tonsil + tongue base crypts → small primary, cystic nodes, better prognosis

11Exam traps ✗

  • Forgetting the exceptions to vagal supply: stylopharyngeus (IX), tensor (V3)
  • Valleculae in the hypopharynx: they are oropharynx
  • Otalgia + normal drum: looking only at the ear
  • Uvula deviates away from the vagal palsy, not towards it
