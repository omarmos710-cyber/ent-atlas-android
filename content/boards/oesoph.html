OESOPHAGUS & DYSPHAGIA

where is it, solids or liquids, how fast · progressive dysphagia + weight loss in an older smoker = cancer until scoped ✎

1A 25 cm muscular tube

At the narrowings: foreign bodies lodge, caustic burns worst, instruments perforate

2Wall, supply, sphincters

  • Squamous mucosa · submucosa (Meissner) · circular + longitudinal muscle (Auerbach) · adventitia, no serosa → leaks spread, anastomoses fragile
  • Striated upper third, mixed, smooth lower → skeletal muscle disease hits top, scleroderma bottom
  • Inferior thyroid · aorta · left gastric + inferior phrenic · lower end portosystemic (varices) · lymph cervical, mediastinal, coeliac → skip lesions
  • UOS = cricopharyngeus, 3–5 cm, closed at rest · Killian's dehiscence above, Laimer's triangle below · LOS: physiological · crura, angle of His, folds, abdominal pressure

3Swallowing · terms

  • Oral voluntary → bolus touches faucial pillars, triggers reflex
  • Pharyngeal < a second: palate to Passavant's ridge · larynx closes below upwards · larynx rises → pulls UOS open (the key) · constrictors contract, cricopharyngeus relaxes
  • Oesophageal 3–8 s · primary (swallow), secondary (distension), tertiary (non-propulsive: elderly, spasm)

Terms · causes by site

  • Odynophagia = ulcer, inflammation, infection · globus = lump between meals, food goes down
  • Lumen: bolus, FB · wall: oesophagitis, stricture, web, ring, tumour, motility · outside: pouch, goitre, DISH osteophytes, nodes, left atrium, aneurysm, vascular ring (lusoria)

6Oesophagoscopy

  • Rigid (GA): sharp/impacted FB, upper biopsy, dilatation, pouch, panendoscopy · not with bad neck, trismus, receding jaw, aneurysm
  • Sniffing position, protect teeth · right of tongue → behind cricoid · gentle sustained lift, never force · aortic pulsation 25 cm · red gastric mucosa = cardia
  • Flexible: topical ± sedation, to duodenum, safe in neck disease, may miss postcricoid · transnasal: unsedated office, Barrett's, second primary, TEP
  • Food bolus: scope within 24 h (sooner if can't swallow saliva) · no fizzy drinks or glucagon instead

7Reflux and inflammation

  • GORD: weak/relaxing LOS, hiatus hernia, obesity, smoking, CCB, nitrates · LPR: hoarseness, clearing, globus, cough, posterior laryngitis, granuloma
  • Lifestyle (no food 3 h before bed) → omeprazole 20 mg or lansoprazole 30 mg daily before breakfast, 4 weeks (8 if severe) → lowest dose · not settling: double PPI or famotidine 20–40 mg at night · Nissen if refractory
  • LPR: alginate 10 ml after meals + bedtime · PPI (omeprazole 20–40 mg twice daily) only with typical reflux, 8–12 weeks · weak evidence
  • Barrett's: salmon-pink intestinal metaplasia → adenocarcinoma · surveil by length + dysplasia (BSG 2014)
  • Infective: candida (plaques, fluconazole) · HSV (punched-out, aciclovir) · CMV (large shallow, ganciclovir)
  • EoE: rings, furrows · ≥ 15 eosinophils/HPF, two levels · omeprazole 20–40 mg twice daily 8–12 weeks · budesonide orodispersible 1 mg twice daily 6 weeks · 2- to 6-food diet · dilate · dupilumab 300 mg SC weekly
  • Pill (doxycycline, KCl, iron, NSAIDs, bisphosphonates) lodges at the aortic narrowing

8Caustic injury

Alkali: liquefactive, deep, oesophagus · acid: coagulative, limited, stomach (pyloric stenosis)
necrosis→slough 2–3 wk (weakest)→stricture→SCC
  • Airway first · clean lips ≠ safe oesophagus · films, CT · gentle scope 12–48 h (avoid days 5–15) · Zargar: 0–2a heal, 2b+ stricture
  • No vomiting, no neutralising, no blind NG · NBM, IV fluids, analgesia, IV PPI (omeprazole 40 mg) · NG under vision/gastrostomy · antibiotics if deep · steroids not routine · resect full-thickness necrosis
  • Stricture: dilate from ~3 weeks · retrograde over a string · colon/stomach replacement
  • Dhingra: neutralise within six hours, steroids from 48–96 h for four to six weeks · current: neither (heat, re-exposure, no proven benefit)

9Perforation

  • Instruments commonest (Killian's, distal) · FB, trauma, tumour · Boerhaave: vomiting, left lower · ≠ Mallory–Weiss
  • Cervical: neck pain, emphysema · thoracic: interscapular pain, shock, Hamman's crunch · Mackler's triad: vomiting, chest pain, emphysema
  • NBM, nutrition, piperacillin–tazobactam 4.5 g IV 8-hourly, fluconazole 400 mg IV (higher risk), IV PPI · cervical contained: conservative ± drain · thoracic: repair within ~24 h, or stent/clips
Pain, fever, tachycardia, crepitus after scope or dilatation = perforation until proved otherwise

10Motility

  • Achalasia: lost inhibitory myenteric neurons → aperistalsis, LOS won't relax · solids + liquids from start · bird's beak, no gastric bubble · HRM, Chicago I–III · exclude pseudoachalasia · Chagas · SCC risk
  • Fit: pneumatic dilatation, Heller + partial fundoplication, or POEM (favoured in type III) · frail: botulinum 100 units into LOS · bridge: nifedipine 10–30 mg SL before meals
  • Distal spasm: angina-like, corkscrew · nitrates, CCB · jackhammer: strong, peristaltic
  • Scleroderma: lower two-thirds aperistaltic, weak LOS (CREST) · myositis: upper
  • CP dysfunction: posterior bar (up to a third of healthy elderly) · dilate, botox, myotomy (not with severe reflux)

11Structural · congenital

  • Plummer–Vinson: women, iron deficiency, postcricoid web · ferrous sulfate 200 mg daily (or alternate days), 3 months after Hb normal · dilate, watch: cancer ~10%
  • Schatzki: over 50 · < 13 mm → steakhouse · dilate + PPI · diverticula: Zenker (pulsion), mid (traction, TB), epiphrenic
  • Hiatus: sliding ~90% · rolling strangulates → repair · stricture: biopsy every one, bougie/balloon
  • Atresia + distal TOF ~85%: chokes at first feed, NG coils 9–13 cm, VACTERL · H-type ~4% · aberrant right subclavian: non-recurrent laryngeal nerve

12Tumours

  • Leiomyoma ~two-thirds of benign · EUS, no mucosal biopsy
  • SCC (India, worldwide; mid + upper): tobacco, alcohol, hot drinks, achalasia, caustic, P–V, tylosis · adenocarcinoma (West; distal): Barrett's, obesity
  • Solids → liquids + weight loss · hoarse = RLN · cough on swallow = TOF · apple-core · CT, PET-CT, EUS
  • Early: endoscopic resection · CROSS CRT or perioperative chemo → oesophagectomy · definitive CRT for (cervical) SCC · stent palliates · five-year ~15–20%

13Exam traps ✗

  • "Globus" in progressive dysphagia, no scope · forcing a rigid scope · missed perforation
  • Achalasia without excluding cardia cancer · vomiting or neutralising after caustic
