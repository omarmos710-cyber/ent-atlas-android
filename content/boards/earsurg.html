2Myringotomy & grommets

  • AOM with bulging painful drum or complication · incomplete resolution · recurrent AOM · OME · barotrauma · atelectasis · intratympanic access
  • Site: posteroinferior for pus · anteroinferior / inferior for effusion + tubes · anterosuperior tube stays longest
  • Risks: ossicles or high jugular bulb · persistent hole · blockage, extrusion · otorrhoea, granuloma, tympanosclerosis
  • US 2022: grommets for bilateral OME ≥ three months with hearing loss · NICE NG233 (2023): grommets or aids after three months' watchful waiting
Look for a high bulb or aberrant carotid before cutting the drum

3Myringoplasty

Repair of the perforation alone, middle ear not inspected (≠ type I tympanoplasty, which examines it) · grafts: temporalis fascia (commonest), tragal perichondrium, cartilage (resists retraction), vein

5Ossicular reconstruction

  • Commonest defect: incus long process / IS joint (COM, retraction) · trauma: incus · gap >~30 dB = good indication
  • Stapes present: incus interposition, PORP, bone cement (small incus gap), myringostapediopexy · footplate only: TORP / columella ± footplate shoe
  • Titanium / hydroxyapatite + cartilage cap against extrusion · PORP hears better than TORP
  • Gap <20 dB: ~60–90% with a stapes, 30–80% without · repeated failure → active middle ear implant
  • Timing: often ~six months later at the second look (CWU cholesteatoma); primary after trauma / fixation

7Which, and why

  • Cortical: coalescent / masked mastoiditis · access for sac surgery, VII decompression, implants, translab
  • MRM: attic + antrum cholesteatoma · radical: uncleared disease, failed surgery, tumours
  • Every CWD cavity needs a wide meatoplasty · obliteration cuts discharge; never bury disease

8Complications & care

  • Facial nerve · lateral canal fistula (vertigo, loss) · dura + CSF leak · sigmoid bleeding · incus dislocation · ~a quarter of radical cavities keep discharging
  • Before: audiogram + forks · CT for cholesteatoma, revision, complications · check the other ear + tube
  • After: water out while healing · review at three and six weeks · graft epithelialised in six to eight weeks · cavities cleaned every four to six months in year one, then yearly
  • Endoscopic ear surgery: better view of hidden areas; long-term evidence limited

9Exam traps ✗

  • Repairing a drum while the ear still discharges
  • Körner's septum taken for the antrum floor
  • Burying cholesteatoma under an obliteration
