THE THYROID

the whole topic on one board ✎

1Applied anatomy

  • Berry's ligament → gland rises on swallowing. Commonest site of RLN injury
  • RLN: R oblique, L vertical in TO groove; crosses ITA (any way); enters behind inferior cornu
  • Find it in the RLN triangle or at Zuckerkandl
  • Non-recurrent nerve: R only, 0.5–1% → aberrant subclavian (dysphagia lusoria)
  • EBSLN → cricothyroid (high pitch): tie sup. vessels on capsule
  • Ansa from below → cut straps high

Embryology

foramen caecum (wk 4) → thyroglossal tract → neck (wk 7)
• stops → lingual thyroid
• pyramidal lobe ≤40%
• C cells: upper/middle ⅓ → MTC

Vessels & lymph

Sup. thyroid a. = 1st ECA branch
Inf. thyroid a. (thyrocervical) → parathyroids
Middle vein = first divided
Lymph: VI → VII & II–IV
Lingual thyroid (1 in 3000–4000, women): scan + US before any treatment — may be the only thyroid!

2Physiology & tests

trap I⁻→TPO: MIT/DIT→couple T4/T3→colloid→release
  • TRH → TSH → T4/T3 ⟲ negative feedback
  • Gland makes mostly T4 (t½ 1 wk); T3 (t½ 1 day) made peripherally
  • TSH = best single screen
  • TPO Ab → Hashimoto
  • TRAb → Graves
  • Tg → recurrence (after total + ablation)
  • Calcitonin + CEA → MTC

6Treating differentiated cancer

surgery→± RAI→TSH suppression→Tg + US follow-up
  • Lobectomy: low-risk, intrathyroidal, ≤4 cm, N0 · micro-PTC <1 cm → surveillance option · NIFTP = benign
  • Total: bigger, ETE, nodes, metastases, RAI planned
  • Neck: therapeutic level VI; lateral = compartmental II–IV, never berry-picking
  • Nerve: working + shaved = keep · paralysed/encased = resect
  • RAI: high risk yes · low risk can omit (ESTIMABL2) · low dose = high dose
  • TSH: <0.1 high risk · low-normal low risk · re-grade at 9–12 months
  • Rising Tg = recurrence; Tg+ iodine− → PET-CT

7Thyroidectomy

Indications = 4 Cs + 1: cancer, suspicion, compression, cosmesis + hyperthyroid · Pre-op: euthyroid, Ca/vit D, check cords, US/FNA, CT if retrosternal

8Exam traps ✗

  • MTC surgery without excluding phaeo
  • Cystic lateral neck node in a young adult = papillary until proven otherwise (not "branchial cyst")
  • Excising a lingual thyroid without a scan
  • Iodinated contrast just before RAI
  • Not checking the cords before and after
