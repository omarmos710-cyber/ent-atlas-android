2The common cold

  • Rhino-, adeno-, entero-, coronaviruses · droplets · incubation one to four days · lasts two to three weeks
  • Burning in the back of the nose → stuffiness, sneezing, watery drip, chills · mucopurulent if bacteria follow
  • Rest, fluids, non-aspirin analgesia (aspirin ↑ viral shedding), antihistamine, short decongestant · antibiotics only for complications
  • Complications: sinusitis, tonsillitis, otitis media, bronchitis, pneumonia
  • Influenza more severe · measles, rubella, chickenpox: rhinitis two to three days before the rash · COVID-19: sudden loss of smell + taste

3Bacterial & irritative

  • Non-specific: children, pneumococcus, strep, staph · grey-white membrane that bleeds
  • Diphtheritic: rare now · grey, tenacious membrane over turbinate + floor, bleeds when lifted, sore nostrils + lip · isolate, penicillin or erythromycin + antitoxin
  • Irritative: dust, smoke, ammonia, fumes, instruments · settles once removed

  • Simple: infected sinuses, tonsils, adenoids · smoke, dust · septal deviation · vasomotor · hypothyroid · block on the dependent side lying down, thick discharge, postnasal drip, headache
  • Hypertrophic: mucosa, glands, periosteum + bone thicken, mostly inferior turbinate · whole, front, back or lower border → mulberry
  • Reduce: cautery, submucosal diathermy, cryo, laser, coblation, radiofrequency, submucous bone resection, partial turbinectomy
  • Compensatory hypertrophy on the wide side of a bent septum: doesn't reverse → reduce at septoplasty
Too much turbinate removed → crusts, empty nose

5Rhinitis sicca & caseosa

  • Sicca: hot, dry, dusty jobs (bakers, ironsmiths, goldsmiths) · anterior septum only · crusts → ulcer, bleed, perforation · change workplace, ointment, douche, stop picking
  • Caseosa: rare, one side, men · offensive cheesy pus from chronic sinusitis · bone erosion mimics cancer · clear it out + drain the sinus · good outlook

  • Columnar → squamous · glands, sinusoids, nerves atrophy · endarteritis · turbinate bone resorbed · sinuses small
  • Young woman from puberty · foul smell she can't smell: merciful anosmia · paradoxical obstruction · bleeds when crusts lift
  • Green-black crusts · posterior nasopharynx easily seen · perforation, saddle · atrophic pharynx + larynx, glue ear · improves in middle age

7Secondary atrophic

  • Syphilis, lupus, leprosy, rhinoscleroma · long purulent sinusitis · radiotherapy
  • Over-resected turbinates: empty nose syndrome → why reduction now spares mucosa
  • One-sided on the wide side of a bent septum

8Treating ozaena

  • Douche: warm saline or alkaline (bicarbonate, biborate, chloride) two or three times a day, then less · lift hard crusts
  • 25% glucose in glycerine painted on: stops the proteolytic organisms that smell
  • Antibiotics topical or 7–10 days systemic (quinolone + metronidazole) for active infection · not long-term
  • Potassium iodide · diet rich in A, D, folate, iron, protein

9Evidence

  • Colds (Cochrane): antibiotics no benefit (2013) · decongestants small benefit in adults (2016) · antihistamine–decongestant small benefit · vitamin C after onset: little or none
  • Ozaena: case series only · douching + crust removal = mainstay · topical aminoglycoside drops for Klebsiella · surgery if severe

10Exam traps ✗

  • Klebsiella ozaenae = secondary invader, not the cause
  • Removing too much turbinate
  • Destroyed nose: granulomatous or lymphoma?
  • Simple vs hypertrophic: probe + vasoconstrictor
