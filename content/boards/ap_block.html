APPROACH TO NASAL OBSTRUCTION

inflammatory (fluctuating, bilateral) or structural (fixed, one-sided)? · treat the mucosa before the skeleton · never miss the one-sided bloody nose ✎

1How the nose blocks

  • About a third of adults · nose = about two-thirds of airway resistance
  • Internal valve: septum + caudal upper lateral + head of inferior turbinate · narrowest point · Poiseuille: small narrowing, big symptom · rich in receptors
  • Nasal cycle: ~80%, alternates every two to six hours · unmasks a fixed deviation
  • Airflow felt by trigeminal cold receptors → menthol "clears", symptoms ≠ measurements, empty nose feels blocked

2Red flags

  • One-sided + blood, pain, swelling, numbness, loose teeth, diplopia, neck node → sinonasal or NPC cancer
  • Adolescent boy + heavy epistaxis → angiofibroma: no clinic biopsy
  • Child, one-sided foul discharge → foreign body
  • Newborn, cyclical cyanosis relieved by crying → bilateral choanal atresia
  • Midline mass in a child → dermoid, glioma, encephalocele: image first

3History

  • Fixed, one-sided → septum, valve, polyp, tumour, FB · fluctuating, both → rhinitis, CRS
  • Onset after trauma or surgery · nostril collapses on sniffing → valve
  • Sneeze, itch, water · smell loss, pressure, discharge · bleeding, crusting · snoring
  • Drugs: decongestant sprays, antihypertensives, OCP, cocaine · asthma, aspirin, smoking, job
  • NOSE (five items), SNOT-22

5Pathway

Red flag? scope, MRI + CT, biopsy (not JNA) · boy: contrast first · child: remove FB Fluctuating → treat mucosa · polyps, smell → CRS pathway · fixed → step 3 Where? mid/posterior septum → septoplasty · high/caudal, valve → septorhinoplasty · turbinates → medical then reduce Still blocked? revision · wide + paradoxical → empty nose, no more resection

6Cottle's areas

Valve or caudal deviation → standard septoplasty fails → functional septorhinoplasty (spreaders, struts, alar grafts)

7Examine and measure

  • Outside: deviation, twisted pyramid, narrow ala, caudal dislocation · watch alae on deep breath
  • Cottle's test: pull cheek laterally, better = valve · modified: support from inside
  • Endoscope before and after decongestant: shrinks = mucosa · deviation, spur, polyp unchanged · meatal pus, choanae, nasopharynx
  • PNIF ~120–140 L/min · rhinomanometry = reference · acoustic rhinometry · CT pre-op, MRI for tumour

8Treat

  • Mucosa first: saline + steroid spray (head forward, nozzle away from septum) for six to eight weeks · antihistamine, avoidance, leukotriene antagonist (selected), immunotherapy
  • Decongestant spray a few days only → medicamentosa
  • Septoplasty beats medical (2019; NAIROS 2024) · L-strut ≥ 1 cm
  • Turbinates: submucosal diathermy, radiofrequency, outfracture, SMR · fades · mucosa-sparing · avoid over-resection
  • Objective tests correlate only loosely with symptoms

9Children

  • Adenoids commonest: mouth breathing, snoring, hyponasal
  • One-sided foul = FB until proved otherwise
  • Bilateral atresia: oral airway, CT, endoscopic repair · unilateral presents later
  • Piriform aperture stenosis, lacrimal cysts, midline masses → image first

10Exam traps ✗

  • Septoplasty for untreated rhinitis
  • Standard septoplasty for high or caudal deviation
  • Clinic biopsy of a vascular mass in a boy
  • Over-reducing the turbinates
