APPROACH TO LOSS OF SMELL

conductive (can't reach the cleft) or sensorineural (receptors, pathways)? · look in the olfactory cleft · measure it · train the nose ✎

1Why it matters

  • Lost warning for smoke, gas, spoiled food
  • Most "taste" is retronasal smell → patients complain of taste loss
  • Mood, relationships · in older people predicts neurodegeneration and death
  • Anosmia total · hyposmia reduced · parosmia distorted (often regeneration, fair prognosis) · phantosmia phantom

2Where it fails

Receptors regenerate from basal stem cells → recovery possible

3Red flags

  • One-sided + block or bleeding → sinonasal tumour, olfactory neuroblastoma
  • Headache, personality, vision → olfactory groove meningioma
  • Brief phantosmia episodes → temporal lobe seizures
  • Older adult, tremor, memory → Parkinson's, dementia (smell goes years first)
  • Absent from birth + delayed puberty → Kallmann

4History and testing

  • Fluctuating, better with steroids → conductive (CRSwNP) · sudden after a cold, COVID-19 or blow → sensorineural
  • Distortions, phantoms, nose, smoking, chemicals, drugs, neurology, family · safety at home
  • Endoscope the olfactory clefts (may be blocked when the rest looks normal) · cranial nerves
  • Measure, don't estimate: UPSIT (40 items), Sniffin' Sticks (TDI) · below chance = malingering
  • MRI: unexplained, one-sided, neurology (bulb volume) · CT: CRS or mass

6Viral and traumatic

  • Post-viral: commonest sensorineural · COVID-19: sudden complete, most recover within weeks, a minority lasting loss + parosmia
  • Trauma: fibres sheared, bulbs/frontal lobes bruised · only about a third to a half improve over years

7Treat

  • Conductive: saline, steroid drops head-down, short oral steroid (also a test) · ESS opens the clefts · dupilumab in severe polyps
  • Olfactory training: four strong odours (rose, lemon, eucalyptus, clove), about 20 seconds each, twice a day, three to six months · change odours every few months
  • Early oral steroid after viral/trauma · topical if cleft swollen · omega-3, vitamin A, PRP: limited
  • Safety: smoke and gas detectors, food dates, hygiene · parosmia = regeneration
  • 2023 position paper: test, scope, MRI if unclear · train · no routine systemic steroids outside CRS

8Pathway

Red flags: scope, image, biopsy · neurology → MRI Cleft: polyps/oedema → steroids, CRS pathway · normal → sensorineural Measure: confirm, find cause · below chance → non-organic Sensorineural: training, early steroids, safety, MRI if no cause

9Exam traps ✗

  • Olfactory clefts not examined
  • "Nothing can be done" after viral loss
  • One-sided tumour behind "polyps"
