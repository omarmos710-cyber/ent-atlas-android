1Three fossae

  • Anterior: cribriform (thinnest) + lateral lamella (CSF leaks) · crista galli in front, planum behind · fovea ethmoidalis, ethmoidal arteries across the roof
  • Central: sphenoid, sella, cavernous sinus: III, IV, V1, V2 in the wall · VI free beside the ICA → fails first · clivus: dorsum sellae → foramen magnum (basisphenoid above, basiocciput below)
  • From the sphenoid: carotid + optic bulges, opticocarotid recess, carotid bone dehiscent in some · Onodi cell may hold the optic nerve
  • Lateral: petrous temporal bone · apex (arcuate eminence, GSPN, Kawase's triangle) · IAC on the back

3Nerves, images, tissue

  • Adult one-sided glue ear → look at the nasopharynx · pulsatile tinnitus → vascular tumour · numb V2 / V3 = perineural
  • CT bone · MRI dura, orbit, nerves (dura > 5 mm = invaded) · balloon test occlusion · PET-CT
  • No biopsy: paraganglioma, schwannoma, meningioma, angiofibroma · unresectable: brainstem, cord, both ICAs / cavernous sinuses

4Parapharyngeal space

  • Inverted pyramid: skull base → hyoid greater cornu · medial: constrictor + tonsil · lateral: medial pterygoid, ramus, deep parotid
  • Poststyloid: ICA, IJV, IX–XII, sympathetic chain, nodes

7Endoscopic endonasal

Binostril, four hands · wide sphenoidotomy + posterior septectomy
  • Transsphenoidal sella · transplanum suprasellar · transcribriform frontal sinus → planum · transclival → foramen magnum · transpterygoid PPF, ITF, Meckel's cave, petrous apex
No incisions or brain retraction · = open survival for selected cancers (Lund 2010) · limits: lateral to mid-orbit, orbit, skin, front sinus walls, carotid encased

8Closing the skull base

  • Multilayer: inlay (fascia lata ± fat) 5–10 mm under the dura + onlay, ideally vascularised · strip mucosa under it
  • Low-flow: free grafts, bath-plug · high-flow (cistern / ventricle), big, irradiated → flap
  • Nasoseptal flap (Hadad 2006): posterior septal artery · leaks > 20% → ~5% · keep a rescue pedicle
  • Lumbar drain three days, high-flow: 21% → 8% leaks (2019)

9Open routes & outcome

  • Craniofacial: transfacial + bifrontal craniotomy, en bloc, pericranial flap
  • Fisch ITF: A jugular foramen (VII rerouted) · B clivus, apex · C parasellar, nasopharynx
  • CSF leak (commonest), tension pneumocephalus (Mount Fuji), carotid injury (pack, muscle, angiography), DI · 5-year survival ~50%, margins matter most

10Exam traps ✗

  • Transoral biopsy of a parapharyngeal mass
  • Operating on a prolactinoma first
  • Missing a VI palsy or facial numbness
