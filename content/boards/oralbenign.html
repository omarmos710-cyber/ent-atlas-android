BENIGN ORAL LESIONS & PREMALIGNANT PATCHES

mostly benign lumps · then the white and red patches that may become cancer · biopsy the red ✎

1Three groups

  • Benign: solid (papilloma, fibroma, haemangioma, lymphangioma, torus, pyogenic granuloma, granular cell, salivary adenoma) · cystic (mucocele, ranula, dermoid)
  • Premalignant: leukoplakia, erythroplakia, submucous fibrosis…
  • Malignant: SCC + non-squamous
Lump growing, ulcerated, indurated or fixed · patch red, speckled or nodular → biopsy

3Vascular

  • Haemangioma: children · lip, tongue, cheek · red-blue, compressible, bleeds when bitten · capillary, cavernous, mixed · at birth: observe (many regress) · propranolol, steroids · large: embolisation, laser, excision · sclerotherapy poor in diffuse · older adult: phlebectasia
  • Lymphangioma: anterior two-thirds of tongue → macroglossia · does not regress · excise small, debulk large

4Above or below mylohyoid

coronal, schematic · above the muscle: through the mouth · below: through the neck

5Cysts

  • Mucocele: minor salivary gland, almost always lower lip, after a bite · bluish, bursts + refills · excise with the gland
  • Ranula: floor beside the frenulum, lifts the tongue · sublingual gland ducts · plunging: through or behind mylohyoid → submandibular/submental swelling
  • Dhingra: excise small, marsupialise large · recurrence common → excise with the sublingual gland through the mouth, guard Wharton's duct + lingual nerve · cures most plunging

6Premalignant

  • Lesion = altered tissue (leukoplakia, erythroplakia) · condition = general state (submucous fibrosis, lichen planus, iron deficiency)
  • Also hyperplastic candida, reverse-smoking palate, actinic cheilitis, Plummer–Vinson, melanosis, discoid lupus

7Leukoplakia

  • WHO: white patch not characterised as any other disease · clinical term, no histology · exclude lichen planus, candida, leukoedema, white sponge naevus
  • Tobacco, areca, alcohol, teeth, candida · India: buccal + commissures where the quid sits · men, forties

PVLmultifocal, warty, older women · very high risk
  • A quarter dysplastic · change < 1% to 17.5%, ~5% · worst: speckled, erosive, verrucous, floor + ventral tongue
  • Stop habits, smooth teeth → many regress · biopsy the reddest, most nodular part
  • Mild/none: review, excise if changing · moderate or worse: excise or laser/cryo · retinoids, beta-carotene: relapse off them

8Erythroplakia

  • Velvety red, sharp edge, slightly depressed · floor, ventral tongue, soft palate, gingivobuccal sulcus
  • Vascular submucosa through thin epithelium · most already severe dysplasia, CIS or cancer · risk many times leukoplakia
  • Excision biopsy + close follow-up
Carcinoma lives in erythroplakia + the red of speckled leukoplakia: biopsy the red
Pigment: about a quarter of mucosal melanomas look benign → new, enlarging, irregular on palate or gum: biopsy

11Exam traps ✗

  • "Leukoplakia" used as a histological diagnosis
  • Marsupialising a ranula, leaving the sublingual gland
  • Biopsying the white of a speckled leukoplakia, not the red
  • Excising a pregnancy granuloma during pregnancy
  • Calling a patch leukoplakia before excluding thrush, lichen planus, leukoedema
