SWALLOWED FOREIGN BODIES

most pass · find the stuck, the sharp, the magnets, the batteries · a battery is measured in hours ✎

1Who and where

  • ~80% children under five: coins, toys, beads, button batteries, magnets
  • Adults: meat bolus, fish + chicken bones, dentures (upper plate dulls the palate) · haste, alcohol, sleep, seizures · deliberate in psychiatric patients, prisoners

2Symptoms and signs

  • Choke or gag at the time → pricking beside the trachea, worse swallowing · localises well high, poorly low
  • Partial → complete dysphagia as oedema builds · drooling = complete, aspiration risk
  • Child: stridor, cough (soft tracheal wall), wheeze, recurrent chest infection · may be silent
  • Normal eating + a normal film don't exclude a foreign body
  • Jackson's sign: tender beside trachea · pain rocking larynx · saliva pooling in pyriform fossae that won't clear · object sometimes seen postcricoid
  • Emphysema, fever, tachycardia, chest or back pain: perforation

3Reading the film

  • PA + lateral neck, chest, abdomen · child: mouth to anus
  • Invisible: many fish bones, wood, plastic, aluminium, glass · clues: air in cervical oesophagus, prevertebral thickening, lost lordosis
  • CT: most sensitive (bones, perforation, abscess) · no barium: coats, aspirates, hampers scope

5Getting it out

  • Rigid oesophagoscopy (GA): sharp, impacted, at cricopharyngeus · ensheathe the point · open safety pin: close it or point trailing
  • Flexible (sedation): bolus, coins, blunt · overtube for sharp · biopsy the cause
  • Open: neck oesophagotomy (hooked denture) · thoracotomy
✗ blind Foley (aspiration) · ✗ blind push into stomach · ✗ papain (digests wall) · ✗ emetics · ✗ glucagon (little benefit)

6Button batteries

  • Current hydrolyses tissue fluid → hydroxide at negative pole: alkaline liquefactive burn + leak + pressure
  • Mucosa within an hour · muscle two to four hours · perforation 8–12 hours · worst: lithium ≥ 20 mm
  • Late: TOF, mediastinitis, stricture, cord palsy, aorto-oesophageal fistula (days–weeks)
  • Remove at once, any hour (guideline: within two hours) · within 12 hours of swallowing + child over one: honey or sucralfate en route, never delaying · then CT/MRI
  • Stomach: most pass · remove if symptoms, big battery in young child, still there at ~48 hours (one textbook: under six, ≥ 1.5 cm) · nose + ear too · significant injuries observed in hospital
Any round opacity in a child's oesophagus = battery until halo + step excluded · now, not tomorrow's list

7Magnets, sharps, beyond

  • One magnet passes · two+ (or magnet + metal) attract across bowel → necrosis, fistula · remove while reachable, early surgical advice
  • Pins, needles, bones, open safety pins, hooked dentures: urgent · long objects (> ~5 cm small child, ≥ 6 cm adult) may not pass the duodenum → endoscopic removal if reachable, or daily films
  • In stomach: most pass within a week, normal diet, no purgatives, serial films · operate for pain, tenderness, no progress, sharp, obstruction

8Food bolus

  • Swallowing saliva, comfortable: a short wait may let it pass
  • Older adult: sudden bolus may be the first sign of a narrowed oesophagus
  • Can't swallow saliva or > 12–24 hours: endoscopy · push gently if no stricture, or piecemeal
  • Almost every adult: biopsy for EoE + look for ring, stricture, cancer

9Why we don't wait

  • Airway: laryngeal oedema · tracheal compression in children
  • Perforation → retro/parapharyngeal abscess, mediastinitis, empyema, pericarditis
  • Sharp object into aorta: sentinel bleed → exsanguination
  • TOF, ulcer, stricture · needles + bones migrate into the neck

11Guidelines

  • ESGE 2016: emergency (ideally < 6 hours, max 24) for obstruction, battery, sharp · others < 24 hours · CT, no barium
  • Batteries (2021): out within two hours · honey/sucralfate if over one year, within 12 hours
  • Coins: distal, no symptoms → a quarter to a third pass in 8–16 hours

12Exam traps ✗

  • Calling a button battery a coin
  • Sending home persistent FB feeling with a "normal X-ray"
  • Barium as the first study
  • Adult bolus without looking for stricture, EoE, cancer
