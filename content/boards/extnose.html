EXTERNAL NOSE & VESTIBULE

never squeeze a nasal furuncle · biopsy any lesion that does not heal ✎

1Skin, cartilage, vestibule

  • Upper third bony (nasal bones, frontal processes of maxillae) · lower two-thirds cartilage (upper + lower laterals, septal)
  • Skin thin, mobile over the dorsum · thick, sebaceous over tip + alae
  • Vestibule: skin-lined, hairy (vibrissae), up to the limen nasi
schematic profile of the framework

2Furuncle & the danger area

  • Furuncle: staph in a hair follicle (picking, plucking) · exquisitely tender red vestibule, spreads to tip + dorsum · may point, rupture
  • Warm compresses, analgesia, topical + systemic anti-staph · incise only if fluctuant · recurrent → check diabetes + staph carriage
Never squeeze a furuncle: squeezing or early incision can carry infection to the cavernous sinus · also upper lip cellulitis, septal abscess

3Vestibulitis & cellulitis

  • Vestibulitis: S. aureus dermatitis, kept going by discharge + wiping · acute: red, tender, crusts over erosions · chronic: thick skin, painful fissures
  • Clean (peroxide) · antibiotic-steroid ointment beyond cure · silver nitrate for a fissure · treat the source
  • Cellulitis: strep / staph · red, swollen, tender · systemic antibiotics, warmth, analgesia
  • Lupus vulgaris (apple-jelly nodules), leprosy, syphilis: own topic

7Rhinophyma

  • The potato nose: slow, lobulated, pink overgrowth of sebaceous glands + connective tissue over tip + alae, dilated vessels
  • Men past middle age with long-standing acne rosacea · cosmetic, may block breathing or vision

9Vestibule

  • Narrow nares: trauma, burns, surgery, destructive inflammation (formerly smallpox), Young's operation · congenital atresia rare · excise scar, flaps / grafts, stent
  • Nasoalveolar cyst: floor swelling, lifts the ala, fills the nasolabial fold · sublabial excision
  • Warts excised · vestibular SCC → floor, columella, lip, parotid + submandibular nodes

10Exam traps ✗

  • Squeezing or incising an early furuncle
  • Creams for a non-healing ulcer without biopsy
  • Synthetic implants that later extrude
