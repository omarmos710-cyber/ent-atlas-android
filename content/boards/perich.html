2Why the lobule is spared

Cartilage has no blood supply of its own: it lives on the perichondrium · pus or blood separates them → cartilage starves, necroses
Lobule has no cartilage → a cartilage disease spares it

3Perichondritis

  • Causes: infected haematoma, laceration, burn, high (cartilage) piercing, ear surgery, spread from OE
  • Organism: Pseudomonas ± staph
  • Early: red, hot, tender, thickened pinna, sparing the lobule
  • Late: fluctuant pus between perichondrium and cartilage → necrosis
early: oral ciprofloxacin (IV anti-pseudomonal if severe)+cool aluminium acetate
abscess: incise along a fold→remove dead cartilage, irrigate→bolster for days
antibiotics two to four weeks
Outcome of lost cartilage: shrivelled cauliflower ear · flucloxacillin alone won't cover Pseudomonas

4Skin infections

  • Cellulitis: staph / strep after scratch, piercing, OE · may involve the lobule
  • Erysipelas: strep, sharp raised red edge, can spread to the face → penicillin

5Ramsay Hunt syndrome

Herpes zoster oticus: VZV reactivates in the geniculate ganglion
severe burning pain→vesicles: concha, canal, ± palate, tongue→facial palsy
VIII often: tinnitus, SNHL, vertigo
  • Prednisolone ~1 mg/kg × 14 days + antiviral (valaciclovir) + analgesia + eye protection
  • Gabapentin for post-herpetic neuralgia
  • No decompression: damage is diffuse
  • Worse than Bell's: only ~60% regain normal function
  • Cochrane: no randomised evidence antivirals help; steroids insufficient → combined treatment rests on observational data

6Not infections

  • Relapsing polychondritis: autoimmune, recurrent painful red pinna sparing lobule, often both ears · saddle nose, larynx, trachea → airway threat · high-dose steroids, immunosuppression, rheumatology
  • Chondrodermatitis nodularis helicis: painful nodule on the helix, older men sleeping on that side · relieve pressure; excise + histology (skin cancer)
  • DDx: gout (tophi), lymphoma, skin tumours

7On call: a red, swollen ear

lobule spared? = cartilage→fluctuant: drain + bolster→anti-pseudomonal
Vesicles + facial weakness → steroids, antiviral, eye protection · eye symptoms → check cornea

8Exam traps ✗

  • Flucloxacillin alone for perichondritis
  • Leaving pus under the perichondrium
  • Ramsay Hunt called Bell's because nobody looked in the ear
