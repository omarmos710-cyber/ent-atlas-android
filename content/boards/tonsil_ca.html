ORAL & OROPHARYNGEAL CANCER

one visible and surgical, one hidden and often HPV · look, feel, and feel the neck ✎

1Two regions, one disease

  • Oral: lip, buccal mucosa, oral tongue (front two-thirds), floor, alveolus, retromolar trigone, hard palate · oropharynx: tongue base, tonsil + pillars, soft palate + uvula, posterior + lateral walls
  • India: ~a fifth of male cancers, buccal + tongue lead · West: lip (if counted), then tongue + floor (tongue rising in the young) · oropharynx rising (HPV)
  • Smoking ~6× · alcohol multiplies · areca, pan, chewed tobacco · reverse smoking → palate · HPV 16 (tonsil, tongue base; oral sex) · teeth, Plummer-Vinson, immunosuppression, sun (lower lip)
  • Field cancerisation: ~15% get a second primary (head + neck, oesophagus, lung)
  • SCC ~90% · verrucous: warty buccal variant, rarely spreads

2Look, feel, the neck

  • Non-healing ulcer: rolled, everted edge, shaggy base, induration · pain referred to the ear (V3, IX) · hot-potato voice (tongue base) · loose teeth, bad denture fit · trismus · numb lip · neck lump · fetor
  • Light every subsite · palpate lesion + bimanual floor / tongue · feel the tongue base + tonsils · both necks level by level · V, VII, XII
Refer urgently: ulcer or white / red patch > 3 weeks · unexplained lump in mouth or neck · loose tooth · one-sided sore throat or earache with a normal ear

3Stage before you treat

  • Biopsy + p16 / HPV (every oropharynx) · node: US-guided FNA / core (p16) · MRI + CT, OPG · chest CT, PET-CT for III–IV + unknown primary · panendoscopy · dental, nutrition, swallow, audiogram before cisplatin

5Treatment

  • MDT · Oral: surgery first · early: excision, elective neck by depth · advanced: resect, reconstruct, neck → RT, or chemoRT for positive margins / ENE
  • Oropharynx early (T1–T2, N0–N1): single modality · TORS / laser + neck, or RT ~70 Gy / 35 (ORATOR 2019: similar swallowing) · gross ENE on imaging → chemoRT (no triple therapy)
  • Advanced (III–IV): 70 Gy / 35 + cisplatin 100 mg/m² days 1, 22, 43 (or 40 mg/m² weekly) · mandibulotomy mostly for salvage
  • Cetuximab instead of cisplatin worse in HPV + (De-ESCALaTE 97.5% vs 89.4%, RTOG 1016) · residual nodes: PET-CT at 12 weeks, dissect only if positive (PET-NECK)
  • Recurrent / metastatic: pembrolizumab ± platinum + 5-FU (PD-L1 CPS ≥ 1; KEYNOTE-048) · around surgery (KEYNOTE-689, 2025)
  • N0: elective neck if risk > ~20% (I–III oral, II–IV oropharynx) · beat waiting: 80% vs 67.5% (2015) · sentinel node in early oral · N+: comprehensive dissection
  • Flaps: radial forearm, ALT, fibula, pectoralis · early oral cured in most, advanced ~a third to a half · worse with ENE, low nodes, margins, perineural

7Depth and the mandible

  • Thickness predicts nodes: occult spread rises beyond ~4 mm → depth of invasion in T stage (AJCC 8, TNM 9) · elective neck if deeper than ~3–4 mm
  • Tongue T1–2: partial glossectomy ± graft / flap, or RT + brachytherapy · advanced: commando (glossectomy + floor + mandible + neck) · total glossectomy may need laryngectomy
  • N0 tongue past the depth threshold: selective neck I–III (supraomohyoid), both sides if midline · sentinel node an alternative
Periosteum → marginal · medulla or atrophic edentulous jaw → segmental · enters at the alveolar crest (teeth) or occlusal surface (edentulous) · never irradiate an invaded mandible

9Oropharynx, site by site

  • Tongue base: silent, blamed on lingual tonsil · ~70% have nodes (occult primary site) · late otalgia, hot-potato voice, fixation · bigger to the finger than the mirror
  • Tonsil: necrotic ulcer or smooth big tonsil · ~half nodes, jugulodigastric first · lymphoma: smooth one-sided
  • Faucial arch: superficial, early, slow, like oral cancer · walls: submucosal, nodes ~60%, retropharyngeal
  • Benign: papilloma, haemangioma, pleomorphic adenoma, vallecular cyst

10Not squamous

  • Minor salivary: 80–90% malignant in some series · palate · adenoid cystic ~40% (perineural) · wide excision + RT
  • Melanoma: palate, gum, sixth decade, men 2× · five-year ~15% · lymphoma: tonsil, tongue base, smooth bulky · Kaposi: purple palate, AIDS
