APPROACH TO POSTNASAL DRIP AND CATARRH

the sensation often outlasts the mucus · treat the nose, sinuses and reflux · recognise the hypersensitive throat · miss nothing serious ✎

1Why it's felt

  • Often more than one · UACS: cough attributed to the nose · many with chronic cough have a sensitised reflex instead

2Red flags

  • One-sided nose, blood-stained mucus, neck lump → NPC, sinonasal tumour
  • Hoarse, dysphagia, weight loss, odynophagia → laryngeal/pharyngeal cancer
  • Smoker, changed cough, haemoptysis, breathless → CXR, chest team
  • Cough on an ACE inhibitor → the drug

3History

  • Mucus to spit out, or a sensation nothing relieves? Clear, thick, discoloured?
  • Nose: block, discharge, sneeze, smell, pressure → rhinitis, CRS
  • Reflux: heartburn, sour taste, morning hoarseness, globus, clearing
  • Cough: chronic = eight weeks or more · smoking, asthma · ACE inhibitors, drying drugs (antidepressants, anxiolytics, anticholinergics)
  • Score: RSI, SNOT-22

4Look at both ends

  • Nasal endoscopy: pus in middle meatus or sphenoethmoidal recess, polyps, adenoid, Thornwaldt cyst, nasopharyngeal mass
  • Down to the larynx: posterior commissure thickening, oedema, erythema · any lesion · neck
  • CT sinuses only if CRS suspected and not settling

6Treat what is found

  • Rhinitis: saline + steroid spray · antihistamine (allergy) · ipratropium (watery)
  • CRS: saline, steroid, long macrolide (selected), surgery if failing (EPOS 2020)
  • Reflux: diet, lifestyle, alginate · PPI trial for typical acid symptoms · weak for throat alone
  • Review drying or causative drugs with the prescriber

7When nothing is found

  • Many: normal endoscopy and CT, altered sensation not excess mucus
  • Explain, reassure, hydrate, steam
  • Clearing perpetuates it → speech therapy: laryngeal hygiene, cough suppression
  • Refractory cough: low-dose amitriptyline or gabapentin (specialist)
  • No repeated antibiotics when examination is normal

8Pathway

Red flags? full endoscopy + imaging · smoker: CXR Nose: rhinitis → spray · pus/polyps → CRS · nasopharynx lesion → biopsy Larynx: reflux → lifestyle, alginate, PPI trial All normal: drugs, explanation, speech therapy, neuromodulator

9Exam traps ✗

  • Repeated antibiotics for "catarrh"
  • ACE inhibitor missed in chronic cough
  • Sinus surgery for drip with normal CT and scope
