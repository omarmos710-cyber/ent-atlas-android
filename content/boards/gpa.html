4Rhinoscleroma

Klebsiella rhinoscleromatis (Frisch bacillus, Gram-negative) · endemic in Egypt, Africa, Central America, E. Europe, N. India · nose → pharynx → subglottis → bronchi
atrophic: foul crusts→granulomatous: woody Hebra nose, lip→cicatricial: stenosis, subglottic stridor
  • Mikulicz cells (foamy, hold bacilli) + Russell bodies = diagnostic · culture the biopsy
  • Antibiotics until two negative biopsy cultures · Dhingra: streptomycin 1 g + tetracycline 2 g daily, ≥ four to six weeks, steroids for fibrosis · Lee: fluoroquinolone or tetracycline for months
  • Modern: ciprofloxacin 500–750 mg twice a day for months · or doxycycline 100 mg twice a day, co-trimoxazole
  • Surgery for airway (tracheostomy) + deformity

6Parasites

  • Rhinosporidiosis (R. seeberi, aquatic protistan) · ponds · S. India, Sri Lanka · pink-purple strawberry mass with white dots, bleeds
  • Sporangia 200–250 µm full of endospores · cannot be cultured · excise with diathermy, cauterise base · recurs · dapsone helps some
  • Leishmaniasis: sandfly · septal nodule → ulcer → perforation, years after a skin sore · biopsy, culture, PCR
  • Mucosal leishmaniasis: antimonial 20 mg Sb/kg IV or IM daily × 28–30 days (ECG, liver, pancreas) · or liposomal amphotericin B, miltefosine

7GPA (Wegener's)

  • Nose: persistent cold / sinusitis, bloody crusts, perforation, saddle · orbit, palate, middle ear · subglottic stenosis
  • Body: fatigue, arthralgia · cavitating nodules, haemoptysis · glomerulonephritis = usual cause of death untreated

8GPA: treatment

  • Induce: rituximab 375 mg/m² weekly × 4 or 1 g twice 2 weeks apart · or cyclophosphamide IV 15 mg/kg pulses
  • + prednisolone ~1 mg/kg (max 60–75 mg), tapered · reduced-dose taper (PEXIVAS) · avacopan 30 mg twice daily spares steroid
  • Maintain: rituximab 500 mg every 6 months (or azathioprine, methotrexate) × 24–48 months
  • Limited ENT disease: methotrexate (+ folic acid) or mycophenolate + steroid
  • Co-trimoxazole 480 mg daily or 960 mg three times a week: Pneumocystis cover
  • Nose: saline douches, emollient, mupirocin for S. aureus carriage · rebuild only in long remission
ACR/EULAR 2022: nasal disease + PR3 weigh heavily · RAVE: rituximab = cyclophosphamide, better in relapse · PEXIVAS: plasma exchange no help, low-dose steroid as good · ADVOCATE: avacopan, better remission at one year · MAINRITSAN: rituximab > azathioprine

9EGPA and sarcoidosis

  • EGPA (Churg-Strauss): asthma, polyps, eosinophilia, vasculitis · often p-ANCA
  • Sarcoid: non-caseating · septal + turbinate nodules, lupus pernio · hilar nodes, ↑ ACE + calcium
  • Sarcoid treatment: saline, steroid spray / drops → prednisolone 20–40 mg tapered over months · methotrexate, hydroxychloroquine

10NK/T-cell lymphoma

  • EBV · East Asia, Latin America · relentless midline destruction, fever, weight loss
  • Deep, repeated biopsies of viable tissue · CD56, cytotoxic markers, EBV · surface biopsy = only necrosis
  • Asparaginase-based chemotherapy + radiotherapy · plasma EBV DNA to follow · must not be treated as GPA

11Exam traps ✗

  • Months of antibiotics without a biopsy · superficial biopsies in lymphoma
  • Forgetting the kidneys in GPA
