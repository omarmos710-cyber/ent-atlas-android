APPROACH TO A LUMP IN THE NECK

child: reactive node or congenital cyst · adult over forty who smokes: metastatic SCC until proved otherwise · examine, scope, image, needle before any open biopsy ✎

1Age, time, site

  • Child/young: inflammatory, congenital · lymphoma, thyroid · adult > forty: neoplasm, mostly metastases
  • Rule of 80: of adult non-thyroid masses ~80% neoplastic → 80% malignant → 80% metastatic (above the clavicles)
  • Days inflammatory · months neoplastic · years congenital/benign

2Node levels

  • II (IIA/IIB by XI): oral, oropharynx, nasopharynx, larynx · III–IV: hypopharynx, larynx, thyroid · V: nasopharynx, scalp · VI: thyroid, subglottis · VII: mediastinum
  • Cystic level II node in an adult = HPV oropharynx or papillary thyroid, not a branchial cyst · left supraclavicular = Troisier

3History and exam

  • Duration, growth, pain, meals, infections · hoarse, dysphagia, odynophagia, otalgia, nasal block/bleed, one-sided effusion, weight
  • Tobacco, alcohol, betel · sexual history (HPV) · TB, HIV · cats (Bartonella, toxo) · travel · skin cancer, RT · B symptoms
  • Palpate from behind, level by level: size, consistency, fixity, pulsation, transillumination · moves on swallowing (thyroid, TGDC) or tongue protrusion (TGDC)
  • Whole head and neck: scalp, skin, ears, nose, mouth, palpate tonsils + tongue base, flexible scope · axillae, groins, liver, spleen

4Investigate in order

US + FNA: p16/HPV, EBV, thyroglobulin, flow, AFB, culture · non-diagnostic → repeat or core CT/MRI neck · PET-CT before panendoscopy for unknown primary · chest imaging Bloods: FBC, ESR, EBV, HIV, toxo, Bartonella, TB tests Panendoscopy + biopsies, tonsillectomy, tongue-base mucosectomy
Open biopsy of a metastatic SCC node breaches fascia → recurrence, complications · if unavoidable: incision fits a neck dissection, frozen section, ready to proceed
US core: 96% accuracy, specific in 87% (2012, 2016) · NG12: two-week referral for unexplained adult neck lump · one-stop clinics

5Inflammatory

  • Reactive: tender, mobile, bilateral, settle in two to six weeks · persisting, hard, supraclavicular → investigate
  • Suppurative (S. aureus, GAS), young children → antibiotics, drain · adult abscess = necrotic metastasis or TB?
  • TB: discrete → matted → cold abscess → collar-stud → sinus · FNA/biopsy, ZN, culture, GeneXpert, CXR, IGRA, HIV · HRZE two months + HR four · paradoxical enlargement · aspirate, don't incise
  • Atypical mycobacteria: one to five years, violaceous thin skin, sinuses · excise (curette near VII), clarithromycin · I&D → chronic sinus
  • Cat-scratch (azithromycin), toxo, EBV, HIV, actinomycosis
  • Sarcoid, Kikuchi (young women, self-limiting), Kimura (Asian men, eosinophilia, IgE), Castleman, Rosai–Dorfman

6Metastases, lymphoma

  • Hard, painless, growing, level II/III · old smoker or young HPV+ (cystic) · fixed = ENE
  • Seek primary: tonsil, tongue base, nasopharynx, piriform, larynx, oral · supraclavicular: lung, breast, GI, kidney, testis, ovary
  • Unknown primary: PET-CT → panendoscopy → tonsillectomy + TBM (finds about two-thirds, 2016) → p16, EBV · dissection + RT or CRT · HPV+ does well
  • Hodgkin: young, rubbery, low neck, mediastinum, alcohol pain, Reed–Sternberg · NHL: older, extranodal (Waldeyer's, thyroid, salivary, NK/T)
  • Lymphoma: core or excision, fresh tissue

7Not nodes, thyroid

  • Laryngocele: through thyrohyoid membrane, bigger on Valsalva (trumpeters) · pyocele · exclude ventricular cancer
  • Pouch (low left, gurgles) · plunging ranula → remove sublingual gland · cervical rib · lipoma · sebaceous · carotid aneurysm
  • Thyroid rises on swallowing (Berry) · TGDC also on tongue protrusion · isthmus over rings second to fourth
  • Diffuse (Graves bruit, Hashimoto; rapid → lymphoma) · MNG (5–10% cancer) · solitary (5–15%) · Pemberton
  • Worry: age under 20–30 or over 60, male, irradiation, family/MEN 2, growth, pain, hard, > 4 cm, nodes, palsy
  • TSH → low: isotope, no FNA of hot nodule · normal/high: US U1–U5 → FNA U3–U5 → Bethesda/Thy1–Thy5 · scope the larynx in every thyroid patient

8Children

  • Mostly reactive · TGDC, branchial, dermoid, hygroma, haemangioma · NTM toddler
  • Worry: > 2 cm or growing, hard, matted, supraclavicular, beyond six weeks, B symptoms · lymphoma commonest
  • US first · CXR before GA · excision biopsy often needed

9Pathway, dissection

Acute tender → antibiotics, review two to three weeks · thyroid → TSH, US, FNA · red flag or > three weeks → step 2 Full exam + scope · obvious primary → biopsy, stage US + FNA/core → SCC: imaging, PET, panendoscopy · lymphoma: core/excision · TB: culture
Radical I–V + XI, IJV, SCM · modified spares ≥ one · selective: supraomohyoid I–III (oral), lateral II–IV (larynx, pharynx) · elective if occult risk > 15–20% · PET-CT surveillance after CRT = dissection (2016)

10Exam traps ✗

  • Adult cystic level II mass called branchial cyst
  • Excision biopsy before the aerodigestive tract is examined
  • Incising TB or atypical mycobacterial abscess
  • Nasopharynx missed with posterior nodes + one-sided effusion
