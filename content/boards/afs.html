3Mimics

  • Bacterial CRS with polyps · mucocele: expansion, no hyperdense mucin
  • Inverted papilloma, cancer: one-sided mass, erosion
  • GPA, NK/T-cell lymphoma: destroyed, crusted nose

4Fungal ball

  • Cheesy grey-black mass of hyphae · usually one maxillary sinus (then sphenoid, ethmoid, frontal) · healthy, often older patient · one-sided sinusitis or nothing
  • CT: one opaque sinus, hyperdense centre / calcific flecks, no erosion, wall may be thick
  • Histology + culture of removed material: hyphae, no invasion · immunocompromised or erosion → mucosal biopsy
  • Endoscopic removal + wide opening · no antifungal

6Acute invasive: an emergency

  • Who: diabetes with ketoacidosis (acid favours Mucor), neutropenia, leukaemia, chemotherapy, transplant, HIV, high-dose steroids · after COVID-19's second wave: "black fungus" in steroid-treated diabetics
  • How: hyphae invade arteries → thrombosis → ischaemic necrosis → along vessels + nerves to orbit, cribriform, cavernous sinus, brain
  • Picture: fever, facial pain or numbness, bloody discharge → proptosis, ophthalmoplegia, ↓ vision, palate / skin discoloured, confusion

10Exam traps ✗

  • Diabetic with facial numbness + black turbinate treated as sinusitis
  • Waiting for culture before amphotericin · voriconazole for Mucor
  • Antifungals for a fungal ball · no steroids in allergic fungal disease
