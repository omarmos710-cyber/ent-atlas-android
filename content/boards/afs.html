3Fungal ball

  • Cheesy grey-black mass of hyphae · usually one maxillary sinus (then sphenoid) · healthy, older patient · one-sided sinusitis or nothing
  • CT: one opaque sinus, hyperdense centre / calcific flecks, no erosion, thick wall
  • Endoscopic removal + wide opening · no antifungal

4Allergic fungal rhinosinusitis

Type I + III hypersensitivity to fungi in the mucin, no invasion · young, atopic, immunocompetent · warm, humid climates

  • Often one-sided polyps · peanut-butter mucin: eosinophils, Charcot-Leyden crystals, hyphae
  • Sinuses expand → proptosis, telecanthus, skull base erosion
  • CT: double density, expanded thinned bone · MRI: mucin dark on T2
  • Complete endoscopic clearance + oral steroid before + after + long-term topical steroid, saline · recurs: follow closely · antifungals, immunotherapy: small role

5Mimics

  • Bacterial CRS with polyps
  • Inverted papilloma, cancer: one-sided mass, erosion
  • GPA, NK/T-cell lymphoma: destroyed, crusted nose
  • Mucocele: expansion, no hyperdense mucin

6Acute invasive: an emergency

  • Who: diabetes with ketoacidosis (acid favours Mucor), neutropenia, leukaemia, chemotherapy, transplant, HIV, high-dose steroids · post-COVID-19 "black fungus"
  • How: hyphae invade arteries → thrombosis → ischaemic necrosis → orbit, cribriform, cavernous sinus, brain
  • Picture: fever, facial pain or numbness, bloody discharge → proptosis, ophthalmoplegia, ↓ vision, palate / skin discoloured, confusion
pale, insensate mucosa→black eschar: turbinate, septum, palate
Diagnosis: urgent endoscopic biopsy of the middle turbinate, frozen section, silver / PAS stains, culture · contrast CT + MRI (non-enhancing dead mucosa)

9Guidelines

  • Mucormycosis (global, 2019): early complete debridement + high-dose liposomal amphotericin · isavuconazole / posaconazole alternatives · control the cause, above all ketoacidosis
  • Aspergillosis (US, 2016): voriconazole first + debridement
  • COVID-19 surge in India: check glucose, limit steroids
  • AFRS: antifungal + immunotherapy evidence weak

10Exam traps ✗

  • Diabetic with facial numbness + black turbinate treated as sinusitis
  • Waiting for culture before amphotericin
  • Voriconazole for Mucor
  • Antifungals for a fungal ball · no steroids in allergic fungal disease
