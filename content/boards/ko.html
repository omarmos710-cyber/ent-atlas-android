KERATOSIS OBTURANS & CANAL CHOLESTEATOMA

when the canal stops cleaning itself ✎

1Self-cleaning fails

drum + deep canal skin migrate outwards→carry keratin + wax to the entrance
Migration fails → keratin builds in the deep canal → one of two diseases, now accepted as distinct: a painful plug that fills and widens the whole canal, or a focal patch of eroded, dead bone
schematic cross-sections of the bony canal · erosion usually posteroinferior

4Keratosis obturans

  • Migration failure, maybe from canal skin inflammation · young: look for bronchiectasis + chronic sinusitis
  • Acute severe pain (hallmark), conductive loss, ± otorrhoea
  • Dense white laminated plug; removed → widened canal, ± thick drum
  • Age: Dhingra5–20 years Lee30–60 years · the younger patient's disease
StepTreatment
firstremove plug under the microscope (GA in children if needed)
soften beforeolive oil or sodium bicarbonate 5% drops, two or three times a day × 3–5 days
secondary OEantibiotic–steroid drops × 7 days
preventregular microsuction · salicylic acid 2% in alcohol drops · every few months · lifelong follow-up
Complication: progressive widening → erodes into the mastoid (auto-mastoidectomy) and exposes the facial nerve

5Canal cholesteatoma

  • Spontaneous in the old, or after surgery, trauma, radiation, stenosis
  • Dull chronic ache, foul scanty discharge, normal hearing
  • Localised ulcer on floor / posterior wall, keratin, exposed necrotic bone
  • Microscopy · HRCT: extent, mastoid, facial nerve · biopsy granulation in every patient → exclude SCC · audiogram (usually normal)
  • Limited: debridement + drops · recurrent / extensive: canalplasty — remove matrix + dead bone, cover with fascia or skin graft

6Exam traps ✗

  • Syringing a keratin plug again and again instead of the microscope
  • Missing carcinoma in canal granulation
