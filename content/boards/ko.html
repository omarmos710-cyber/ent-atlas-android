3Keratosis obturans

  • Migration failure, maybe from canal skin inflammation · young: look for bronchiectasis + chronic sinusitis
  • Acute severe pain, conductive loss, ± otorrhoea
  • Dense white laminated plug; removed → widened canal, ± thick drum
  • Age: Dhingra 5–20 years · Lee 30–60 years

Treatment

remove plug under the microscope (GA in children) · treat secondary OE · regular cleaning · salicylic acid 2% in alcohol slows reaccumulation · lifelong follow-up
Progressive widening → erodes into the mastoid (auto-mastoidectomy) and exposes the facial nerve

4Canal cholesteatoma

  • Spontaneous in the old, or after surgery, trauma, radiation, stenosis
  • Dull chronic ache, foul scanty discharge, normal hearing
  • Localised ulcer on floor / posterior wall, keratin, exposed necrotic bone
  • CT: extent, mastoid, facial nerve · biopsy granulation → exclude carcinoma
  • Debridement + drops → recurrent / extensive: remove matrix + dead bone, cover with fascia or skin graft (canalplasty)

5Also think of

impacted wax · necrotising OE · SCC of the canal · middle ear cholesteatoma eroding the canal wall

6Exam traps ✗

  • Syringing a keratin plug again and again instead of the microscope
  • Missing carcinoma in canal granulation
