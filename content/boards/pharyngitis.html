2Acute sore throat

  • Mild: discomfort, low fever, no nodes · moderate–severe: odynophagia, high fever, exudate, follicles · very severe: palate + uvula oedema, tender nodes
  • Virus vs bacteria: can't tell by looking

4Test and treat

  • Rest, fluids, gargles, analgesia, viscous lidocaine · swab culture finds ~90% of GAS · rapid antigen in minutes · diphtheria: special media · gonococcus: plate at once · Monospot, EBV serology, film
  • Strep: penicillin V ten days (US) or five to ten (NICE), or benzathine IM · macrolide if allergic
  • Rheumatic fever common (Egypt, developing world): full ten days or benzathine
  • Diphtheria: antitoxin + penicillin or erythromycin

5Glandular fever

  • EBV, older child, young adult · grey-white membrane, palatal petechiae, posterior + anterior nodes, spleen, liver
  • Atypical lymphocytes · Monospot (heterophile) or EBV serology
  • Amoxicillin / ampicillin → rash in most: avoid
  • Airway: steroids, rarely nasopharyngeal airway or tonsillectomy · no contact sport for weeks (splenic rupture)

7Fungus, uvula, swelling

  • Candida spreading from the mouth: immunosuppressed, diabetic, steroids (incl. inhaled), antibiotics · pain, dysphagia, white plaques, hyphae · nystatin, clotrimazole, fluconazole, itraconazole
  • Quincke: swollen uvula · tonsillitis, burns, heavy snoring
  • Angioedema: rapid, painless, watery · lips, tongue, floor, uvula, larynx · allergic = histamine · ACE inhibitor + hereditary (C1 esterase) = bradykinin, poor antihistamine response · stop ACE-i · C1 inhibitor or icatibant · awake fibreoptic or tracheostomy
Drooling, can't swallow saliva, muffled voice, stridor, sitting forward: don't lie flat, no tongue depressor · senior airway help

8Chronic pharyngitis

TypeLook
Catarrhalcongested wall, engorged vessels, mucus
Hypertrophic (granular)thick wall, red lymphoid granules, lateral bands, long uvula
Atrophicdry, glazed, crusts · with atrophic rhinitis
  • Causes: postnasal drip, dental + tonsil sepsis · mouth breathing (dry unfiltered air) · smoking, pan, alcohol, spice, fumes · LPR · voice misuse, throat clearing
  • Worse in the morning · lump sensation, urge to swallow · tiring voice, cough, gagging
  • Remove cause → voice rest, speech therapy, stop hawking → morning saline gargles → Mandl's paint → cautery of granules (silver nitrate, diathermy)
  • Atrophic: alkaline sprays + irrigation for crusts, Mandl's, potassium iodide by mouth, treat the atrophic rhinitis
One-sided pain, earache, dysphagia, weight loss, haemoptysis, neck lump: not chronic pharyngitis until endoscopy + neck palpation

9Pain without inflammation

  • Eagle: long styloid or calcified stylohyoid ligament · often after tonsillectomy · tip felt in the fossa · X-ray or 3D CT · many long styloids are silent · shorten via fossa or neck
  • Glossopharyngeal neuralgia: electric pain tonsil, tongue base, ear · swallowing, yawning · carbamazepine, microvascular decompression
  • Globus: lump between meals, food goes down · reflux, anxiety · red flags → investigate

10Keratosis pharyngis

  • White-yellow horny spikes, firmly adherent · tonsils, posterior wall, lingual tonsil
  • Hypertrophy + keratinisation of epithelium
  • No inflammation, no fever → not follicular tonsillitis · harmless, often regresses · reassure

11Exam traps ✗

  • Amoxicillin for exudative throat + big posterior nodes
  • Stopping penicillin at three days because it feels better
  • Smoker's persistent pain called chronic pharyngitis, no endoscopy
  • ACE inhibitor angioedema treated with antihistamines alone
  • Lying down or depressing the tongue of a drooling, stridulous patient
