CHRONIC RHINOSINUSITIS & NASAL POLYPS

not an infection that won't clear · surgery opens the sinuses so the medicine can reach them ✎

1Twelve weeks + proof

blockagedischargefacial pain / pressure↓ smell
  • ≥ 12 weeks · two or more, one must be block or discharge
  • + objective: endoscopy (oedema, polyps, mucopus in middle meatus) or CT (OMC / sinus mucosa)
  • Without polyps: more infective, neutrophilic · with polyps: type 2 (IL-4, IL-5, IL-13), eosinophilic, asthma
  • Exacerbation: flare that settles back to baseline

3Endoscopy, then CT

  • Endoscopy before + after decongestion · Lund-Kennedy score
  • Non-contrast CT, three planes = gold standard · Lund-Mackay · confirms unclear disease, maps before any operation
  • Normal people: cysts 10–15%, thickening a quarter, score up to about 4 · > 5 = disease
  • MRI too sensitive: thickening in about half of everyone
  • Allergy tests, sweat test / genetics (child), cilia, immunoglobulins, eosinophils, ANCA, culture · SNOT-22

4The vicious cycle

  • Anatomy: septal deviation, concha bullosa, paradoxical MT, Haller + agger nasi cells, bent uncinate
  • Cilia: PCD (Kartagener: situs inversus, bronchiectasis) · CF thick mucus · Young: + azoospermia
  • Host: allergy, asthma (~half), aspirin, immune deficiency, smoking, teeth, osteitis · biofilms
  • Bugs: S. aureus (MRSA), Pseudomonas, Klebsiella, E. coli, anaerobes

  • From the lateral wall (middle meatus), never septum or floor · oedematous stroma, eosinophils
  • Central compartment atopic disease: MT head, upper septum, posterosuperior nose · "chewing gum" mucin → eosinophilic / fungal
  • Pale, glistening, insensitive, don't bleed · long-standing → broad nose, frog face (Woakes)

7Saline and steroids first

Without polypsWith polyps
high-volume saline (breaks biofilm) · steroid spray / drops after decongestion · antibiotics only for flares, by culture · treat allergylong-term steroid spray / drops · short oral courses (limit) · doxycycline · treat asthma · AERD: leukotriene modifiers, aspirin desensitisation after surgery
Macrolides: MACRO, three months clarithromycin = placebo → not routine · Cochrane 2016: large-volume saline helps

8Biologics (EPOS)

Dupilumab (IL-4R) · omalizumab (IgE) · mepolizumab (IL-5) · bilateral polyps after sinus surgery (or unfit) + 3 or more of:
  • type 2: tissue eos ≥ 10 / HPF, blood eos ≥ 0.25, or IgE ≥ 100
  • systemic steroids: ≥ 2 courses a year or long-term
  • SNOT-22 ≥ 40 · anosmia · asthma on inhaled steroid
Review at 16 weeks and one year · cost limits access · dupilumab (Lancet 2019, two phase 3 trials): smaller polyps, less block, fewer steroid courses + operations

9Endoscopic sinus surgery

  • When symptoms persist despite ≥ eight weeks of saline + steroid
  • Remove polyps, open natural ostia, fix anatomy → a cavity the spray can reach · medicine continues after
  • Diffuse type 2: complete, wide "neo-sinus" · localised: limited opening · revision common in polyps
  • CF / cilia: wide dependent maxillary + sphenoid drainage
  • Frontal: sinusotomy → Draf III (modified Lothrop) · obliteration for failures
  • Historical: polypectomy alone, Caldwell-Luc, Howarth / Lynch, inferior meatal antrostomy
  • MACRO (Lancet 2025, ~500 adults): surgery beat placebo at six months

10Exam traps ✗

  • Repeated antibiotics without steroid + saline
  • A one-sided polyp called benign without histology
  • Operating before proper medical treatment
  • No sweat test in a child with polyps
