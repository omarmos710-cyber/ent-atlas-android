LASERS, RADIOFREQUENCY, CRYO & HYPERBARIC OXYGEN

each tool works through one physical principle · prevent the airway fire ✎

1How a laser works

pump→stimulated→inversion→mirrors
  • Active medium: gas (CO2, argon, He–Ne), crystal (Nd:YAG, KTP, Er, Ho), dye, diode
  • Light: monochromatic, coherent, collimated, intense, focusable
  • Only absorbed energy acts · chromophore: water (infrared: CO2, Er:YAG) · haemoglobin (peaks 418, 542, 577 nm: KTP 532, pulsed dye 585–595) · melanin
  • ~60°C coagulation · ~80°C collagen breaks · 100°C vaporises → crater, necrosis (seals vessels), reversible repair zone
  • CO2 + Er penetrate microns (precise) · Nd:YAG ~4 mm (haemostasis, imprecise)
  • Focused cuts, defocused coagulates · pulsed/superpulsed beat thermal relaxation time · power density W/cm², fluence J/cm²
  • Fibres: argon, KTP, Nd:YAG, diode, Ho · CO2: articulated arm + micromanipulator or waveguide · invisible beams need a He–Ne aiming beam

3The fire triangle

  • Cuff filled with saline + methylene blue → puncture shows · or jet ventilation, no tube
Fire: stop laser → stop ventilation, O₂ off → tube out → saline in → mask with air → reintubate → bronchoscopy → steroid, antibiotics, follow for stenosis

4Laser safety

  • Laser safety officer, trained staff, "laser in use" sign, closed door
  • Goggles for the wavelength: clear for CO2 · orange/amber argon + KTP · green-blue Nd:YAG
  • 400–1400 nm pass to the retina · CO2 burns the cornea
  • Patient: wet eye pads + towels · smoke evacuator (HPV in plume, mutagenic)
  • PDT: photosensitiser + light ~630–652 nm → free radicals · superficial cancers, field change · avoid sunlight for weeks

5Radiofrequency and coblation

  • RFA: ~350 kHz–4 MHz (often 460 kHz) probe within tissue → 70–85°C, no charring → fibrosis shrinks it over three to six weeks
  • Turbinates, palate, tongue base (OSA), tonsils, thyroid nodules · XII bundle ~2.7 cm deep, 1.6 cm lateral to foramen caecum
  • Coblation: RF through saline → plasma breaks bonds at 40–70°C · one wand ablates, suctions, irrigates, coagulates
  • Tonsils (intracapsular: less pain, bleeding), adenoids, tongue base, papillomas, subglottis, JNA

6Cryosurgery

  • Liquid nitrogen to −196°C · N₂O/CO2 probes (Joule–Thomson) ~−70°C
  • Freeze fast, thaw slowly, repeat · to −20 to −30°C or colder · margin · thermocouple
  • Ice crystals + dehydration, denatured lipoproteins, thermal shock, vascular stasis (vascular lesions respond)
  • Haemangiomas, leukoplakia, actinic keratosis, Bowen's, small BCC (> 90%, over cartilage) · palliation · turbinates
  • + outpatient, bleeding disorders, no keloid, cheap · − no histology, imprecise, hypopigmentation, slough

7Hyperbaric oxygen

  • 100% O₂ at 2.0–2.5 ATA, 90–120 minutes, 10–40 sessions · dissolved O₂ up several-fold → angiogenesis, fibroblasts, killing, less oedema
  • Osteoradionecrosis: adjunct · Marx before extraction vs HOPON (2019): no benefit · pentoxifylline–tocopherol
  • Necrotising OE, skull base osteomyelitis, flaps · sudden SNHL salvage or + steroid within two weeks
  • Decompression sickness, air embolism, CO poisoning, necrotising fasciitis
  • Ear barotrauma commonest (grommets) · sinus, seizures, myopia · untreated pneumothorax absolute · ET dysfunction, URTI, bullae, epilepsy, bleomycin relative

8Narrow band imaging

  • 415 nm + 540 nm absorbed by Hb, superficial · capillaries brown, veins cyan
  • Abnormal IPCLs (dilated, tortuous, brown dots) = dysplasia, early cancer
  • Small cancers < 1 cm, second primaries, biopsy targets, margins, post-RT surveillance · spoiled by secretions, blood, keratosis

9Exam traps ✗

  • N₂O or high O₂ during laser airway surgery (ASA 2013: < 30%)
  • Wrong goggles for the wavelength
  • Laser or cryo on an undiagnosed lesion, no biopsy
  • Forgetting ear barotrauma in hyperbaric oxygen
