SKIN CANCER & MELANOMA OF THE HEAD AND NECK

biopsy first, clear margins before complex reconstruction, think parotid + neck ✎

1Who gets it

  • BCC 70–75% of NMSC · SCC ~20% · Merkel rare · a fifth to a third of melanomas in H&N · sebaceous, DFSP, scalp angiosarcoma, AFX
  • UV: cumulative → BCC, SCC · intermittent + childhood sunburn → melanoma · tanning beds · Fitzpatrick I–II, many naevi
  • Immunosuppression: transplant SCC up to a hundredfold, aggressive · HIV, CLL · reduce or switch to sirolimus
  • Radiation, arsenic, Marjolin (burn scar) · xeroderma pigmentosum · Gorlin (PTCH1: BCCs, jaw keratocysts, pits, falx; no RT) · FAMM (CDKN2A) · albinism · Merkel polyomavirus

2Assess

  • Size, border, colour, ulcer, fixation · whole skin · nodes · V + VII for perineural spread (cheek, temple, forehead SCC)
  • Shave (superficial non-pigmented) · punch/incisional · small pigmented: excision biopsy, 1–3 mm
  • CT/MRI: large, recurrent, bone, nodes, perineural (MRI along V, VII) · US-FNA · PET-CT advanced
✗ shave a suspected melanoma (loses depth) · ✗ wide excision before diagnosis (ruins sentinel mapping)

3Where it drains

Posterior scalp/neck → posterolateral dissection (II–V + postauricular + suboccipital)

4Basal cell carcinoma

  • Pearly papule, rolled edge, telangiectasia → rodent ulcer · nodular (commonest), superficial, pigmented · aggressive: morphoeic, infiltrative, micronodular, basosquamous
  • Slow, spreads along fusion planes (ala, inner canthus, preauricular) · metastasis very rare
  • High risk: H zone (nose, lids, lips, ear, temple) · ≥ 10 mm cheek/forehead/scalp/neck · ill-defined, aggressive subtype, PNI · recurrent, irradiated, immunosuppressed
  • Low risk: excise ~4 mm · curettage off the face
  • High risk, recurrent, H zone: Mohs (whole margin, tissue-sparing) · halved recurrence at ten years (2014)
  • RT if unfit (not Gorlin) · superficial: imiquimod, 5-FU, PDT, cryo · advanced: vismodegib, sonidegib (cramps, taste, alopecia) → cemiplimab

5Cutaneous SCC

  • Actinic keratosis → Bowen's (in situ) · keratoacanthoma: treat as SCC, excise · lip, ear, scalp, temple, cheek
  • High risk: ≥ 2 cm, > 2 mm deep or beyond fat, poorly differentiated, desmoplastic/acantholytic/adenosquamous, PNI (named nerves), LVI · ear, lip, recurrent, scar, immunosuppressed
  • In situ: cryo, 5-FU, imiquimod, PDT · low risk: 4–6 mm · high risk: 6–10 mm or Mohs + bone/cartilage/parotid · PORT for PNI, close/+ margins, recurrence, T3–4
  • ~5% of high-risk metastasise → parotid + neck · parotidectomy + neck + PORT (cisplatin for ENE, + margin) · SLNB selected
  • Cemiplimab, pembrolizumab first-line advanced (~half respond, 2018)
  • Lip: lower vermilion, outdoor workers · ≤ a third: V/W wedge · Abbe, Estlander, Karapandzic, Bernard–Burow · vermilionectomy for actinic cheilitis

6Merkel cell

  • Elderly, sun-damaged, immunosuppressed · polyomavirus · red-violet dome · AEIOU (asymptomatic, expanding, immunosuppressed, older than 50, UV site)
  • Small round blue cells · CK20 dot +, neuroendocrine +, TTF-1 − (vs small-cell lung)
  • ~a third occult nodes · five-year 30–65% · excise 1–2 cm + SLNB · RT · avelumab, pembrolizumab, nivolumab

7Melanoma: recognise

  • Up to a third from a naevus · giant congenital > 20 cm highest · lentigo maligna (Hutchinson) = in situ
  • Superficial spreading ~70% · nodular 10–15%, thick · lentigo maligna melanoma (elderly face) · acral (dark skin, not UV) · desmoplastic: amelanotic, scar-like, neurotropic → wide margins + RT
  • ABCDE: asymmetry, border, colour, diameter > 6 mm, evolution · ugly duckling · dermoscopy
  • H&N worse than trunk: scalp > ear > face
  • Mimics: seborrhoeic keratosis, pigmented BCC, blue, Spitz, Ota naevus

8Stage and excise

TBreslow
T1a< 0.8 mm, no ulcer
T1b< 0.8 + ulcer, or 0.8–1.0
T2> 1.0–2.0
T3> 2.0–4.0
T4> 4.0 · a/b = ulceration
  • Breslow + ulceration = prognosis · Clark historical · S-100, SOX10, HMB-45 · BRAF V600 ~half · c-KIT mucosal/acral
  • Margins: in situ 0.5–1 cm · ≤ 1 mm: 1 cm · 1–2 mm: 1–2 cm · > 2 mm: 2 cm · scalp to periosteum · ear: auriculectomy ± temporal bone
  • Thick/ulcerated, III–IV: CT/PET-CT, brain MRI, LDH
Complex flaps after clear margins · graft or dressing first (delayed reconstruction)

9Nodes and drugs

  • SLNB for > 0.8 mm (T1b+), N0 · Tc-99m lymphoscintigraphy + SPECT-CT · gamma probe ± dye · VII monitoring in parotid
  • Positive sentinel → ultrasound surveillance · MSLT-II (1,939, 2017), DeCOG-SLT (2016): completion dissection no survival gain
  • Clinical nodes: therapeutic dissection + superficial parotidectomy (anterior primaries) or posterolateral (posterior scalp) · RT for ENE, many/large nodes
  • Anti-PD-1 ± ipilimumab first-line (~half durable) · BRAF + MEK (dabrafenib + trametinib) · adjuvant nivolumab (2017), pembrolizumab (2018) · T-VEC in-transit
  • Unknown primary in parotid/neck: treat as stage III

10Mucosal melanoma, repair

  • ~1% of melanomas, H&N commonest site: septum, turbinates, sinuses, palate, gingiva · amelanotic, late · always ≥ T3 · resect + PORT · therapeutic neck only · five-year 20–30%
  • Ladder: secondary intention (concave sites) → primary → FTSG/SSG → local (rhomboid, bilobed, forehead, nasolabial) → regional → free · tissue expansion for scalp

11Exam traps ✗

  • Shave biopsy of a pigmented lesion
  • Wide excision before diagnosis
  • Flap before margins clear
  • Facial numbness/weakness missed as perineural SCC
  • Radiotherapy in Gorlin syndrome
