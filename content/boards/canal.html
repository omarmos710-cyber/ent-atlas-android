WAX, FOREIGN BODIES & CANAL STENOSIS

common problems, easy to make worse ✎

1The canal

schematic, not to scale
  • ~24 mm long · bony skin thin, sensitive
  • Isthmus ~6 mm from the drum: objects pushed past it impact
  • Anterior recess (in front of + below the drum) collects debris
  • Straighten: adult pinna up + back · infant down + back

2Wax

  • Ceruminous + sebaceous secretion + keratin · acidic, water-repellent, antibacterial · cleared by migration
  • Impacts: narrow / tortuous canal, stiff hairs, exostoses, cotton buds, hearing aid moulds
  • Blocked ear, hearing loss, ± tinnitus, giddiness · cough via Arnold's nerve (X) · sudden block after bathing (dry wax swells)
  • Soften: sodium bicarbonate glycerine, olive / almond oil, hydrogen peroxide

Syringing

pinna up + back · water at body temperature along the posterosuperior wall → flows behind the wax · dry, inspect drum
Complications: perforation, laceration, bleeding, OE, vertigo (wrong temperature)
Don't syringe: perforation, discharge or ear surgery history · thin old perforation · grommet · vegetable FB (swells) · only hearing ear (relative)
Otherwise microscope: suction, wax hook, Jobson-Horne, crocodile forceps

3Wax evidence

US 2017: treat only if symptomatic / blocks view · no cotton buds, candling or routine removal · NICE NG98 (2018): microsuction or irrigation for hearing loss / blocked view · Cochrane 2018: any drops (even water) beat none, no clear winner

4Foreign bodies

Non-living: beads, stones, paper, cotton, seeds, peas, beans, pencil tips, button batteries · vegetable matter swells, infects
Living: insects (pain, noise) · maggots in a foul-discharging ear
