HEAD & NECK CANCER: BIOLOGY, STAGING, PRINCIPLES

several diseases on one slide · tobacco, HPV, EBV · AJCC 8th · the team decides with the patient ✎

1Who and why

  • ~seventh–eighth commonest worldwide · India: oral cavity (smokeless tobacco, betel) · > 90% SCC · > 70% stage III–IV in low-resource settings
  • Tobacco (cigarettes, bidis, hookah, gutka, betel + areca, snuff · reverse smoking → palate) × alcohol = multiplicative
  • HPV-16: tonsil + tongue base, oral sex, rising · EBV: undifferentiated NPC (salted fish, southern China)
  • Poor dentition, submucous fibrosis, Plummer–Vinson, immunosuppression, RT, wood dust/leather/nickel (sinonasal), Fanconi, dyskeratosis congenita
Field cancerisation: second primaries ~3–5% a year (H&N, lung, oesophagus) → panendoscopy, stop smoking, lifelong follow-up
Premalignant: leukoplakia, erythroplakia (highest), submucous fibrosis, erosive lichen planus, laryngeal dysplasia

58th edition changes

  • Depth of invasion (oral) · ENE in N · separate p16+ oropharynx · unknown primary by EBV/HPV
  • 7th-edition books give different stages · AJCC 9 (NPC), UICC 9 (2025) → state the edition

6Work-up

  • Symptoms, risks, comorbidity, ECOG/Karnofsky, nutrition, support · palpate tongue base + floor · nasendoscopy · cranial nerves · neck levels
  • Biopsy + FNA · p16/HPV (oropharynx, unknown primary) · EBV (NPC)
  • MRI: tongue, nasopharynx, skull base, perineural · CT: bone, larynx · PET-CT stage III–IV, unknown primary, recurrence · CT chest
  • Panendoscopy: extent + synchronous primary · unknown primary: tonsillectomy + tongue-base mucosectomy
  • Before: dental extractions, dietitian ± PEG, SLT baseline + prehabilitation, stop smoking, airway plan, CNS support

7The decision

  • MDT: surgeons, oncologists, radiology, pathology, restorative dentist, SLT, dietitian, nurse, palliative · patient's priorities
  • Stage I–II: one modality (surgery or RT) · choose by function, access, keeping options
  • Stage III–IV: combined · surgery → (C)RT for oral cavity, cartilage/bone destruction · primary CRT + salvage for larynx, hypopharynx, oropharynx, NPC
  • T4b: CRT or palliation · recurrent/metastatic: salvage, re-RT, pembrolizumab ± platinum/5-FU, cetuximab
  • Margins: ≥ 5 mm clear, < 5 close · frozen sections · TLM, TORS → open · free flaps · trach

8The neck

  • N0: elective dissection or RT if occult risk > ~15–20% · oral DOI > 3–4 mm (D'Cruz) · sentinel node
  • N+: therapeutic dissection or CRT + PET-CT at 12 weeks (PET-NECK 2016)
  • Tongue base, soft palate, supraglottis, midline floor → both necks

9Survivorship

  • Most recur in two years · every 1–3 months year one, 2–4 year two, 3–6 years three to five, then yearly
  • Nasendoscopy each visit · TSH every 6–12 months after neck RT · teeth, smoking, second primaries
  • Swallow exercises, dilatation, PEG · TE valve, electrolarynx · shoulder physio, lymphoedema · trismus, xerostomia, hearing, ORN
  • Depression, fear, alcohol, suicide → screen · palliation: fungating tumour, carotid blow-out plan (dark towels, sedation)
  • MACH-NC (107 trials): no adjuvant chemo benefit · KEYNOTE-689 (HR 0.73), NIVOPOSTOP (2025): perioperative immunotherapy

10Exam traps ✗

  • Staging oropharyngeal cancer without p16
  • 7th-edition definitions in an 8th-edition world
  • Forgetting the contralateral neck in midline tumours
  • No dental review or smoking support before treatment
  • No thyroid or second-primary surveillance
