HEAD AND NECK RECONSTRUCTION AND FACIAL REANIMATION

what is missing, what did it do, what is the bed like? · reanimation: what is missing, and for how long? ✎

1Defect analysis

  • Tissues: skin, lining, muscle, bone, cartilage, nerve
  • Function: skeleton (occlusion, implants) · cover (carotid, dura) · lip sphincter · mobile tongue · volume, sensation, mucosa
  • Bed: irradiated or infected → vascularised tissue · fistula · vessels
  • Patient: health, prognosis, wishes

2Ladder and elevator

1 · Secondary intention: concave sites ✓ · free margins ✗ 2 · Primary closure: RSTL, about three times as long as wide 3 · Grafts 4 · Local flaps 5 · Regional flaps 6 · Free tissue transfer 7 · Prostheses
Elevator: go straight to the best rung, often a free flap

3Grafts

  • Imbibition ~48 hours → inosculation three to five days · killed by haematoma, seroma, shear, infection → bolster, quilting, NPWT
  • No take on bare bone/cartilage or irradiated bed
  • STSG: takes better, contracts more, poor colour · mouth, maxillectomy cavity, scalp
  • FTSG: postauricular, eyelid, supraclavicular · nose, lids, ear
  • Septum (L-strut 1–1.5 cm) · concha (ala, ear) · costal (warps) · composite up to about 1–1.5 cm
  • Calvaria, rib, iliac crest · vascular unirradiated bed + rigid fixation
  • Nerve: great auricular ~10 cm · sural 30–35 cm · MABC

4Local flaps

  • Random, subdermal plexus · face up to about 3:1 · scars in RSTL and subunit borders · borrow from lax skin
  • Advancement (V-Y, perialar crescent) · rotation (cervicofacial, scalp)
  • Transposition: rhomboid (Limberg) · bilobed (Zitelli) ≤ 1.5 cm lower nose · nasolabial for ala · note
  • Paramedian forehead: supratrochlear, divide at three weeks · workhorse for nose
  • Full-thickness nose: three layers: lining + cartilage framework + cover
  • Enemies: smoking, tension, irradiation, haematoma

6Regional flaps

  • Pectoralis major: thoracoacromial · salvage workhorse, carotid cover · reaches the zygomatic arch · bulky, sags
  • Deltopectoral: internal thoracic perforators 2nd–4th spaces · delay
  • Temporalis (deep temporal) · TPF (STA) · supraclavicular (transverse cervical) · submental (beware nodes in oral cancer)
  • SCM · trapezius · latissimus (thoracodorsal) · nasoseptal, pericranial: skull base · palatal (greater palatine)

8Beyond tissue

  • Osseointegrated prostheses, obturator (elderly, irradiated, watch for recurrence) · expansion · virtual planning · face transplant (immunosuppression)

9Reanimation: time decides

10Reanimation detail

  • Ask: cause, continuity, stump, duration, which regions, patient goals
  • Eye: drops, ointment, moisture chamber · gold/platinum weight · tarsal strip · brow lift · tarsorrhaphy · botulinum to levator (protective ptosis)
  • Neurorrhaphy at once · interposition graft → function at 6–12 months, synkinesis · skip branches medial to lateral canthus
  • Masseteric (V→VII): commonest, fast, strong, smile on biting
  • XII→VII (split/jump): tone, synkinesis, tongue
  • Cross-face: sural graft, stage two at 8–12 months (Tinel) · only spontaneous smile, weaker · masseteric babysitter
  • Free gracilis (masseteric or cross-face): gold standard, Möbius · lengthening temporalis myoplasty
  • Synkinesis: retraining, botulinum both sides, neurectomy/myectomy

11Exam traps ✗

  • Skin graft on irradiated bed or bare bone
  • Anterior mandible left unreconstructed
  • Radial forearm without Allen test
  • Delaying theatre for a congested flap
  • Waiting beyond two years to reinnervate
