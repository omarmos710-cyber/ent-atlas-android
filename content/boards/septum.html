THE NASAL SEPTUM

deviation, haematoma, abscess, perforation · a swollen septum after a blow is the emergency ✎

1The partition

schematic, front to the left · dashed = the two named fractures
  • Three parts: columellar · membranous · septum proper (cartilage + bone under mucoperichondrium / mucoperiosteum)
  • Small parts: frontal nasal spine, sphenoid rostrum · Little's area anteroinferiorly

2The broken septum

  • Jarjaway: frontal blow · horizontal, just above the cartilage–vomer junction
  • Chevallet: blow from below · vertical, nasal spine → bony–cartilaginous dorsum
  • Cartilage can dislocate off the vomerine groove with no bone fracture · torn mucosa bleeds; intact mucosa traps a haematoma
  • Drain haematoma · reposition, mattress sutures + packing · treat nasal bones together · ignored → crooked nose, asymmetric tip
On call, after any nasal blow:
look at the septum→boggy = haematoma: drain today→pack both sides + antibiotics→review: refilling? abscess?

3Deviated septum

Causes: trauma (lateral blow dislocates, frontal blow telescopes; childhood + birth injury) · unequal palate / skull base growth (adenoid face, cleft, dental) · race, family
caudal dislocationC-shapedS-shapedspurthickening
  • Caudal: lower border in one nostril, seen with head tilted back
  • C: wider side has compensatory turbinate hypertrophy → paradoxical block
  • S can block both sides · spur at bone–cartilage junction presses on the lateral wall
  • High deviations block more: main airflow runs high
  • Also: spur headache, sinusitis, epistaxis, ↓ smell, external deformity, middle ear infection

  • Douches + ointment → silicone button (can irritate or enlarge it) → flap closure; > about 2 cm: anterior ethmoidal artery flap
  • Ask about cocaine at any age

8Evidence

  • Septoplasty beats medical treatment: Lancet 2019 (better quality of life at 12 + 24 months) · NAIROS 2024 (SNOT-22 20 points better at six months)

9Exam traps ✗

  • Not looking at the septum after every nasal injury · a missed haematoma in a child shows years later as a saddle nose
  • Operating when rhinitis is the real block
  • Biopsying the healed anterior edge
