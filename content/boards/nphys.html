NASAL PHYSIOLOGY & SMELL

the lungs' air conditioner · and the organ of smell ✎

1What the nose does

respirationair conditioningprotectionresonancereflexessmell
Explains disease: dry crusts after turbinate surgery · hypoxia with packs · sinusitis when cilia fail · anosmia with polyps

2The natural airway

  • Natural route · bilateral choanal atresia can asphyxiate a newborn
  • Quiet breathing: air runs between turbinates and septum · little reaches the olfactory cleft → weak smells need a sniff
  • Expiration: eddies at the limen nasi ventilate the sinuses
  • Nasal valve = narrowest point, main resistance · inferior turbinate head tunes inflow
  • Nasal cycle: sides alternate every 2.5–4 hours, total flow constant
  • Rhinomanometry (pressure + flow = resistance) · acoustic rhinometry (area along the nose) · peak nasal inspiratory flow

3The lungs' radiator

JobHow
Filtervibrissae: big particles · mucus: down to ~0.5 µm
Warmsinusoids as radiator: air at 0°C near body heat at the nasopharynx in about a quarter of a second
Humidifyto 75% or more · about a litre of water a day
At 50% humidity cilia stop within minutes · bilateral packs lower O₂ (nasopulmonary reflex) · adenotonsillar block → pulmonary hypertension in children

4The mucociliary conveyor belt

  • Whole sheet cleared every 10–20 min, swallowed · 600–700 mL a day
  • Sinus cilia sweep to the natural ostium, even past a new one → surgery enlarges natural ostia
  • Harm: drying, heat, cold, smoke, infection, gases, adrenaline, preservatives, pH change (normal ~7)
  • PCD: immotile cilia → CRS, otitis media, bronchiectasis · low nasal nitric oxide = screen
  • Lysozyme, secretory IgA, IgE, interferon · saccharin test: tasted within about 20 minutes

6The sense of smell

epithelium over cribriform, upper septum, superior turbinate→bipolar neurons regenerate→through cribriform → bulb→tract → piriform, amygdala, entorhinal
  • Taste (VII, IX, X) = sweet, salty, sour, bitter, umami · flavour = taste + smell → "food has no taste" = lost smell

8Assessment

  • Timing: sudden (cold, injury) · fluctuating (sinus, allergy) · lifelong (congenital) · taste vs flavour
  • Age: over 80 most are impaired, about half anosmic · smoking, poor diet add
  • Neuro exam, cognition, mood · endoscopy of olfactory cleft + middle meatus · no topical anaesthetic before testing
  • Tests: coffee, clove, peppermint, lemon each side · alcohol sniff · Sniffin' Sticks (threshold, discrimination, identification) · UPSIT: 40 items
  • CT for sinus disease · MRI bulbs + anterior fossa if unclear, trauma, phantosmia, neuro signs

9Treat what can be treated

  • Conductive: steroid drops head-down · short oral steroid tests reversibility · ESS for polyps
  • Sensorineural: olfactory training: strong odours twice daily for months (post-viral, post-traumatic)
  • Safety: smoke + gas alarms, food dates, hygiene
  • Feeling of a clear nose = TRPM8 cool receptors (menthol) → radical turbinate surgery → empty nose (ENS6Q)
  • Sinus nitric oxide: antiviral, helps V/Q matching · smell loss predicts Parkinson's, dementia (Rhinology 2023)

10Exam traps ✗

  • Calling smell loss a taste disorder
  • Missing polyps as a treatable cause
  • No MRI for unexplained phantosmia
  • Parosmia in recovery is often a good sign
