APPROACH TO EARACHE

primary: the ear looks diseased · referred: the ear is normal, the pain travels along five shared nerves · about half of adult earache is referred ✎

1The rule

  • Primary (otogenic): external, middle, inner ear or mastoid
  • Referred (secondary): another structure sharing the ear's nerves · commonest from teeth and TMJ
  • Normal canal, drum, hearing → examine everything that shares the nerves before "idiopathic" or "psychogenic" · keep under review

2Red flags

  • Elderly/diabetic, severe pain, discharge, granulation on canal floor → necrotising OE
  • Normal ear + dysphagia, odynophagia, hoarseness, weight loss, neck lump, smoker or drinker → aerodigestive cancer
  • Facial weakness, vertigo, hearing loss → Ramsay Hunt, OM complication, tumour
  • Jaw claudication, scalp tenderness, older → giant cell arteritis
  • Swelling behind the ear, pinna pushed forward → mastoiditis

3Smoker, normal ear

Adult smoker, normal ear, any throat symptom → endoscopy · cancer until tongue base, piriform fossa and larynx are seen · still normal: image and EUA · especially over about 45 (NG12)

4Primary otalgia

  • Pinna: perichondritis, cellulitis, trauma, frostbite, CNH (tender helix nodule, wakes when lying on it), skin cancer
  • Canal: OE, furuncle (exquisite), otomycosis, wax, FB, necrotising OE, keratosis obturans, carcinoma
  • Middle ear: AOM (in adults look at the nasopharynx), bullous myringitis, barotrauma, tube, mastoiditis, carcinoma
Zoster oticus: deep burning pain days before vesicles (concha, canal, palate) · facial palsy ± hearing, vertigo · early = mistaken for OE or Bell's

5Referred causes

  • V: dental (caries, abscess, impacted third molars, cracked) · TMD: preauricular, worse chewing, clicking, bruxism · Costen: bad bite + earache, blocked, tinnitus, headache
  • IX: tonsillitis, quinsy, after tonsillectomy, tonsil/tongue base cancer · Eagle: long styloid, pain on swallowing and turning, palpable in the fossa · glossopharyngeal neuralgia
  • X: vallecula, epiglottis, larynx, piriform fossa (classically ear first), oesophagus, reflux
  • C2–C3: spondylosis, whiplash · also GCA, migraine, cardiac, nervus intermedius neuralgia

6Five nerves to the ear

7Assess

  • Pain vs chewing, swallowing, talking, neck · discharge, hearing, itch, water · voice, dysphagia, weight, smoking, alcohol · teeth, bruxism, clicking
  • Pinna, otoscopy, forks → teeth, bite · TMJ and masseters while opening · mouth, tongue · palpate tongue base and tonsils · nasopharynx · flexible laryngoscopy · neck, parotid, spine, temporal arteries, cranial nerves
  • OPG, TMJ/styloid imaging, CT/MRI neck and skull base, ESR/CRP, biopsy · MRI for neuralgia (vascular loop, tumour)

8Pathway

Abnormal ear → treat · granulation: necrotising OE · vesicles/palsy: steroid + antiviral Normal ear → teeth, TMJ, mouth, tongue base, tonsils, nasopharynx, larynx, neck, spine Found → dentist · TMD · biopsy + imaging · neuralgia Nothing → smoker: imaging + EUA · others: review

9Treat the source

  • TMD: explain, soft diet, warmth, analgesia, jaw exercises, no wide opening or gum, occlusal splint · low-dose amitriptyline for chronic muscle pain
  • Eagle: analgesia → styloidectomy (tonsillar fossa or neck)
  • IX and VII neuralgias: carbamazepine → microvascular decompression or section
  • Post-tonsillectomy earache: analgesia, reassurance

Pearl

Ear pain after tonsillectomy is referred via IX, not an ear infection

10Exam traps ✗

  • Ear drops for a normal ear
  • "Psychogenic" before the throat and larynx are examined
  • Early Ramsay Hunt missed before vesicles
