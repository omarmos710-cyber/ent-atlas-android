ORAL ULCERS, WHITE PATCHES & SUBMUCOUS FIBROSIS

most sore mouths are harmless · not healed in three weeks once the cause is gone → biopsy ✎

1Sorting the ulcer

History: duration, recurrence, pain, tobacco, areca, alcohol, dentures, drugs, skin + eye + genital lesions · examine every subsite, palpate for induration, feel the neck
  • Infective: herpangina, herpes, hand, foot + mouth · Vincent, TB, syphilis · candida
  • Immune: aphthous, Behçet · skin: EM, lichen planus, pemphigus, pemphigoid, lupus
  • Trauma: cheek bite, tooth, denture, chemical, thermal · neoplasm: SCC, minor salivary carcinoma, lymphoma
  • Blood: leukaemia, agranulocytosis, cyclic neutropenia, pancytopenia · other: drugs, B12 + folate, mucositis, diabetes, uraemia
Biopsy: ulcer > three weeks after the cause is removed · rolled or indurated edge · fixed · numbness · hard neck node

3Viral

  • Herpangina (Coxsackie A, child): vesicles on pillars, tonsils, soft palate, uvula → 2–4 mm ulcers, yellow base, red areola · within a week
  • Primary herpes (child): fever, vesicles anywhere, swollen bleeding gums · one to two weeks · recurrent (adult): trigeminal ganglion · stress, fever, sunlight, immunosuppression · labialis, hard palate, attached gingiva
  • Hand, foot + mouth: palate, tongue, cheek ulcers + vesicles on hands, feet

4Bacterial

  • Vincent (ANUG): fusiform bacillus + Borrelia vincentii · interdental papillae → gum margins, grey slough, foul · tonsil = Vincent angina · smear · penicillin or erythromycin + metronidazole, mouthwash, dental hygiene · malnourished child → noma
  • TB (painful undermined, tongue), syphilis, actinomycosis (sulphur granules): biopsy vs cancer

5Candida

  • Infants, diabetics, immunosuppressed, dentures, antibiotics, inhaled steroids, RT, dry mouth
  • Thrush: white plaques wipe off → raw red base
  • Erythematous: red, often under dentures · denture stomatitis: clean, out at night, chlorhexidine soak, miconazole gel to fitting surface
  • Hyperplastic: white, won't wipe off, commissures · dysplasia → biopsy, stop smoking, antifungal
  • Angular cheilitis: miconazole 2% cream (± hydrocortisone) 2×/day, up to 10 days · check iron, B vitamins · also median rhomboid glossitis
  • Topical nystatin or clotrimazole · fluconazole if severe · remove the cause
ThrushDrug · course
Mild (child > 4 months, adult)miconazole oral gel 2.5 ml 4×/day after food, or nystatin 100,000 units/ml 1 ml 4×/day · 7 days + 2 after healing · miconazole × warfarin, statins
Extensive, immuno­suppressedfluconazole 50–100 mg daily · 7–14 days
Hairy leukoplakia: corrugated lateral-tongue streaks, won't rub off · EBV in HIV · marks immune failure · regresses on antiretrovirals

6Aphthous ulcers

  • Commonest non-traumatic · recurrent, shallow, painful · movable mucosa, spare hard palate + attached gingiva
  • Minor: 2–10 mm, multiple, red halo · about two weeks, no scar · major: > 1 cm, deep, back of mouth · six weeks or more, scar · herpetiform: crops of tiny ulcers
  • B12, folate, iron, stress, hormones, food, coeliac, Crohn's, NSAIDs, nicorandil · avoid triggers (SLS toothpaste, sharp food)
  • vs viral: recur, spare fixed mucosa, no fever or nodes · severe or atypical: test Behçet, coeliac, IBD, haematinics

7Behçet · erythema multiforme

  • Behçet: recurrent punched-out aphthous-like oral ulcers + genital ulcers + uveitis · ± skin, joints, nervous system
  • Behçet: topical steroids · colchicine 0.5 mg 2×/day · apremilast 30 mg 2×/day for resistant oral ulcers · rheumatology
  • EM: hypersensitivity to herpes simplex or drugs (sulphonamides, anticonvulsants) · bullae → ulcers under pseudomembrane · haemorrhagic lip crusts, skin target lesions · severe → Stevens–Johnson · supportive, steroids if severe

8Lichen planus

  • T-cell · reticular: lacy white Wickham striae, both cheeks · no treatment
  • Erosive: painful ulcers, keratotic edge → betamethasone 500 microgram soluble tablet rinse 4×/day or clobetasol in adhesive base · resistant: topical tacrolimus
  • Erosive + atrophic: small cancer risk → review

9Where the blister splits

  • Pemphigus vulgaris: anti-desmoglein 3 · fragile blisters → painful erosions, often before the skin · fifty to seventy · prednisolone 0.5–1 mg/kg daily + rituximab (first line), taper over months
  • Mucous membrane pemphigoid: gums, cheek, palate · conjunctival scarring · topical steroids · dapsone or systemic immunosuppression if severe/eye · ophthalmology

10Blood disorders, mucositis

  • Leukaemia swells + ulcerates gums · agranulocytosis, cyclic neutropenia: defences fail · blood film, counts, marrow
  • Mucositis (RT; methotrexate, fluorouracil, bleomycin): red, then ulcerated from about the second week · supportive

11Traumatic ulcers

  • Jagged tooth or denture → lateral tongue, cheek · pencil, toothbrush → palate · acid, alkali, hot fluid, aspirin held on a tooth
  • Can mimic cancer (exophytic, ulcerated) · smooth the tooth or adjust the denture, look again in two to three weeks · heals = trauma · not healed → biopsy

12The tongue and its clues

  • Geographic: red smooth areas, white margin, shift daily · harmless · fissured + VII palsy + lip swelling = Melkersson–Rosenthal
  • Hairy: long filiform papillae, brown/black · smoking, antibiotics · median rhomboid: in front of foramen caecum, chronic candida
  • Fordyce: ectopic sebaceous, normal · leukoedema: milky film gone on stretch · smooth red: iron, B12, folate (Plummer–Vinson) · gum hyperplasia: scurvy, leukaemia, phenytoin · strawberry: scarlet fever, Kawasaki · Koplik: measles
  • Pigment: racial, amalgam, Peutz–Jeghers, Addison, Kaposi, lead line · new or changing (palate) → biopsy for melanoma
  • Macroglossia: lymphangioma, haemangioma, hypothyroid, acromegaly, amyloid, Down
  • Nicotine stomatitis: red dots in white palatal papules, pipe + reverse smokers · heat, not nicotine · regresses
  • Tongue-tie: protrudes past lower incisors → rarely affects speech · symptomatic → release

13Submucous fibrosis

  • Joshi, India 1953 · areca (betel) nut essential · IARC group 1 carcinogen
  • T-cells + macrophages → more collagen, less collagenase · fibroelastic avascular lamina propria, atrophic epithelium
  • Twenty to forty · chilli intolerance, burning, vesicles → blanched stiff palate + pillars, fibrous bands, progressive trismus
  • Cancer ~3–7.6% (Lee 7–30%, meta-analysis ~5%) · trismus hides it → biopsy anything suspicious
  • Stop areca + tobacco · intralesional dexamethasone + hyaluronidase twice a week, eight to ten weeks · jaw exercises · surgery: release + graft or flap, coronoidectomy · recurrence common

14Jaw cysts

  • Dentigerous: around crown of unerupted/impacted 3rd molar or upper canine · 2nd–3rd decade, painless jaw swelling · X-ray: unilocular cyst with the crown (maxilla: tooth up to orbital floor)
  • Small: out with the tooth · large: marsupialise first (avoid fracture) · ameloblastoma or carcinoma in the wall → examine lining
  • Radicular: inflammatory, apex or side of a dead tooth · enucleate at extraction · lining left → residual cyst

15Exam traps ✗

  • Repeated mouthwashes for a persistent single ulcer, no biopsy
  • White patch that won't rub off treated as thrush
  • Missing early cancer behind submucous fibrosis + trismus
