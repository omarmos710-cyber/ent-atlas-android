@stand Methotrexate is an antifolate. In head and neck cancer it is an older, well-tolerated palliative drug for recurrent disease. In ENT inflammatory disease it is a steroid-sparing immunosuppressant used in granulomatosis with polyangiitis, autoimmune inner ear disease and other vasculitides. It is given once weekly, and prescribing it daily by mistake has killed patients.

# At a glance | The drug on one page.
## Summary
!table Methotrexate
 | Summary
Class | Antifolate antimetabolite and immunosuppressant
How it works | Inhibits dihydrofolate reductase (cytotoxic doses); at low doses, increases adenosine release and inhibits T-cell activation (anti-inflammatory)
ENT uses | Palliative treatment of recurrent or metastatic HNSCC (weekly intravenous); maintenance of remission in non-severe granulomatosis with polyangiitis; autoimmune inner ear disease (steroid-sparing, limited evidence); sarcoidosis
Dose | Palliative 40 mg/m2 intravenously weekly; immunosuppression commonly 15 to 25 mg orally or subcutaneously **once weekly**, with folic acid (typically 5 mg weekly on another day)
Monitoring | Full blood count, liver and renal function every 2 weeks at first, then every 2 to 3 months
Avoid | Pregnancy (teratogenic; stop 3 to 6 months before conception), significant renal or liver impairment, active infection
!!

# Using it in ENT | Practice.
## Granulomatosis with polyangiitis
After induction with steroids and rituximab or cyclophosphamide, maintenance is often with rituximab, azathioprine or methotrexate; limited sinonasal disease may be treated with methotrexate and steroids. ENT management of crusting, septal perforation, saddle nose and subglottic stenosis continues alongside.

# Safety | What goes wrong.
## Adverse effects
Mucositis, myelosuppression, hepatotoxicity and fibrosis, pneumonitis (cough and breathlessness: stop and investigate), nausea, infection risk, teratogenicity.
## Interactions
**Trimethoprim and co-trimoxazole** (severe marrow toxicity), NSAIDs, penicillins and PPIs (reduced excretion), other hepatotoxic drugs and alcohol.

## Common pitfalls
!pit
- Prescribing methotrexate daily.
- Adding co-trimoxazole for a sinus infection in a patient on methotrexate.
!!
