@stand Sore throats, aphthous ulcers, post-tonsillectomy pain and radiotherapy mucositis all hurt where tablets cannot reach well. Benzydamine is an anti-inflammatory and mildly anaesthetic mouthwash and spray; lidocaine gels and viscous solutions numb ulcers; and barrier products coat raw mucosa. They are adjuncts to systemic analgesia, not replacements for it.

# At a glance | The agents on one page.
## Summary
!table Benzydamine and mucosal analgesics
 | Summary
Agents | Benzydamine 0.15% mouthwash and spray; lidocaine gels and 2% viscous solution; choline salicylate gel (not under 16 years); barrier gels (polyvinylpyrrolidone and hyaluronic acid products); doxepin rinse (specialist)
How they work | Benzydamine: local NSAID-like inhibition of inflammatory mediators and mild anaesthesia; lidocaine: sodium-channel block; barrier gels: coat and protect
ENT uses | Painful pharyngitis and tonsillitis; aphthous ulcers; pain after tonsillectomy (adjunct); oral mucositis during head and neck radiotherapy (benzydamine is recommended for prevention in moderate-dose radiotherapy by MASCC guidelines)
Benzydamine dose | Mouthwash 15 ml rinsed or gargled every 1.5 to 3 hours (dilute with water if it stings); spray 4 to 8 sprays every 1.5 to 3 hours
Avoid | Viscous lidocaine in young children and patients with impaired swallowing (aspiration, choking, toxicity); choline salicylate in children (Reye syndrome)
!!

# Using them in ENT | Practice.
## Radiotherapy mucositis
Mouth care during head and neck radiotherapy includes regular saline or bicarbonate rinses, good oral hygiene, benzydamine from the start of treatment, systemic analgesia escalating to opioids, nutritional support (often a feeding tube), and treatment of candidal infection.
## After tonsillectomy
Regular oral analgesia matters most; benzydamine sprays may help before meals.

# Safety | What goes wrong.
## Adverse effects
Stinging, numbness and taste disturbance with benzydamine; lidocaine toxicity and aspiration when the throat is numbed; salicylate toxicity with large amounts of choline salicylate.
