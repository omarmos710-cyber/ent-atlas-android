@stand Nystatin suspension and miconazole oral gel treat oral candidiasis (thrush) by direct contact with the mucosa. They are the first step for pseudomembranous candidiasis, denture stomatitis and angular cheilitis. Miconazole is absorbed enough to interact dangerously with warfarin.

# At a glance | The drugs on one page.
## Summary
!table Nystatin and miconazole oral gel
 | Summary
Agents | Nystatin oral suspension 100,000 units/ml (a polyene); miconazole oral gel 20 mg/g (an imidazole)
ENT uses | Oral thrush (in infants, inhaled steroid users, after antibiotics, during radiotherapy); denture stomatitis; angular cheilitis (miconazole cream or gel)
Adult dose | Nystatin 1 ml four times daily after food, held in the mouth, for 7 days and continued for 48 hours after resolution; miconazole gel 2.5 ml four times daily after food, held in the mouth, for 7 days and 7 days after resolution
Child dose | Nystatin 1 ml four times daily; miconazole gel 4 months to 2 years 1.25 ml four times daily, older children 2.5 ml four times daily
Avoid | Miconazole with warfarin; miconazole in infants under 4 months or with poor swallowing (choking)
!!

# Using them in ENT | Indications.
## Practice
Treat the cause (rinse after steroid inhalers, review antibiotics, check glucose and HIV status if unexplained). For dentures, remove them at night and soak them in an antifungal or chlorhexidine solution, and apply the gel to the fitting surface. Fluconazole is used for extensive or resistant infection and in the immunocompromised.

# Safety | What goes wrong.
## Adverse effects
Nystatin: nausea, unpleasant taste. Miconazole: nausea, taste change.
## Interactions
**Miconazole** inhibits CYP2C9 and CYP3A4 even as an oral gel: it markedly raises the **INR with warfarin** (bleeding deaths have been reported) and raises levels of sulfonylureas, phenytoin and some statins. Avoid with warfarin, or monitor INR very closely.
