@stand Mepolizumab, reslizumab and benralizumab target interleukin-5, the cytokine that drives eosinophil maturation and survival. They are used in severe eosinophilic asthma, severe chronic rhinosinusitis with nasal polyps, and eosinophilic granulomatosis with polyangiitis (EGPA), a vasculitis that ENT surgeons may be the first to suspect in a patient with polyps, asthma and eosinophilia.

# At a glance | The drugs on one page.
## Summary
!table Anti-IL-5 biologics
 | Summary
Agents | Mepolizumab and reslizumab (bind IL-5); benralizumab (binds the IL-5 receptor alpha and depletes eosinophils by antibody-dependent cytotoxicity)
How they work | Reduce eosinophil production, survival and tissue infiltration
ENT uses | Severe CRS with nasal polyps (mepolizumab is licensed in many countries); severe eosinophilic asthma; EGPA (mepolizumab 300 mg)
Doses | Mepolizumab 100 mg subcutaneously every 4 weeks (300 mg every 4 weeks in EGPA); benralizumab 30 mg every 4 weeks for 3 doses, then every 8 weeks; reslizumab intravenously by weight every 4 weeks
Pregnancy | Limited data; use only if needed
!!

# Using them in ENT | Indications.
## Polyps and asthma
For patients meeting the EPOS biologic criteria, especially those with blood eosinophilia and severe eosinophilic asthma, mepolizumab reduces polyp size and steroid use and the need for surgery. Response is assessed at 6 months and annually.
## Recognising EGPA
Adult-onset asthma, nasal polyps, eosinophilia above 10%, and then neuropathy, purpura, cardiac or renal involvement suggest EGPA (formerly Churg-Strauss syndrome); about 40% are ANCA (MPO) positive. Refer to rheumatology; mepolizumab is used for relapsing or refractory disease.

# Safety | What goes wrong.
## Adverse effects
- Injection-site reactions, headache, back pain; hypersensitivity rarely.
- **Herpes zoster** has been reported: consider vaccination before starting.
- Helminth infection: treat before starting.
