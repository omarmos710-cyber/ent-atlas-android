@stand Alfacalcidol and calcitriol are active forms of vitamin D that do not need renal 1-alpha-hydroxylation, which depends on parathyroid hormone. They are therefore the right vitamin D for patients with hypoparathyroidism after thyroid or parathyroid surgery, and for hungry bone syndrome. Ordinary vitamin D (colecalciferol) is used to correct deficiency, including before parathyroidectomy.

# At a glance | The drugs on one page.
## Summary
!table Active vitamin D
 | Summary
Alfacalcidol | 1-alpha-hydroxycholecalciferol, converted in the liver to calcitriol; adult 0.5 to 1 microgram daily initially, adjusted to calcium (commonly 0.25 to 2 micrograms daily); child by weight
Calcitriol | 1,25-dihydroxycholecalciferol (active hormone); 0.25 to 0.5 micrograms once or twice daily
Colecalciferol | Native vitamin D3 for deficiency (for example a loading course then maintenance by local protocol)
How they work | Increase intestinal absorption of calcium and phosphate and bone resorption; raise serum calcium within days
ENT uses | Hypoparathyroidism after thyroidectomy (with oral calcium); hungry bone syndrome after parathyroidectomy (often started before surgery in severe disease); vitamin D deficiency before parathyroidectomy
Monitoring | Calcium two or three times a week at first, then weekly to monthly; aim for low normal calcium in hypoparathyroidism to limit hypercalciuria
!!

# Using them in ENT | Practice.
## After thyroidectomy
When PTH is low after total thyroidectomy, alfacalcidol is often started with oral calcium; wean both as parathyroid function recovers, guided by calcium and PTH, usually over weeks.
## Before parathyroidectomy
Correct vitamin D deficiency before surgery for primary hyperparathyroidism (it does not usually worsen hypercalcaemia and reduces post-operative hungry bone effects).

# Safety | What goes wrong.
## Adverse effects
**Hypercalcaemia** (thirst, polyuria, constipation, confusion) and hypercalciuria with nephrocalcinosis and stones if overtreated; alfacalcidol and calcitriol have a short half-life, so hypercalcaemia resolves within days of stopping.
## Interactions
Thiazides (hypercalcaemia); digoxin; enzyme inducers reduce vitamin D levels.

# For the examination | What the examiner wants to hear.
## Viva essentials
!exam What the examiner is looking for
- Why **active** vitamin D is needed in hypoparathyroidism.
- Calcium targets in long-term hypoparathyroidism.
!!
## Common pitfalls
!pit
- Using colecalciferol alone in post-surgical hypoparathyroidism.
- No calcium monitoring after starting alfacalcidol.
!!
