@stand Many drugs block the nose, cause rhinorrhoea or damage the sense of smell. Recognising them saves patients from unnecessary investigations and operations. The commonest is rhinitis medicamentosa from decongestant sprays; others include antihypertensives, hormones, phosphodiesterase inhibitors, psychotropics, NSAIDs in aspirin-sensitive patients, cocaine, and drugs that impair smell and taste.

# At a glance | The drugs on one page.
## Summary
!table Drug-induced rhinitis and smell loss
Group | Examples | Effect
Topical decongestants | Xylometazoline, oxymetazoline | Rebound congestion and rhinitis medicamentosa after more than 5 to 10 days
Recreational drugs | Intranasal cocaine and other drugs | Rhinitis, septal perforation, midline destruction; levamisole vasculitis
Antihypertensives | Alpha blockers (doxazosin, prazosin, tamsulosin), beta blockers, methyldopa, reserpine, ACE inhibitors (cough and sometimes rhinitis) | Nasal congestion (neurogenic, from reduced sympathetic tone)
Phosphodiesterase-5 inhibitors | Sildenafil, tadalafil | Congestion (vasodilatation)
Hormones | Oestrogens, combined oral contraceptive pill, pregnancy itself | Congestion
NSAIDs and aspirin | All COX-1 inhibitors | Rhinorrhoea, congestion and asthma in aspirin-exacerbated respiratory disease
Psychotropics | Chlorpromazine, risperidone, amitriptyline, gabapentin | Congestion
Smell and taste loss | Intranasal zinc gluconate (anosmia), terbinafine, ACE inhibitors, metronidazole, clarithromycin, chemotherapy, radiotherapy | Hyposmia, anosmia, dysgeusia
!!

# Using it in ENT | Practice.
## Taking a drug history
In every patient with nasal obstruction or smell loss, ask about prescribed, over-the-counter and recreational drugs and sprays. Stopping or changing the drug, where possible, often resolves symptoms; treat rhinitis medicamentosa with a nasal steroid while the decongestant is withdrawn.
## Smell loss
Most drug-related smell and taste disturbance is reversible on stopping the drug, but zinc nasal sprays have caused permanent anosmia. Olfactory training helps recovery from many causes of smell loss.

## Viva essentials
