@stand Cinacalcet is a calcimimetic: it increases the sensitivity of the parathyroid calcium-sensing receptor so that the glands secrete less PTH at a given calcium. It lowers calcium and PTH in primary hyperparathyroidism when surgery is not possible or has failed, in parathyroid carcinoma, and in secondary hyperparathyroidism in patients on dialysis. It does not improve bone density in primary disease, so surgery remains the definitive treatment.

## Summary

# Using it in ENT | Practice.
## Primary hyperparathyroidism
Surgery is recommended for patients who meet guideline criteria (symptomatic, calcium more than 0.25 mmol/l above the upper limit, osteoporosis or fragility fracture, renal stones or impaired renal function, age under 50) and is curative in about 95% with an experienced surgeon. Cinacalcet controls calcium but does not cure the disease or reverse bone loss.
## Secondary hyperparathyroidism
In dialysis patients, cinacalcet with vitamin D and phosphate binders controls PTH; parathyroidectomy (subtotal or total with autotransplantation) is used when medical treatment fails or causes intolerable effects.

# Safety | What goes wrong.
## Adverse effects
Nausea and vomiting (common), **hypocalcaemia** (paraesthesia, cramps, seizures, prolonged QT), dizziness, rash; gastrointestinal bleeding reported.
## Interactions
A strong **CYP2D6 inhibitor** (raises levels of flecainide, tricyclics, metoprolol and others); levels raised by strong CYP3A4 inhibitors.
