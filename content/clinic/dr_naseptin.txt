@stand Naseptin is a nasal cream combining chlorhexidine (an antiseptic) and neomycin (an aminoglycoside). It treats nasal vestibulitis and crusting, reduces recurrent epistaxis in children, and decolonises Staphylococcus aureus. Mupirocin nasal ointment is the standard agent for MRSA decolonisation. Naseptin contains arachis (peanut) oil and must not be given to patients with a peanut or soya allergy.

# At a glance | The preparations on one page.
## Summary
!table Naseptin and mupirocin
 | Summary
Preparations | Naseptin: chlorhexidine dihydrochloride 0.1% and neomycin sulfate 0.5% cream (contains arachis oil); mupirocin 2% nasal ointment
How they work | Chlorhexidine disrupts bacterial membranes; neomycin inhibits protein synthesis; mupirocin inhibits isoleucyl-tRNA synthetase
ENT uses | Recurrent childhood epistaxis; nasal vestibulitis and crusting; after cautery to reduce crusting and rebleeding; S. aureus carriage (Naseptin); MRSA decolonisation before surgery (mupirocin)
Doses | Naseptin applied to the inside of each nostril four times daily for 10 days to eradicate staphylococci, or twice daily for 2 to 4 weeks for recurrent epistaxis; mupirocin to the inside of each nostril two or three times daily for 5 days, with chlorhexidine body wash
Avoid | Naseptin in peanut or soya allergy; mupirocin for longer than 10 days or repeated courses (resistance)
!!

# Using them in ENT | Indications.
## Recurrent epistaxis in children
Bleeding from Little's area in children is often associated with vestibulitis and crusting; antiseptic cream for 4 weeks reduces bleeding about as well as silver nitrate cautery. Petroleum jelly is a cheaper alternative that also helps.
## MRSA decolonisation
Before elective head and neck surgery in carriers: mupirocin nasally for 5 days with daily chlorhexidine washes, then re-screen.

# Safety | What goes wrong.
## Adverse effects
Local irritation, rash; neomycin contact sensitivity; **anaphylaxis** in peanut allergy (Naseptin); mupirocin resistance with prolonged use.
