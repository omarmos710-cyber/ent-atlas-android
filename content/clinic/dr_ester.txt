@stand Steroid-only ear drops treat inflammation without an antibiotic: eczematous and seborrhoeic otitis externa, the itchy canal, contact dermatitis from other drops, and canal granulation after the infection has been treated. Betamethasone and prednisolone drops are inexpensive and safe in the short term, and they are not ototoxic.

# At a glance | The drops on one page.
## Summary
!table Steroid ear drops
 | Summary
Preparations | Betamethasone sodium phosphate 0.1% drops (ear, eye and nose); prednisolone sodium phosphate 0.5%; steroid in combination with antibiotics is covered elsewhere
How they work | Reduce inflammation, oedema and itch by suppressing cytokines and inflammatory cell activity
ENT uses | Chronic eczematous, seborrhoeic and psoriatic otitis externa; allergic contact dermatitis of the canal (including from neomycin); granulation after treated infection; persistent itch
Dose | Betamethasone 2 to 3 drops every 2 to 3 hours initially, reducing as it settles (commonly three or four times daily for 7 to 14 days)
Avoid | Untreated bacterial, fungal or viral infection of the canal
Pregnancy | Short courses acceptable
!!

# How they work | Mechanism and handling.
## Mechanism
Topical corticosteroids act on keratinocytes and inflammatory cells to reduce cytokine release, vasodilatation and itch. They also reduce canal oedema, which reopens a narrowed canal.

# Using them in ENT | Indications and doses.
## Chronic otitis externa
Treat the dermatosis: avoid scratching, cotton buds and water, keep the canal dry, use steroid drops or a steroid cream on a cotton bud for flares, and look for and treat co-existing fungal infection.
## After infection
Once a bacterial otitis externa has been treated, a short course of steroid drops can help granulation and residual swelling settle.

# Safety | What goes wrong.
## Adverse effects
- Masking or worsening of **fungal** infection.
- Local skin atrophy with prolonged use.
- Rarely, sensitivity to excipients.

## Viva essentials
!exam What the examiner is looking for

## Common pitfalls
!pit
- Using steroid alone in an acutely infected, discharging canal.
- Ignoring a fungal infection that flares under steroid.
!!
