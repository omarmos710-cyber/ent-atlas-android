# Public build media

Public builds include the study text, original diagrams, and source-attributed surgical basics photographs. The source and license for each photograph appear in the relevant lesson and in the surgical figures credits. Photographs may have been resized for display. Adaptations of CC BY-SA photographs carry the source license.

The public web build offers printable vector clinical boards with original artwork. The thyroid board PDF and the other legacy PDFs are excluded because they may contain third-party media. The public Android build offers the HTML boards and lessons offline.

Atlas plates, textbook scans, cropped imaging figures, and legacy PDFs require documented redistribution permission before they can be added to a public build.
