THE THYROID

the whole topic on one board ✎

1Applied anatomy

  • Berry's ligament → gland rises on swallowing. Commonest site of RLN injury
  • RLN: R oblique, L vertical in TO groove; crosses ITA (any way); enters behind inferior cornu · find it in the RLN triangle or at Zuckerkandl
  • Non-recurrent nerve: R only, 0.5–1% → aberrant subclavian (dysphagia lusoria)
  • EBSLN → cricothyroid (high pitch): tie sup. vessels on capsule · ansa from below → cut straps high

Lymph: VI → VII & II–IV
Lingual thyroid (1 in 3000–4000): scan + US before treatment — may be the only thyroid!

3Physiology & tests

I⁻ trap→TPO → MIT/DIT→T4/T3→colloid, release
  • TRH → TSH → T4/T3 ⟲ · gland makes mostly T4 (t½ 1 wk); T3 (t½ 1 day) peripheral
  • TSH = best single screen + free T4 (free T3 if TSH suppressed) · TPO Ab → Hashimoto · TRAb → Graves · Tg → recurrence · calcitonin + CEA → MTC
US (BTA U1–U5 / TI-RADS) → which to FNA · US-guided FNA (Thy1–5 / Bethesda I–VI) = gold standard · scan if TSH low · CT (non-iodinated contrast) or MRI: retrosternal, invasion · MTC: calcitonin, CEA, RET, metanephrines · laryngoscopy before + after · Ca + PTH after

5Hypo, hyper, Graves

  • Hypo ↓: iodine def. · Hashimoto · surgery, RAI, neck RT (~½!) · ENT: hoarse low voice, big tongue, SNHL · anaesthesia → myxoedema coma
  • Hyper ↑: Graves (commonest) · toxic MNG (elderly) · toxic adenoma (1 hot nodule) · thyroiditis → low uptake

9Treating differentiated cancer

TNM (8th = TNM 9): T1a ≤ 1 · T1b 1–2 · T2 2–4 · T3a > 4 cm · T3b gross ETE to straps · T4a larynx, trachea, oesophagus, RLN, subcutis · T4b prevertebral, mediastinal vessels, carotid · N1a VI–VII · N1b lateral
< 55 yr: I M0, II M1 · ≥ 55: I T1–2 N0 · II T1–2 N1, T3 · III T4a · IVA T4b · IVB M1
ATA 2025 recurrence risk: low <10% · low-intermediate 10–15% · intermediate-high 16–30% · high >30% · then dynamic re-grading · surgery → ± RAI → TSH ↓ → Tg + US
  • Lobectomy: low-risk, intrathyroidal, ≤4 cm, N0 · papillary micro ≤1 cm → surveillance or lobectomy · NIFTP = benign
  • Total: > 4 cm, gross ETE, N+, M1, bilateral, irradiated, RAI planned
  • Neck: therapeutic VI (prophylactic debated); lateral = compartmental II–IV, never berry-picking · nerve: working → shave, paralysed/encased → resect
  • RAI: low risk none (ESTIMABL2) or 1.1 GBq (= high dose) · int/high 1.1–3.7 GBq · rhTSH 0.9 mg IM × 2 days · low-iodine diet 1–2 wk · no iodinated contrast ~2 months
  • TSH: <0.1 mU/L high risk · 0.5–2 low risk, excellent response · re-grade at 9–12 months
  • Rising Tg = recurrence; Tg+ iodine− → PET-CT · refractory: lenvatinib 24 mg/day, sorafenib 400 mg bd

10Exam traps ✗

  • MTC surgery without excluding phaeo · lingual thyroid excised without a scan
  • Cystic lateral node, young adult: papillary, not "branchial cyst"
  • Iodinated contrast just before RAI · cords not checked before + after
