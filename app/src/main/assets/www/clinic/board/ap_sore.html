APPROACH TO A SORE THROAT

acute or chronic? · is the airway safe? · antibiotics with judgement · the one-sided chronic sore throat in a smoker is cancer until proven otherwise ✎

1Red flags

  • Drooling, can't swallow saliva, muffled, stridor, sitting forward → supraglottitis or deep abscess · don't lie flat · no tongue depressor in a child
  • Trismus + one-sided swelling + uvula deviated → quinsy
  • Neck swelling, torticollis (child) → retro/parapharyngeal abscess
  • Fever, rigors, neck tenderness after tonsillitis → Lemierre's
  • On carbimazole, clozapine, chemo → neutropenia: urgent FBC
  • Persistent one-sided, otalgia, dysphagia, hoarse, weight loss, lump, smoker → cancer
Acute: days to two weeks · chronic: beyond three weeks

2Acute assessment

  • Duration, fever, swallowing fluids, voice, breathing, earache, trismus · cough + coryza → viral
  • Oropharynx, tonsils, nodes, airway · suspected supraglottitis: flexible scope in a safe setting
  • Antibiotics shorten bacterial tonsillitis by only about a day · analgesia for all (NG84)

3Admit if

  • Can't swallow fluids, dehydrated, collection, airway, systemically unwell
  • IV fluids, analgesia, IV benzylpenicillin, often single dexamethasone (speeds recovery modestly, 2017)

4Scores and thresholds

  • Centor: exudate, tender anterior nodes, fever, no cough · consider at three or four
  • Phenoxymethylpenicillin five to ten days · clarithromycin if allergic · no amoxicillin (EBV rash)
  • SIGN episodes must be disabling, documented, adequately treated · also two quinsies, suspected cancer, child OSA

5Acute causes

  • Tonsillitis: viral or bacterial (GAS commonest)
  • Glandular fever: exudate, palatal petechiae, nodes, spleen · monospot, EBV serology, atypical lymphocytes · no contact sport several weeks · steroids for airway
  • Quinsy: trismus, hot potato voice, swelling above tonsil, uvula deviated · aspirate or incise
  • Supraglottitis: severe odynophagia, near-normal oropharynx, muffled, drooling
  • Deep neck: contrast CT, drain · Lemierre's: IJV thrombophlebitis, Fusobacterium necrophorum
  • HIV seroconversion, diphtheria, gonococcal, herpangina, HFMD

7Pathway

Airway/swallow: drooling, stridor → airway team, safe scope, admit · trismus → drain quinsy · neck swelling → CT Acute, safe: FeverPAIN · can't swallow → admit · carbimazole → FBC Glandular fever: monospot, no amoxicillin, no contact sport Chronic: full scope · asymmetric/node → biopsy, image · criteria met → tonsillectomy · reflux, drip → treat

8Exam traps ✗

  • Tongue depressor in a child with supraglottitis
  • Amoxicillin in glandular fever
  • Agranulocytosis missed on carbimazole
  • Repeated antibiotics for a smoker's one-sided sore throat
