6Intact drum, conductive

  • Otosclerosis (family, young adult, pregnancy, Carhart 2 kHz, reflexes absent) · discontinuity · malleus/incus fixation · tympanosclerosis · congenital cholesteatoma · small effusion · ossicular anomaly
Third window (SCD): low-tone gap, BC better than normal, reflexes present → no stapes surgery until CT
Treat: clear, drain, tympanoplasty, stapes surgery, aid · bone-conduction for discharge or atresia

8Work-up

  • Onset, side, fluctuation, tinnitus, vertigo, discharge, noise, drugs, injury, family
  • PTA with masking, speech, tympanometry, reflexes · OAE, ABR
  • MRI: asymmetric · CT: conductive + normal drum, trauma, malformation · glucose, thyroid, renal, FBC, syphilis
  • Non-organic: repeats differ (normal within about 5 dB) · no shadow curve · SRT > 10 dB better than PTA · Stenger · reflexes present · ABR/ASSR

9Act fast

  • Sudden SNHL (within 72 hours): steroids same day + MRI · NICE NG98: same-day if within 30 days, two weeks if older
  • Asymmetric: MRI for schwannoma
  • Treatable: syphilis, hypothyroid, autoimmune, ototoxics, serous labyrinthitis, fistula
  • Fluctuating with vertigo: Ménière's pathway

10Children

  • Screening: AOAE → AABR · NICU: both · 1-3-6 (screen, diagnose, intervene) · aim 1-2-3
  • 1–2 per 1000 · half genetic (GJB2) · congenital CMV: test within three weeks
  • Work-up: imaging, genetics, CMV, eyes, ECG (Jervell–Lange-Nielsen), renal
  • OME: commonest acquired · watch three months → aids or tubes

11Adults and devices

  • Presbycusis: symmetrical high-frequency · hearing loss ~7% of dementia (2024) · ACHIEVE 977, aged 70–84: no slowing overall, ~48% in high risk (2023)
  • NG98 (2018): aids when communication affected, no threshold
  • CI (TA566, 2019): 80 dB HL or worse at two or more frequencies, both ears · AB words ≤ 50% at 70 dB SPL
  • BAHA, middle ear implants · SSD: CROS, bone-anchored, CI · protect the only ear

12Exam traps ✗

  • Aid for asymmetric loss without MRI
  • Sudden loss called a blocked ear
  • Treatable causes forgotten
