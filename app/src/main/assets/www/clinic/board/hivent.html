HIV AND ENT

an ENT finding is often the first clue · recognise it, test, and protect the theatre team ✎

1The virus

  • Enveloped ssRNA retrovirus (lentivirus) · HIV-1 worldwide, HIV-2 West Africa, milder
  • gp120 binds CD4 · gp41 fuses · p17 matrix · p24 capsid · reverse transcriptase, integrase, protease = drug targets
  • CD4 helper T-cell loss → cell-mediated immunity fails
  • Blood, semen, vaginal fluid, milk, deep body fluids · saliva, tears, urine: negligible unless bloody

2Course

  • Primary, two to four weeks: glandular-fever-like (fever, pharyngitis, nodes, rash, ulcers) · very high viral load
  • Latency ~ten years · persistent generalised lymphadenopathy · virus replicating in tonsils, adenoids
  • AIDS: CD4 < 200 (normal 500–1500) or AIDS-defining illness (Kaposi, PCP, oesophageal candida, extrapulmonary TB, crypto, toxo, lymphomas)

3Ear and nose

  • Seborrhoeic dermatitis, OE, necrotising OE (Pseudomonas, Aspergillus) · Kaposi on pinna
  • Serous OM from adenoids or a nasopharyngeal mass · recurrent/chronic OM · PCP, mycobacteria
  • SNHL (HIV, CMV, syphilis, crypto, drugs) · Ramsay Hunt, facial palsy
  • Sinusitis: usual organisms if CD4 > 200 · low CD4: Pseudomonas, CMV, invasive fungal · HSV ulcers · Kaposi, lymphoma
Adult unilateral effusion in HIV → nasopharyngeal biopsy (lymphoma, Kaposi), not just grommets

4Mouth and throat

  • Candida (commonest): pseudomembranous (wipes off), erythematous, angular cheilitis, hyperplastic (doesn't) · nystatin/clotrimazole if CD4 > 200 · fluconazole if lower: 100–200 mg daily 7–14 days (miconazole gel if mild) · oesophageal (odynophagia) 200–400 mg, 14–21 days · resistance · ART prevents recurrence
  • Hairy leukoplakia: corrugated, lateral tongue, won't wipe · EBV · not premalignant · marks progression → test · biopsy if doubt
  • Linear gingival erythema → necrotising gingivitis/periodontitis · chlorhexidine, debride, metronidazole
  • Giant aphthae: biopsy (HSV, CMV, TB, histo, lymphoma) → steroid, thalidomide · HSV ulcer > a month = AIDS-defining
  • Adult adenotonsillar hypertrophy → biopsy · larynx: candida, HSV, CMV, TB, epiglottitis, Kaposi

5Parotids and neck

  • BLEC ~three-quarters of HIV parotid lesions · painless soft, bilateral (up to 80% on imaging) + nodes · may present HIV (children) · from intraparotid nodes
  • US/CT: many thin-walled cysts · FNA · regress on ART · aspiration, doxycycline/alcohol sclerotherapy, low-dose RT · parotidectomy only if disfiguring or suspicious
  • DILS: Sjögren-like but CD8, antibodies negative, lymphocytic interstitial pneumonitis · ART
  • Nodes: reactive hyperplasia · asymmetric, fast, painful, matted, B symptoms → FNA/excise · TB, atypical mycobacteria, histo, toxo, bacillary angiomatosis, Kaposi, lymphoma · deep neck abscesses

6Tests and ART

  • 4th-generation Ag/Ab assay (p24, positive two to six weeks) → second confirmatory assay · viral load (RNA PCR) · CD4 · WHO 1–4, CDC
  • Before ART: resistance testing, HLA-B*57:01 before abacavir, hepatitis B + C, syphilis · ENT: biopsy any persistent mass, ulcer, node
  • ART at diagnosis, any CD4 (START, TEMPRANO) · usually three drugs, often one tablet · U = U · PrEP tenofovir + emtricitabine
  • NRTI (tenofovir, emtricitabine, lamivudine, abacavir, zidovudine) · NNRTI (efavirenz, nevirapine, rilpivirine) · integrase (dolutegravir, bictegravir, raltegravir) · protease (darunavir, atazanavir + ritonavir) · entry (maraviroc, enfuvirtide)

7Protect the team

  • Needlestick ~0.3% (1 in 300) · mucous membrane ~0.09% · worse: deep, hollow needle from a vessel, visible blood, high viral load
  • Treat all fluids as infectious · double gloves · gown, mask, eyes for trach, endoscopy, drilling, laser · hands-free sharps, no recapping, blunt needles
wash, bleed→report→test source→PEP
  • Don't scrub or squeeze · irrigate eyes, mucosa · source HIV, HBV, HCV · worker baseline
  • PEP within hours, ≤ 72 hours: three drugs × 28 days · UK: tenofovir disoproxil 245 mg + emtricitabine 200 mg (one tablet) + raltegravir 1200 mg, once daily · alt (US): dolutegravir 50 mg daily
  • HBV vaccine or immunoglobulin as needed · follow-up HIV test (6 weeks after PEP ends, or 12 weeks after exposure) · protect partners, no blood donation
  • Older texts: two NRTIs for low risk · now three-drug integrase regimen for all (BHIVA, WHO) · no PEP if source undetectable

8Kaposi sarcoma

  • HHV-8 · classic, endemic, transplant, AIDS (commonest, most aggressive)
  • Face, nose, ears · mouth: hard palate, gingiva, tongue · pharynx, larynx, nodes · violaceous, non-blanching
  • Spindle cells, slit-like channels, haemosiderin · vs haemangioma, pyogenic granuloma, bacillary angiomatosis · look for gut, lung spread
  • ART first · RT (very sensitive), intralesional vinblastine, cryo, laser · advanced: liposomal doxorubicin 20 mg/m² IV every 2–3 weeks, paclitaxel second line · surgery to palliate, airway

9Lymphoma and other cancers

  • NHL (second commonest): DLBCL, Burkitt, plasmablastic (oral) · EBV, extranodal, B symptoms · Waldeyer, sinonasal, orbit, salivary · primary CNS at very low CD4
  • Excision or core biopsy, FNA not enough · R-CHOP/R-EPOCH + ART
  • Hodgkin: commonest non-AIDS-defining · skin cancers, HPV oropharyngeal + anal → HPV vaccine · biopsy what won't heal

10Exam traps ✗

  • Grommets for an adult unilateral effusion, no nasopharyngeal biopsy
  • Excising bilateral parotid cysts that ART would shrink
  • FNA alone for lymphoma
  • Delaying PEP after a needlestick
