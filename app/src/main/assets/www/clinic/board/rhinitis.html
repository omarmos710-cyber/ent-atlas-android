One airway

Allergic rhinitis: about one in five (up to a quarter) · rhinitis → four times the asthma risk · 20–30% of rhinitis patients have asthma · treat the nose, asthma improves
Erectile mucosa: sympathetic empties the sinusoids (opens) · parasympathetic fills them + makes glands secrete · side-to-side swap = nasal cycle

2IgE in the nose

allergen → specific IgE on mast cells→re-exposure bridges two IgE→degranulation: histamine, leukotrienes, prostaglandins
Priming: later, smaller doses and non-specific triggers set it off · child's risk 20% with one allergic parent, 47% with two

3The allergic patient

  • Starts ~three years, rare before two · allergic march: eczema → rhinitis → asthma
  • Seasonal (pollens): bursts of 10–20 sneezes, watery discharge, itchy nose, eyes, palate · perennial (mite, moulds, dander): milder, constant block, ↓ smell, drip, cough

5Tests

  • Skin prick: forearm or back · histamine + diluent controls · read at 10–15 min · wheal > 3 mm over the negative = positive
  • Only about half of positives have disease → read with the history; test when immunotherapy or avoidance is planned
  • Specific IgE (old RAST): slightly less sensitive · smear eosinophils (also NARES) · provocation · endoscopy for polyps, structure
  • Panel: mite, grass, tree, weed, cat, dog, moulds

6Treatment

  • Avoid one allergen: pet out, dust mite covers (alone help little)
  • Nasal steroid = best single drug · full effect in about two weeks · aim away from the septum · children: mometasone, fluticasone furoate
  • Steroid + azelastine spray if either alone fails
  • Antihistamine: sneeze, itch, drip, not block · ipratropium: watery drip · cromoglycate before exposure · montelukast with asthma
  • Decongestant short course · oral steroid short burst only · no depot injections · omalizumab = asthma, not rhinitis

7Immunotherapy

  • Rising allergen doses, injection or sublingual → ↑ IgG (blocking), ↓ IgE · about three years
  • Only treatment that changes the course · uncontrolled on drugs or one dominant allergen
  • Sublingual: anaphylaxis risk almost nil · injections only where anaphylaxis can be treated · Cochrane, 49 trials: fewer symptoms + drugs

9Turbinate surgery

  • Only after a proper trial of steroid spray · fixed hypertrophy
  • Submucosal (microdebrider, radiofrequency), outfracture, partial turbinectomy · reducing conchal bone lasts longest
  • Over-resection → empty nose: crusts, paradoxical block

10Exam traps ✗

  • One-sided symptoms called allergy
  • Decongestant drops without a time limit
  • Surgery before steroid spray · most failures are technique
  • Allergic child with a blocked nose: think adenoids too
