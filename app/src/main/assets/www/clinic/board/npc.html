NASOPHARYNGEAL CARCINOMA

hides where no one looks · shows through its neighbours · treated with radiation, not the knife ✎

1Geography and virus

  • Highest: southern China, Hong Kong, Taiwan · SE Asia, N Africa, Mediterranean, Inuit · rare in India except the North-East · Chinese born in North America: lower risk than in China, still above other Americans (genes + environment)
  • Men ~three to one · peak fifth + sixth decades · ~one in five under thirty (undifferentiated)
  • HLA genes (risk persists after migration) · EBV in virtually every non-keratinising tumour cell (clonal in premalignant lesions: an early event) · salted fish (nitrosamines), low vitamin C, incense + wood smoke · tobacco + alcohol → keratinising

3Spread from Rosenmüller

  • Nodes early, often both sides: Rouvière (retropharyngeal), high level II under the mastoid, level V
  • Highest distant spread of any head + neck cancer: bone, lung, liver · ~a quarter to a third

5See it, sample it, map it

  • Endoscopy + biopsy under LA · nothing seen but suspicious nodes → biopsies of Rosenmüller + posterior wall under GA (sometimes transpalatal)
  • Node FNA with EBV testing points to the nasopharynx · don't excise a high level II node first
  • MRI: soft tissue, spaces, perineural, skull base marrow, intracranial, nodes · CT: bone · PET-CT: distant, residual (chest + abdomen imaging if no PET)
  • Audiogram + dental review before RT · EBV IgA (early antigen specific, VCA sensitive) · plasma EBV DNA: screening, prognosis, recurrence

Older AJCC: T1 = nasopharynx only · N3b = supraclavicular (Ho's triangle) · in every edition low neck nodes carry the worst prognosis, whatever their size

7Radiation first

  • Stage I: RT alone ~70 Gy, nasopharynx + both necks
  • II–IVA: concurrent cisplatin chemoradiation (cumulative ~200 mg/m² matters, 160 after induction) · IMRT spares brainstem, cord, optic, temporal lobes, cochleae, parotids
  • Induction gemcitabine + cisplatin (NEJM 2019, 480 pts): RFS 85.3% vs 76.5% · adjuvant capecitabine for high risk (2021)
  • Metastatic: platinum + immunotherapy
  • Control ~70–80%, five-year survival ~70–80% · failure now mostly distant

8Residual, recurrent, late

  • Neck: comprehensive neck dissection (older texts: radical)
  • Nasopharynx: restage → endoscopic nasopharyngectomy beat IMRT re-irradiation (2021) · open: maxillary swing, transpalatal · not if around ICA · re-RT: protons, brachytherapy
  • Late: xerostomia, caries, glue ear (grommets discharge), SNHL, trismus, hypothyroid, pituitary failure, ORN, temporal lobe necrosis
  • Screening 20,174 men with EBV DNA: sensitivity 97.1%, specificity 98.6% · follow-up 3–4 monthly × two years, imaging yearly × five

9Exam traps ✗

  • Grommet in an adult one-sided effusion without looking
  • Excising a high level II node before endoscopy + FNA
  • Missing a VI palsy
