TUBERCULOUS & SYPHILITIC OTITIS MEDIA

the chronic ear that refuses to heal ✎

1What and how

  • Mycobacterium tuberculosis in the middle ear cleft · syphilis included: same bone destruction + disproportionate hearing loss
  • Uncommon in low-burden countries; important where TB is endemic · rising with drug resistance + HIV

2Painless, thin, resistant

Drum thickened early, then multiple perforations that merge into one large defect
  • Discharge: painless, thin, cloudy, persists despite treatment
  • Hearing loss out of proportion, often mixed (early ossicular + cochlear involvement)
  • Pale thick mucosa, abundant pale granulation · mastoid sequestra
  • Non-tender cervical + preauricular nodes
  • Facial palsy ~10%, may present it in a child

3Suspect it when

  • A chronic ear fails ordinary treatment
  • A mastoidectomy fails
  • Granulation is pale
  • Early facial palsy in a painless ear

Complications

facial palsy · sequestrum · postauricular fistula · Bezold abscess · labyrinthitis

4Prove it

  • PCR of discharge or tissue: answer within days (Xpert MTB/RIF Ultra on tissue)
  • Histology: caseating granulomas, Langhans giant cells
  • AFB smear + culture: a negative smear does not exclude it
  • Chest imaging (normal in many) · tuberculin / IGRA · HIV test
  • CT: looks like ordinary COM, more erosion + sequestra
DDx: ordinary CSOM · middle ear carcinoma · GPA · fungal infection

Viva essentials

  • Painless discharge, multiple perforations, pale granulation, early facial palsy, disproportionate loss
  • Diagnosis by PCR, histology, culture
  • Treatment is medical; surgery for complications

5Drugs first, repair later

HRZE two months→HR four months
isoniazid, rifampicin, pyrazinamide, ethambutol · by sensitivities · at least six months (WHO); longer for bone or CNS involvement
  • Surgery only for biopsy, sequestra, abscesses, complications
  • Reconstruct after treatment: healing slow, fistulae common while active; afterwards repair succeeds in most
  • Facial palsy usually recovers on medical treatment

6Syphilitic otitis

Rare, destructive · congenital or acquired · temporal bone osteitis + necrosis · labyrinthitis: profound, often fluctuating SNHL + vertigo · FTA-ABS · penicillin + steroids

7Exam traps ✗

  • Operating on a chronic ear without sending tissue for TB
  • Reassured by a negative AFB smear
  • Reconstructing during active treatment
