MICROTIA & CONGENITAL CANAL ATRESIA

hearing first, then the ear ✎

1A first-arch malformation

  • Microtia: small malformed pinna · anotia: none · atresia: absent / narrow canal, usually malformed middle ear · usually together, same side
  • ~1 in 6,000 to 1 in 12,000 births · boys, right side · ~10% bilateral
  • Pinna: hillocks round the first cleft · canal: first groove · malleus + incus: first + second arches · inner ear from the otic placode → usually normal
  • Normal pinna + atresia = later insult
  • Syndromes: Treacher Collins, Goldenhar / hemifacial microsomia, Crouzon, branchio-oto-renal

5Investigate

  • OAE + ABR from birth, incl. bone-conduction ABR: cochlear function each side
  • Thin-section CT before any surgery (~five or six years): middle ear + mastoid size and air, ossicles, windows, inner ear, facial nerve · MRI if SNHL
  • Renal ultrasound if pits + neck fistulae (BOR)
  • Jahrsdoerfer score (10 points, CT + pinna): ≥7 → good chance of a useful result

6The timeline

7Hearing and the pinna

  • Bilateral: headband bone-conduction aid in the first months, by six months at the latest
  • Unilateral, normal other ear: speech usually normal · seating, monitoring · more school difficulty than thought → early amplification increasingly offered
  • Pinna: staged rib cartilage, porous polyethylene, or osseointegrated prosthesis

8Opening a canal

  • Select: conductive loss >30 dB · bone better than 20 dB · well-aerated middle ear · favourable facial nerve on CT
  • Pinna first, so the canal is placed correctly
  • Risks: facial nerve, restenosis, graft lateralisation, SNHL
  • Only about half keep a gap under 30 dB → most families now choose bone-conduction devices
  • Preauricular sinus: leave unless infected → excise whole tract
The facial nerve often runs more anterior + lateral, across where the canal would be drilled (first cleft anomalies too) → never drill without a CT map

9Exam traps ✗

  • Delaying amplification in bilateral atresia
  • Opening a canal before the pinna
  • Drilling without a CT map of VII
