SKULL BASE & PARAPHARYNGEAL SPACE

floor of the brain, roof of the nose · in front of or behind the styloid? ✎

1Three fossae

  • Anterior: cribriform (thinnest) + lateral lamella (CSF leaks) · crista galli in front, planum behind · fovea ethmoidalis, ethmoidal arteries across the roof
  • Central: sphenoid, sella, cavernous sinus: III, IV, V1, V2 in the wall · VI free beside the ICA → fails first · clivus: dorsum sellae → foramen magnum (basisphenoid above, basiocciput below)
  • From the sphenoid: carotid + optic bulges, opticocarotid recess, carotid bone dehiscent in some · Onodi cell may hold the optic nerve
  • Lateral: petrous bone (cochlea, labyrinth, VII, ICA) · apex (arcuate eminence, GSPN, Kawase's triangle) · IAC on the back · middle fossa holds the temporal lobes
Foramina: SOF III, IV, V1, VI · rotundum V2 · ovale V3 · spinosum middle meningeal artery · lacerum + carotid canal · jugular foramen IX, X, XI + jugular bulb · hypoglossal canal XII, below + medial
Jugular foramen syndromes: Vernet IX, X, XI · Collet-Sicard + XII · Villaret + Horner (retroparotid)

3Parapharyngeal space

  • Inverted pyramid: skull base → hyoid greater cornu · medial: constrictor + tonsil · lateral: medial pterygoid, ramus, deep parotid
  • Prestyloid: fat, deep parotid, minor salivary rests, maxillary artery, V3 · poststyloid: ICA, IJV, IX–XII, sympathetic chain, nodes

4Nerves, images, tissue

  • Presents via what it reaches first: nose (block, bleed, anosmia) · eye (proptosis, diplopia, vision) · numb V2 / V3 + pain = perineural · trismus · IX–XI hoarse, dysphagia, aspiration · XII tongue · CSF leak, headache
  • Adult one-sided glue ear → look at + biopsy the nasopharynx · pulsatile tinnitus → vascular tumour · exam: all cranial nerves, endoscopy, otomicroscopy, larynx, neck

5Endoscopic endonasal

Binostril, four hands · wide sphenoidotomy + posterior septectomy ± ethmoidectomy, medial maxillectomy, transpterygoid, Draf III
  • Transsphenoidal sella · transplanum / transtuberculum suprasellar · transcribriform frontal sinus → planum · transclival → foramen magnum, odontoid · transpterygoid PPF, ITF, Meckel's cave, petrous apex
No incisions or brain retraction · = open survival for selected cancers (Lund 2010) · limits: lateral to mid-orbit, orbit, skin, anterior / lateral frontal or maxillary wall, dura or brain lateral to the orbit, carotid encased

6Open routes

  • Craniofacial: lateral rhinotomy / degloving + bifrontal craniotomy, en bloc, pericranial flap · exenterate if periorbital fat, muscles, apex invaded
  • Fisch ITF (ear canal closed): A jugular foramen, VII rerouted (glomus jugulare) · B clivus, apex · C parasellar, nasopharynx, PPF
  • Also preauricular subtemporal, far lateral, translabyrinthine / retrosigmoid / middle fossa, maxillary swing, transpalatal

7Closing the skull base

  • Multilayer: inlay (fascia lata ± fat) 5–10 mm under the dura + onlay, ideally vascularised · strip mucosa under it
  • Low-flow: free grafts, bath-plug · high-flow (cistern / ventricle), large, high-risk site, poor healer (RT, steroids, malnutrition) → flap
  • Nasoseptal flap (Hadad 2006): posterior septal artery · leaks > 20% → ~5% · plan before sphenoidotomy or a rescue incision · turbinate, lateral wall, pericranial, temporoparietal fascia, free flaps
  • Lumbar drain three days, high-risk defects: 21% → 8% leaks (2019) → many drain only high-flow, raised ICP, a leak, fluorescein
  • After: no nose blowing, straining, mask ventilation

8Complications & outcome

  • CSF leak (commonest) → meningitis · tension pneumocephalus: sudden confusion, "Mount Fuji" · carotid injury: pack, crushed muscle, urgent angiography
  • Stroke, cranial nerve deficits, vision · sella: hypopituitarism, DI (sodium, urine) · nose: crusts, synechiae, anosmia · trismus, flap failure
  • Malignancy: 5-year survival ~50% · clear margins predict most · postoperative RT for high-risk

12Exam traps ✗

  • Transoral biopsy of a parapharyngeal mass
  • Operating on a prolactinoma first
  • Missing a VI palsy or facial numbness
  • Calling an asymmetric petrous apex a lesion without MRI
