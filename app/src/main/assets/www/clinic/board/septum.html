THE NASAL SEPTUM

deviation, haematoma, abscess, perforation · a swollen septum after a blow is the emergency ✎

1The partition

schematic, front to the left · dashed = the two named fractures
  • Three parts: columellar (medial crura) · membranous (skin) · septum proper (cartilage + bone under mucoperichondrium / mucoperiosteum)
  • Quadrilateral cartilage in front: holds up tip + dorsum · perpendicular plate above-behind · vomer below-behind · small parts: maxillary + palatine crests, frontal nasal spine, sphenoid rostrum
  • Little's area anteroinferiorly: four arteries meet · commonest epistaxis site
  • Nasal valve = narrowest point: septum, lower edge of upper lateral cartilage (10–15°), head of inferior turbinate
Septal disease → obstruction, sinusitis, epistaxis, external deformity

2The broken septum

  • Blows from front, side or below buckle, fracture, dislocate or crush
  • Jarjaway: frontal blow · horizontal, just above the cartilage–vomer junction
  • Chevallet: blow from below · vertical, nasal spine → bony–cartilaginous dorsum
  • Cartilage can dislocate off the vomerine groove with no bone fracture · torn mucosa bleeds; intact mucosa traps a haematoma
  • Drain haematoma · reposition, mattress sutures + packing · treat nasal bones together · ignored → crooked nose, asymmetric tip
On call, after any nasal blow:
look at the septum→boggy = haematoma: drain today→pack both sides + antibiotics→review: refilling? abscess?
Never send home a swollen septum · a missed haematoma in a child may show only years later as a saddle nose

3Deviated septum

Causes: trauma (lateral blow dislocates, frontal blow telescopes; childhood + birth injury) · unequal palate / skull base growth (adenoids + high-arched palate, cleft, dental) · white populations, families
  • Obstruction, one side or both · high deviations block more: main airflow runs high
  • Paradoxical block on the wider side: compensatory turbinate hypertrophy
  • Also: spur headache, sinusitis, epistaxis, ↓ smell, external deformity, middle ear infection

5Five shapes

caudal dislocationC-shapedS-shapedspurthickening
  • Caudal: lower border in one nostril, seen with head tilted back
  • C: one curve, wider side turbinate hypertrophy · S: two curves, can block both sides
  • Spur: shelf at bone–cartilage junction, presses on lateral wall · thickening: organised haematoma, overlapping fragments

9Treating the hole

  • All: saline douches once or twice a day + petroleum jelly to edges, long term
  • Infected crusts: chlorhexidine–neomycin cream or mupirocin × 1–2 weeks
  • Treat the cause first: stop cocaine, treat vasculitis / infection
  • Silicone button (custom from CT): no routine "trial" · can irritate, block or enlarge it
  • Closure: small, inactive · flaps + interposition graft · best under about 2 cm · larger: pedicled anterior ethmoidal artery flap (case series)

10Exam traps ✗

  • Not looking at the septum after every nasal injury
  • Operating when rhinitis is the real block
  • Biopsying the healed anterior edge
