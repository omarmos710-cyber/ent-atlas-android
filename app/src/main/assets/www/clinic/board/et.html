EUSTACHIAN TUBE DISORDERS

a valve that should open with every swallow ✎

1Three jobs, three failures

Jobs

ventilation (equalise pressure) · protection (nasopharyngeal sound + secretions) · clearance (drainage)

Failures

won't open: obstructive · won't close: patulous · locks with pressure: barotrauma

2Adult and infant tube

  • Runs down, forward, medially · bony posterolateral third · cartilaginous anteromedial two-thirds · isthmus at the junction
  • Closed at rest · tensor veli palatini opens it (swallow, yawn) + levator
  • Opening behind inferior turbinate, torus tubarius, fossa of Rosenmüller behind
  • Infant: little elastin, refluxes secretions → otitis media in children · adult pattern by about seven to ten

Also dye / saccharine clearance, sonotubometry, nasopharyngoscopy · symptom score ETDQ-7 · don't Valsalva with an infected nose or atrophic drum

5Treating obstruction

  • Treat nose, sinuses, allergy, adenoids · autoinflation · grommet for persistent effusion / retraction
  • Nasal steroids alone: little effect without rhinitis
  • Balloon dilation of the cartilaginous tube for selected adults: normal tympanogram 52% vs 14% at six weeks (Poe 2018), benefit at one year · consensus 2019: confirmed obstructive ETD after failed medical treatment, not patulous · NICE IPG665

6The tube that won't close

  • Causes: idiopathic · rapid weight loss (fat pad lost) · late pregnancy · oral contraceptives · MS, stroke
  • Autophony: own voice + breathing loud · relieved lying down / head between knees
  • Drum moves with respiration (breathe through one nostril)
  • vs SCD: autophony too, but vertigo or conductive gap + intact reflex
  • Reassure (most settle) · weight gain, nasal drops, cautery / injection at the opening, grommet, plugging
No decongestants for fullness + autophony after weight loss: they make a patulous tube worse

7Barotrauma

Descent / diving: tube fails to open → locks at ~90 mm Hg → pain, effusion, haemorrhage, perforation (see tympanic membrane board)

8Exam traps ✗

  • Missing NPC in an adult with one-sided fluid
  • Decongestants for a patulous tube
  • Valsalva with an infected nose or atrophic drum
