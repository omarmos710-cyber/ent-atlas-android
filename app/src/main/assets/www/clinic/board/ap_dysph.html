APPROACH TO DIFFICULTY SWALLOWING

oropharyngeal or oesophageal? · structural or neuromuscular? · features of malignancy → urgent endoscopy · is it safe to eat? ✎

1Two patterns

Solids → liquids

mechanical: rapid + weight loss = cancer · slow + reflux = stricture · intermittent = ring/web

Both from the start

motility: achalasia

2Red flags

  • Progressive, weight loss, odynophagia, haematemesis, anaemia → urgent OGD at any age (NG12: within two weeks)
  • + hoarseness, otalgia, neck lump, smoker/drinker → H&N cancer
  • Can't swallow saliva → food bolus: emergency
  • Coughing on swallowing, chest infections, weight loss → aspiration
  • Ptosis, fatigable weakness, fasciculation, dysarthria → neurological

3Killian's dehiscence

  • Pulsion diverticulum + failed CP relaxation · older adults · regurgitation of undigested food, halitosis, gurgling, aspiration, weight loss · barium swallow
  • Endoscopic stapling/laser: divides the party wall + cricopharyngeus · open excision/inversion + myotomy for small or difficult
  • Never pass a rigid scope blind

4History and exam

  • Solids, liquids, tablets, saliva? where? how long, worsening? · cough, nasal regurgitation, wet voice · regurgitation hours later · infections, heartburn, weight, pain, voice, neurology
  • Pill oesophagitis: doxycycline, bisphosphonates, potassium, NSAIDs · drying drugs · EAT-10
  • Mouth, teeth, tongue, palate, cranial nerves, neck (nodes, thyroid, gurgling pouch)
  • Scope: pooling in piriform fossae, lesions, cord movement · trial water swallow when safe

6Other causes

  • CP dysfunction: dilate, botulinum, myotomy
  • Plummer–Vinson: post-cricoid web + iron deficiency → post-cricoid cancer risk
  • Osteophytes, thyroid, H&N cancer, post-RT fibrosis
  • Stroke: commonest oropharyngeal, most improve in weeks · Parkinson's, MND, MS, myasthenia, IBM, OPMD
  • Oesophageal cancer · peptic stricture · Schatzki ring · achalasia: dilatation, Heller, POEM · EoE: young atopic men, bolus obstruction · PPI, topical steroid, diet

7Manage

  • Is oral intake safe? SLT: textures, chin tuck, head turn, exercises · NG or gastrostomy if needed
  • Food bolus: endoscopic removal if it doesn't pass → endoscopy afterwards for stricture, EoE, cancer
  • Stroke: validated swallow screen before any oral intake

8Pathway

Red flags → urgent OGD · H&N exam + imaging · saliva → bolus Oropharyngeal → scope, FEES/VFS · gurgling → barium, staple · neurology → SLT Oesophageal → OGD, then barium or manometry Unsafe → SLT, textures, non-oral feeding

9Exam traps ✗

  • Progressive dysphagia blamed on reflux, no endoscopy
  • Blind rigid oesophagoscopy with a pouch
  • No swallow safety check before eating
