2Inverted papilloma

Schneiderian papilloma growing inwards · men 40–70 · one-sided, lateral wall near middle meatus → maxillary, ethmoid · HPV in some
destroys bone by pressurerecurs if leftdysplasia / SCC in 5–10%
  • One-sided block, discharge, bleeding · firm, grey, irregular · masquerades as a polyp
  • CT: focal hyperostosis = attachment site · MRI: convoluted cerebriform pattern
  • Endoscopic (medial maxillectomy): mucoperiosteum off, drill the attachment · long endoscopic follow-up
  • Dysplasia or carcinoma in about 5% → examine the whole specimen · SCC in it behaves like de novo SCC
  • Endoscopic removal + drilling recurs less than old open surgery · malignant change 5–10%

4Maxillary carcinoma

Men 40–60 · early = sinusitis look-alike: stuffy, bloody discharge, facial pain or numbness, epiphora
SpreadShows as
Medialblock, discharge, epistaxis
Anteriorcheek swelling → skin
Inferiorloose teeth, toothache, ill-fitting denture, palate swelling
Superiorproptosis, diplopia, epiphora
Posteriortrismus (pterygoids) · nasopharynx, skull base
Intracranialvia ethmoids, cribriform, foramen lacerum
Five-year survival about a third to a half · worse with apex, skull base, brain
Nodes late (submandibular, upper jugular) · first echelon retropharyngeal, impalpable · lung, bone
  • CT three planes: bone · MRI: tumour vs retained secretion, orbit, perineural, intracranial · chest CT / PET-CT
  • Biopsy endoscopically (not Caldwell-Luc)

5Where above the line?

Above and behind Ohngren's line = worse · dashed = lines of Sebileau (Lederman): orbital floors, antral floors

7Treatment

  • Resect with clear margins + radiotherapy for most
  • Medial maxillectomy (endoscopic or open) · total via Weber-Fergusson · orbital exenteration if orbit invaded beyond periosteum · craniofacial for cribriform / skull base
  • Palate: obturator or free flap · unresectable: chemoradiotherapy · neck: treat nodes
  • Endoscopic = open survival for selected T1–T3 with clear margins, less morbidity (Lund 2010)

8Other sites

  • Ethmoid: adenocarcinoma in woodworkers · medial canthus mass, eye pushed lateral, early skull base
  • Frontal: very rare · forehead, eye down, early intracranial
  • Sphenoid: usually secondary · II–VI palsies, headache

10Exam traps ✗

  • Months of antibiotics for one-sided sinus symptoms
  • A one-sided polyp removed without histology
  • Cheek numbness called sinusitis
