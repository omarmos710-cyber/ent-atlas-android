2Inverted papilloma

Schneiderian papilloma growing inwards · men 40–70 · one-sided, lateral wall near middle meatus → maxillary, ethmoid · HPV in some
destroys bone by pressurerecurs if leftdysplasia / SCC in 5–10%
  • One-sided block, discharge, bleeding · firm, grey, irregular · masquerades as a polyp → biopsy any one-sided polyp
  • CT: focal hyperostosis = attachment site · MRI: convoluted cerebriform pattern; loss of it suggests carcinoma
  • Examine the whole specimen · SCC in it behaves like de novo SCC
  • Endoscopic (medial maxillectomy): mucoperiosteum off, drill the attachment → recurs less than old open surgery
  • Follow-up: endoscopy for years · MRI if the cavity can't be seen

8Treatment

  • MDT · clear margins → postoperative radiotherapy (~60–66 Gy) for most
  • Medial maxillectomy (endoscopic or open) · total via Weber-Fergusson · orbital exenteration if beyond periosteum · craniofacial for cribriform / skull base
  • Positive margins, ENE → chemoradiotherapy, cisplatin 100 mg/m² every 3 weeks
  • Unresectable: chemoradiotherapy · orbit / skull base: induction chemo in selected
  • Neck: dissect or irradiate nodes · obturator or free flap, orbital prosthesis
  • Endoscopic = open survival for selected T1–T3, less morbidity (Lund 2010)
  • Five-year survival about a third to a half · worse with apex, skull base, brain

9Other sites

  • Ethmoid: intestinal-type adenocarcinoma (wood dust): looks like bowel, not a metastasis, multifocal → wide mucosal removal · low-grade non-intestinal: surgery alone, high-grade + radiotherapy · medial canthus mass, eye pushed lateral
  • Frontal: very rare · eye down, early intracranial · sphenoid: usually secondary · II–VI palsies
