2Rhinoscleroma

Klebsiella rhinoscleromatis (Frisch bacillus) · endemic in Egypt, Africa, Central America, E. Europe, N. India · nose → pharynx → subglottis → bronchi
atrophic: foul crusts→granulomatous: woody Hebra nose, lip→cicatricial: stenosis, subglottic stridor
  • Mikulicz cells (foamy, hold bacilli) + Russell bodies = diagnostic · culture the biopsy
  • Antibiotics until two negative biopsy cultures · Dhingra: streptomycin 1 g + tetracycline 2 g daily, four to six weeks, steroids for fibrosis · Lee: ciprofloxacin or tetracycline for months
  • Surgery for airway (tracheostomy) + deformity

5Parasites

  • Rhinosporidiosis (R. seeberi, aquatic protistan) · ponds · S. India, Sri Lanka · pink-purple strawberry mass with white dots, bleeds
  • Sporangia 200–250 µm full of endospores · cannot be cultured · excise with diathermy, cauterise base · recurs · dapsone helps some
  • Leishmaniasis: sandfly · septal nodule → ulcer → perforation, years after a skin sore · biopsy, PCR · antimonials

6Granulomatosis with polyangiitis

upper airwaylungskidneys
  • Granulomatous small-vessel vasculitis (Wegener's)
  • Nose: "a cold that won't go", bloody crusts, perforation, saddle · orbit, palate, ear · subglottic stenosis
  • Body: cavitating nodules, haemoptysis · glomerulonephritis = usual cause of death
  • c-ANCA (PR3) · ESR, CRP · urinalysis, creatinine · chest imaging · biopsy often non-specific
  • Induce: steroid + cyclophosphamide or rituximab · maintain: rituximab, azathioprine, methotrexate · douching · rebuild only when quiet
EGPA (Churg-Strauss): asthma, polyps, eosinophilia, p-ANCA · Sarcoid: septal + turbinate nodules, lupus pernio, hilar nodes, ↑ ACE + calcium · steroids

7NK/T-cell lymphoma

  • EBV · East Asia, Latin America · relentless midline destruction, fever, weight loss
  • Deep, repeated biopsies of viable tissue · CD56, cytotoxic markers, EBV · surface biopsy = only necrosis
  • Radiotherapy + chemotherapy · must not be treated as GPA

9Evidence (GPA)

  • ACR/EULAR 2022: nasal disease + c-ANCA / PR3 weigh heavily
  • RAVE 2010: rituximab = cyclophosphamide, better in relapse
  • PEXIVAS 2020: plasma exchange no help · lower-dose steroid as good, fewer infections
  • ADVOCATE 2021: avacopan (C5a) spares steroid, better remission at one year
  • MAINRITSAN 2014: rituximab maintenance > azathioprine
  • NK/T: asparaginase chemo + radiotherapy · plasma EBV DNA to follow

10Exam traps ✗

  • Months of antibiotics without a biopsy
  • Superficial biopsies in suspected lymphoma
  • Forgetting the kidneys in GPA
