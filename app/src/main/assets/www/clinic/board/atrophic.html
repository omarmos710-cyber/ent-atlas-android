2Viral: the common cold

  • Rhino-, adeno-, coxsackie/entero-, coronaviruses · droplets · incubation one to four days · lasts two to three weeks
  • Burning in the back of the nose → stuffiness, sneezing, watery drip, chills, low fever · mucopurulent if bacteria follow (strep, pneumococci, staph, H. influenzae, Moraxella)
  • Rest, fluids, non-aspirin analgesia (aspirin ↑ viral shedding), antihistamine, short decongestant · antibiotics only for bacterial complications
  • Complications: sinusitis, pharyngitis, tonsillitis, otitis media, bronchitis, pneumonia
  • Cochrane: antibiotics no benefit (2013) · decongestants small benefit in adults (2016) · antihistamine–decongestant small benefit · vitamin C after onset little or none
  • Influenza A, B, C: more severe, more bacterial complications · measles, rubella, chickenpox: rhinitis two to three days before the rash · COVID-19: sudden loss of smell + taste; second wave → mucormycosis surge (diabetics, steroids)

3Bacterial & irritative

  • Non-specific: children, pneumococcus, strep, staph · grey-white membrane that bleeds · or secondary after a cold
  • Diphtheritic: rare with immunisation · grey, tenacious membrane over inferior turbinate + floor, bleeds when removed, sore nostrils + upper lip · isolate, penicillin or erythromycin + antitoxin
  • Irritative: dust, smoke, ammonia, formalin, acid fumes, instruments · sneezing, drip, congestion · settles once removed

  • Simple: infected sinuses, tonsils, adenoids · smoke, dust · septal deviation, synechiae · vasomotor · hypothyroid · block on the dependent side lying down, thick discharge, postnasal drip, headache · treat cause, saline/alkaline irrigation, short decongestant, antibiotics for infection
  • Hypertrophic: infections, chronic sinusitis, irritants, long-term drops, vasomotor/allergic · mucosa, glands, periosteum + bone thicken, mostly inferior turbinate · whole, front, back or lower border → mulberry
  • Reduce: cautery, submucosal diathermy, cryo, laser, coblation, radiofrequency, submucous bone resection, partial turbinectomy
  • Compensatory hypertrophy on the wide side of a bent septum: doesn't reverse → reduce at septoplasty

6Ozaena: picture & tests

  • Columnar → squamous · glands, sinusoids, nerves atrophy · endarteritis · turbinate bone resorbed · sinuses small, thick-walled
  • Young woman from puberty · foul smell she can't smell: merciful anosmia · paradoxical obstruction (numb nerves) · bleeds when crusts lift
  • Green / grey-black crusts · roomy cavities, turbinates = ridges, pale mucosa · posterior nasopharynx easily seen · perforation, saddle · atrophic pharynx + larynx, ear effusion
  • Persists for years · improves in middle age

9Secondary atrophic

  • Syphilis, lupus, leprosy, rhinoscleroma · long purulent sinusitis · radiotherapy
  • Over-resected turbinates: empty nose syndrome → why reduction now spares mucosa
  • One-sided on the wide side of a bent septum

10Rhinitis sicca & caseosa

  • Sicca: hot, dry, dusty jobs (bakers, ironsmiths, goldsmiths) · anterior septum only · squamous metaplasia, crusts → ulcer, bleed, perforation · change workplace, ointment, douche, stop picking
  • Caseosa: rare, one side, men · offensive cheesy pus from chronic sinusitis · bone erosion mimics cancer · clear it out + drain the sinus · good outlook

11Exam traps ✗

  • Klebsiella ozaenae = secondary invader, not the cause
  • Removing too much turbinate
  • Destroyed nose: granulomatous or lymphoma?
  • Simple vs hypertrophic: probe + vasoconstrictor
