CHRONIC LARYNGITIS, REFLUX & SPECIFIC INFECTIONS

a description, not a diagnosis · name the cause, biopsy what doesn't behave ✎

1Prolonged laryngeal inflammation

  • Smoking (main), alcohol, dust, fumes · reflux · voice misuse, throat clearing
  • Sinus drip, dental/tonsil sepsis, bronchitis · unresolved acute laryngitis · allergy
  • Specific: TB, syphilis, leprosy, scleroma, fungi, MRSA · systemic: sarcoid, RA, GPA, polychondritis, amyloid, pemphigus · radiotherapy
Persistent, asymmetric, ulcerated, exophytic, or a smoker: biopsy · TB, granuloma + cancer look alike
  • Dhingra: an entity, hyperaemic + hypertrophic · voice rest, steam, no irritants
  • Lee: drop the label, name the cause · reflux central
  • In practice: describe, name the cause, biopsy what could be cancer or TB

3Non-specific forms

  • Hyperaemic: diffuse, symmetrical · voice fails by evening · hawking, dryness, tickle · dull red rounded folds, sticky mucus
  • Hypertrophic: men thirty to fifty · starts at glottis → false folds, epiglottis, subglottis · squamous change, keratosis · thick dusky folds, swollen false folds (≠ ventricular prolapse), sluggish movement
  • Localised forms: nodules, polyps, Reinke's, contact ulcer
treat sepsis→stop irritants→voice rest + therapy
+ steam, expectorants, treat reflux · stripping one fold at a time, ligament kept, histology for dysplasia

4Laryngopharyngeal reflux

  • Hoarseness, globus, cough, clearing, mucus, dysphagia · heartburn in only a quarter to a third (larynx hurt by small amounts of acid + pepsin)
  • Worse after late meals, alcohol · bitter taste on waking
  • Posterior + interarytenoid oedema, pseudosulcus, diffuse fold oedema, thick mucus, granuloma
  • Clinical, by response · pH-impedance most accurate · scope for Barrett's
  • Diet + behaviour, bed head up · PPI twice daily, half an hour before meals, two to three months · H2 blocker or alginate at night · fundoplication if proven refractory
  • Evidence mixed → treat for a set period, review, stop if no response

7Other infections

  • Syphilis: tertiary gumma, ulcerates, stenoses · never primary · biopsy + serology
  • Leprosy: nodules on epiglottis, AE folds, arytenoids → deformity · dapsone, multidrug
  • Scleroma (Klebsiella rhinoscleromatis): smooth red subglottic swelling · catarrhal → granulomatous → sclerotic · long tetracycline/ciprofloxacin + steroid
  • Candida: inhaled steroids, antibiotics · plaques mimic leukoplakia · systemic antifungal · biopsy if persists

9Evidence

  • 14 RCTs (2016): PPI ~15% higher symptom response, no change in signs
  • US hoarseness guideline (2018): no antireflux drugs without signs of reflux
  • Reflux signs non-specific · 24-hour impedance-pH is the reference
  • Leukoplakia: biopsy · stopping smoking = best treatment for laryngitis + Reinke's

10Exam traps ✗

  • Antibiotics for a smoker's persistent hoarseness, no laryngoscopy
  • No chest film with posterior laryngeal ulceration
  • Short once-daily PPI for LPR, then calling it a failure
