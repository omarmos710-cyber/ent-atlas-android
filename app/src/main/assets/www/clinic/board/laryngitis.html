CHRONIC LARYNGITIS, REFLUX & SPECIFIC INFECTIONS

a description, not a diagnosis · name the cause, biopsy what doesn't behave ✎

1Prolonged laryngeal inflammation

  • Smoking (main), alcohol, dust, fumes, polluted air · reflux · voice misuse, throat clearing
  • Sinus drip, dental/tonsil sepsis, bronchitis, bronchiectasis · unresolved acute laryngitis · allergy (unified airway)
  • Specific: TB, syphilis, leprosy, scleroma, fungi, MRSA · systemic: sarcoid, RA, GPA, polychondritis, amyloid, pemphigus/pemphigoid · radiotherapy
  • Dhingra: an entity, hyperaemic + hypertrophic · voice rest, steam, no irritants
  • Lee: drop the label → "prolonged laryngeal inflammation" + named cause · reflux central · both: biopsy what could be cancer or TB

4Non-specific forms

  • Hyperaemic: diffuse, symmetrical · voice fails by evening · hawking, dryness, tickle, dry cough · dull red rounded folds, sticky mucus
  • Hypertrophic: men thirty to fifty · starts at glottis → false folds, epiglottis, subglottis · squamous change, keratosis, glands enlarge → atrophy · thick dusky folds → bulky, nodular · swollen false folds (≠ ventricular prolapse), sluggish movement
  • Localised forms: nodules, polyps, Reinke's, contact ulcer
treat sepsis→stop irritants→voice rest weeks → therapy
+ steam, expectorants, treat reflux · selected: stripping under microscope, one fold at a time, ligament kept, histology for dysplasia

5Laryngopharyngeal reflux

  • Hoarseness, globus, cough, clearing, mucus, dysphagia · heartburn in only a quarter to a third (small amounts of acid + pepsin)
  • Worse after late meals, alcohol, exertion · bitter taste on waking = nocturnal
  • Posterior + interarytenoid oedema, pseudosulcus (subglottic oedema), diffuse fold oedema, thick mucus, granuloma, contact ulcer
  • Usually clinical: RSI + RFS → treatment trial · 24-hour multichannel pH–impedance most accurate (acid + non-acid) · alarm (dysphagia, weight loss, age > 55) → upper GI endoscopy or transnasal oesophagoscopy (oesophagitis, Barrett's, cancer) · persistent lesion: biopsy
StepDrug and dose · course
Alldiet + behaviour, weight loss, no meals 3 hours before bed, bed head up
First line (mild; many UK units)alginate liquid 10 ml after meals + bedtime · 8–12 weeks
Typical symptoms or signsPPI twice daily, 30 minutes before breakfast + dinner (omeprazole 20–40 mg or lansoprazole 30 mg) · 8–12 weeks → review, step down or stop if no benefit
Not respondingpH–impedance before escalating, other causes · fundoplication only if proven
  • On PPI: + H2 blocker or alginate at night (add-on) · alginate alone = mild
  • Evidence: 14 RCTs (2016): PPI ~15% higher symptom response, no change in signs → set period, review, stop if no response · US hoarseness guideline (2018): no antireflux drugs without signs of reflux laryngitis or gastro-oesophageal reflux · signs non-specific, poorly reproducible

6Reinke's, pachydermia, sicca

FormPicture · treatment
Reinke's oedemabilateral pale translucent fusiform folds, flop with breathing · low rough voice · middle-aged smokers (women present) · voice misuse, reflux, sinusitis, myxoedema · stop smoking, treat reflux + hypothyroidism, voice therapy → decortication (lateral incision, aspirate gel, trim), one side at a time
Pachydermiared/grey heaped granulation, interarytenoid + posterior folds, ± contact ulcer · alcohol, smoking, forceful speech, reflux · not premalignant but biopsy (cancer, TB) · removal, antireflux, voice therapy
Siccaatrophic, foul crusts → dyspnoea · women with atrophic rhinitis · voice better after coughing a crust · humidify, glucose-glycerine, expectorants, treat the nose

8Other infections

  • Syphilis: tertiary gumma, ulcerates, stenoses · never primary · biopsy + serology
  • Leprosy: nodules on epiglottis, AE folds, arytenoids → deformity, stenosis · dapsone, multidrug
  • Scleroma (Klebsiella rhinoscleromatis): smooth red subglottic swelling · catarrhal → granulomatous → sclerotic · long tetracycline/ciprofloxacin + steroid · reconstruct stenosis
  • Candida: inhaled steroids, antibiotics, immunosuppression · plaques mimic leukoplakia · fluconazole 200 mg day 1 → 100–200 mg daily, 14–21 days (nystatin doesn't reach) · spacer, rinse + spit · biopsy if doubt · blastomycosis, histoplasmosis mimic cancer

10Exam traps ✗

  • Antibiotics for a smoker's persistent hoarseness, no laryngoscopy
  • No chest film with posterior laryngeal ulceration
  • Short once-daily PPI for LPR, then calling it a failure
