CYSTS AND TUMOURS OF THE JAWS

the radiograph does most of the work · lucent or opaque? single or multiple? crown, apex or neither? ✎

1Approach

  • Painless expansion · egg-shell crackling · teeth displaced, loose or missing
  • Pain/infection = secondary infection · numb lower lip (IAN) → malignancy or osteomyelitis
  • Pulp vitality: every jaw cyst except the radicular cyst has vital teeth
  • OPG + intraoral → CT/CBCT (cortex, IAN canal, sinus) · MRI soft tissue

Aspirate before biopsy

Blood → AVM, aneurysmal bone cyst (never biopsy blind) · straw → dentigerous/radicular · cheesy keratin → OKC · dry → solid tumour or traumatic cavity

3Where they sit

4Radicular and dentigerous

  • Rests of Malassez → radicular · reduced enamel epithelium → dentigerous · dental lamina (Serres) → OKC, lateral periodontal
  • Radicular: commonest · dead pulp → granuloma → cyst at the apex · root canal or extract + enucleate · residual cyst after the tooth is gone
  • Dentigerous: second · around the crown of an unerupted lower third molar or upper canine · age 10–30 · enucleate with tooth · large in a child: marsupialise · lining to histology (ameloblastoma, carcinoma) · eruption cyst

5Keratocyst (OKC)

  • "KCOT" 2005 → cyst again 2017 (and 2022) · aggressive whatever the name
  • Posterior body + ramus, young adult · grows along the medulla, little expansion → large when found
  • Thin parakeratinised lining, palisaded basal layer, daughter cysts · cheesy aspirate · recurrence up to 30%
  • Decompress → enucleate + peripheral ostectomy or Carnoy · resect if recurrent · long follow-up
  • Orthokeratinised cyst: no palisading, rarely recurs, no syndrome
Multiple OKCs = Gorlin (AD, PTCH1): BCCs, palmar pits, calcified falx, bifid ribs, bossing, medulloblastoma · no radiation

6Other cysts

  • Lateral periodontal (vital lower premolars) · glandular (anterior mandible, ≈ central MEC, recurs) · calcifying (Gorlin) cyst: ghost cells · paradental
  • Nasopalatine: commonest non-odontogenic · heart-shaped between vital upper incisors · enucleate
  • Nasolabial: soft tissue under the ala, women · sublabial or endoscopic marsupialisation
  • Pseudocysts: traumatic bone cavity (teen, scallops, heals after exploration) · aneurysmal bone cyst (curettage) · Stafne (leave)

7Ameloblastoma

  • Benign, locally aggressive · third–fifth decades · ~80% mandible, molar–ramus
  • Soap bubble, expansion, knife-edge root resorption · palisading with reverse polarisation · BRAF V600E → targeted therapy
  • Unicystic (luminal: enucleate; mural: solid) · peripheral · metastasising · ameloblastic carcinoma
  • Resect 1–1.5 cm beyond the radiological edge + soft tissue if cortex breached · fibula + implants
  • Curettage recurs 50–90% · maxillary → skull base · lifelong follow-up

8Other tumours

  • Odontoma: commonest (hamartoma) · compound anterior maxilla, complex posterior mandible · blocks eruption · enucleate
  • AOT: teenage girls, canine · Pindborg: amyloid, Liesegang rings · myxoma: tennis racket, recurs about a quarter · cementoblastoma fused to root

9Bone and fibro-osseous

  • Fibrous dysplasia: GNAS · ~80% monostotic · McCune–Albright · ground glass, blends · observe, recontour after growth, optic canal if vision
  • Ossifying fibroma: well-demarcated neoplasm · enucleate · COD: leave alone
  • CGCG: under 30, crosses midline · check Ca + PTH (brown tumour) · curettage, steroids, denosumab
  • Cherubism: bilateral angles, two to five, regresses
  • Osteomas + polyps = Gardner (APC) → colonoscopy · cartilage tumour = chondrosarcoma until proved otherwise
  • Osteosarcoma: third–fourth decades · sunburst, widened PDL (Garrington) · resect + chemo
  • Metastases: numb chin · LCH floating teeth · Paget: cotton wool, ALP ↑

10Dead bone

  • Osteomyelitis: sequestra, Vincent sign · debride + long antibiotics
  • ORN: above 60 Gy · dental care before RT · PENTO → fibula · HOPON (2019): HBO no benefit
  • MRONJ: exposed bone > eight weeks, antiresorptive/antiangiogenic, no jaw RT · ~1% oncology, < 0.1% osteoporosis · dental work first

11Exam traps ✗

  • Biopsy without aspiration → AVM
  • Curetting an ameloblastoma
  • Several keratocysts, Gorlin missed
  • Giant cell lesion without Ca + PTH
  • Extraction in irradiated jaw or IV bisphosphonates
