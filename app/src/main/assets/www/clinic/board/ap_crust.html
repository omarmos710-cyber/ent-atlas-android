APPROACH TO NASAL CRUSTING

persistent crusting = damaged mucosa or clearance · local or systemic? · ANCA, ACE, biopsy the edge · saline for almost everyone ✎

1Why the nose crusts

  • Mucus dries faster than it clears: damaged mucosa (squamous, scar) · fast turbulent airflow (perforation edge, wide cavity) · cilia fail · granulomatous inflammation
  • Crusts → bacteria, block, bleeding, odour, more damage

2Red flags

  • + cough, haemoptysis, arthralgia, rash, red eyes, deafness, kidneys, weight loss → GPA, EGPA, sarcoid
  • Perforation/palate defect + cocaine → CIMDL · levamisole adds vasculitis
  • One-sided ulcer, mass, bleeding → NK/T lymphoma, SCC
  • Saddle nose → GPA, polychondritis, cocaine, surgery

3History

  • Duration, after surgery? bleeding, whistle, odour (others notice), block, smell
  • Picking, sprays, decongestants, intranasal cocaine · chrome, industrial dust · RT · NG tubes
  • Systems: vasculitis, sarcoid, TB · chest disease, infertility, childhood infections → PCD, CF

4Examine and test

  • Endoscope before and after softening and removing crusts: perforation, granulations, ulcer, yellow nodular mucosa (sarcoid), wide cavity, synechiae · palate, eyes, ears, skin
  • FBC, renal, inflammatory markers · ANCA: c-ANCA/PR3 = GPA, p-ANCA/MPO = other vasculitis · ACE, calcium · syphilis, TB
  • Urinalysis: blood, protein (renal)
  • Biopsy the edge of active disease · often needs repeating · CT: bone destruction, sinuses

6Atrophic rhinitis

  • Ozaena: women, tropical and low-income · atrophy, wide cavities, green foul crusts, anosmia (patient can't smell it) · Klebsiella ozaenae
  • Secondary: over-surgery, RT, trauma, granulomatous
  • Empty nose: wide clear cavity, feels blocked and breathless

7GPA

  • Necrotising small-vessel vasculitis · upper airway in most · may be limited to it
  • Crusts, bleeding, block, perforation, saddle · OME, SNHL, subglottic stenosis, lung nodules, glomerulonephritis
  • c-ANCA + biopsy · rheumatology, renal · ACR/EULAR 2022: crusting, bloody discharge, perforation + PR3 weigh heavily
  • Levamisole cocaine can make ANCA positive (mimic)

8Treat

  • High-volume saline several times a day for all · ointments, oils, humidify · glucose-glycerine, alkaline douche (atrophic) · topical antibiotic for S. aureus · stop sprays that worsen it
  • Perforation: irrigate, silicone button, repair
  • Atrophic: irrigation, Klebsiella antibiotics · severe: Young's operation (nostrils closed for months), implants
  • Vasculitis, sarcoid: steroids, immunosuppression, rituximab · cocaine: abstinence before reconstruction

9Pathway

Red flags: ANCA, ACE, urinalysis, chest imaging, biopsy · one-sided mass → biopsy + imaging Clean and look: perforation · wide post-op cavity · foul green + wide → ozaena, culture · anterior only → dry, picking, sprays Treat: saline for all · cause · stop drugs · re-biopsy if progressing without diagnosis

10Exam traps ✗

  • Vasculitis treated as CRS
  • Biopsying crusts, not the edge
  • Repairing a perforation in an active cocaine user
  • Over-resected turbinates → empty nose
