APPROACH TO FACIAL PAIN AND HEADACHE

most "sinus headache" is not sinus disease · no nasal symptoms + normal endoscopy = very unlikely · CT is not a screening test ✎

1The pain history

  • Site (side, dermatome) · character · severity out of ten (migraine, cluster reach ten)
  • Timing: attack length, frequency, clusters and remissions
  • With: nausea, photophobia, aura (migraine) · red watering eye, rhinorrhoea, ptosis same side (TAC) · nasal symptoms (sinus)
  • Triggers: alcohol (cluster), touch/chewing (TN), menses (migraine)
  • What's been tried · analgesic overuse → daily headache

Sinus pain

Comes and goes with nasal symptoms · unchanged by years of antibiotics and sprays = not sinogenic

2Where it hurts

3Red flags

  • Thunderclap, fever, neck stiffness, confusion, focal signs → emergency imaging
  • New, over 50, jaw claudication, scalp, vision → GCA: ESR, CRP, steroids today
  • Worse lying, on waking, straining, obscurations, pulsatile tinnitus → raised ICP
  • Numbness, diplopia, proptosis, loose tooth, nasal mass → sinonasal cancer
  • Acute sinusitis + periorbital swelling, vision ↓, severe headache → complication

4Examine and test

  • Endoscopy: meatal pus or oedema supports sinus · absent → very unlikely
  • Cranial nerves (V, eyes), TMJ, teeth, temporal arteries, neck muscles
  • CT not a screen: incidental thickening in about a third of normal people · for cancer, fungus, complication, pre-op CRS
  • Dental films · MRI for TN (vessel, demyelination) and atypical features

5Midfacial segment pain

  • Tension-type of the middle third · pressure over bridge, behind eyes, cheeks · feels blocked, isn't · tender skin
  • Normal scope, CT normal or incidental
  • Amitriptyline 10 mg at night, up if needed · judge at six weeks · continue about six months · sinus surgery doesn't help

7Migraine and the nose

  • About one in seven · congestion, discharge, tearing · pain may be facial only → called sinusitis
  • 88% of "sinus headache" met migraine criteria (2004)
  • Triptans no more than a few days a week (overuse) · prevent: propranolol, topiramate (not pregnancy), amitriptyline → candesartan, botulinum, CGRP
  • Contact point: controversial · most with contacts have no pain · surgery only if LA block abolishes pain and others treated

8Pathway

Red flags: emergency imaging · GCA steroids · mass biopsy Nasal symptoms + pus/polyps → sinus pathway, CT if surgery · none + normal scope → step 3 Pattern: throbbing + nausea → migraine · symmetrical → midfacial · short + autonomic → TAC · shocks → TN Not improving: analgesic overuse, neurology, pain clinic
EPOS 2020: pain + no nasal symptoms + normal scope and CT = unlikely sinusitis

9Exam traps ✗

  • Sinus surgery for pain, normal scope, incidental CT
  • Cluster missed because the nose runs
  • Repeated antibiotics for migraine
  • Medication-overuse headache missed
