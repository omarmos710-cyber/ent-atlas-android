THE HOARSE CHILD & THE CHILD WHO CAN'T SWALLOW

same tools for both: flexible endoscopy, instrumental swallow studies, ENT + speech therapist ✎

1Why children differ

  • Larynx high (C2–C3; C3–C4 by other landmarks) → epiglottis meets palate → breathe while sucking · down to C6–C7 by fifteen (C5–C6 other texts)
  • A third adult size · subglottis narrowest · short folds, immature layers
  • Nose-breathers for ~six months → nasal block breaks suck–swallow–breathe
  • Mature chewing by ~two years, following motor development

3Assess the voice

  • Pitch, range, projection, breaks, resonance · voice use, noisy breathing, feeding, cardiac surgery, intubation · syndromes
  • Flexible scope (down to 2.1 mm) · rigid strobe from three or four years · laryngeal ultrasound for movement in uncooperative children
  • Palsy → image the nerve · reflux/EoE → swallow or oesophagoscopy

4Nodules and papillomas

  • Nodules: bilateral, anterior/middle third junction, boys, overuse · voice therapy, regress at puberty, almost never operated
  • RRP: from mother's genital tract at birth · before five: hoarse → stridor · called asthma or croup
  • Repeated microdebrider or cold-steel debulking, spare normal tissue + anterior commissure (web) · KTP alternative · avoid trach (spreads)
  • Aggressive (> ~four procedures a year): cidofovir (discuss small cancer risk), systemic bevacizumab · HPV vaccination prevents next generation

5Paralysis and webs

  • Second commonest congenital lesion · unilateral, often left after arch surgery (~5%) → screen before discharge · weak cry, feeding, aspiration
  • Bilateral: stridor, good cry · Chiari, hydrocephalus
  • Many idiopathic recover → observe if safe · injection: Gelfoam ~six weeks, hyaluronic acid months, CaHA, fat (GA + overnight oximetry) · thyroplasty · ansa → RLN reinnervation works well in children
  • Webs: congenital (subglottic extension, 22q11.2) or after repeated MLS · thin: divide + keel or mitomycin · thick: open
  • Phonosurgery more conservative: cold steel, no laser on true folds, subepithelial infusion

6Penetration or aspiration

  • ~1% of children, many more with prematurity, neurodisability, heart disease
  • History (textures, meal length, cough, colour, chest infections, weight) + observed meal by SLT first
  • Videofluoroscopy: all phases incl. oesophagus · FEES: bedside, breastfeeding, secretions, sensation, no radiation · equal for pharyngeal phase

8Laryngeal cleft

Benjamin–InglisExtent
Iinterarytenoid, above folds
IIinto cricoid lamina
IIIthrough cricoid → cervical trachea
IVthoracic trachea
  • Type I: cough on feeds, chest infections, weak cry · missed unless palpated at MLS
  • Thickened feeds → injection augmentation or endoscopic repair · III–IV: repair + trach + gastrostomy
  • TOF (H-type: later cough on feeds) · rings: stridor + solids · EoE: refusal, vomiting, impaction · tongue-tie: frenotomy when it clearly matters

9Pathway

airway first→voice→swallow→structure
  • Stridor or distress → MLB first
  • Instrumental study before thickened or tube feeds · positioning, pacing, teats, textures, dietitian, reflux · NG or gastrostomy if unsafe ± fundoplication
  • Palpate for cleft · contrast for fistula, ring, stricture · biopsy for EoE · nasal scope
  • Reviews (2022): therapy helps children's nodules · four-week scope rule applies to children (2018)

10Exam traps ✗

  • Papillomatosis treated as asthma or croup
  • Type I cleft missed: interarytenoid not palpated
  • Operating on a child's nodules
  • Laser to the anterior commissure → web
