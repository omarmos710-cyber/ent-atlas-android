3Nasal anatomy & tip support

  • Thirds: bony · ULC + dorsal septum (internal valve, 10–15°) · alar cartilages (crura, domes, external valve)
  • Scroll = ULC–LLC junction · keystone = bones meet ULC + septum: break it → middle vault collapse

Major tip support

LLC size, shape, strength · medial crural footplates on caudal septum · scroll attachment
Minor: interdomal ligament, dorsal septum, sesamoids, skin, spine, membranous septum
Tripod: two lateral crura + conjoined medial crura · shorten / lengthen legs → plan projection + rotation

8The ageing face

  • Skin thins · fat descends + empties · bone resorbs (orbital rim, piriform, maxilla, pre-jowl)
  • Brow: starts over medial canthus, ends on alar base–lateral canthus line · women arch above the rim
  • Lifts: coronal (raises hairline) · pretrichial if high hairline · endoscopic · direct / midforehead (older men, palsy)
  • Frontal branch in temporoparietal fascia on Pitanguy's line (0.5 cm below tragus → 1.5 cm above lateral brow) → stay deep, on deep temporal fascia

9Blepharoplasty

Upper: crease 8–10 mm above lashes, keep ≥ 20 mm lash → brow · fields, Schirmer, thyroid, true ptosis · lower: transconjunctival (fat) or subciliary (skin) · snap + distraction (> 6–10 mm = lax → canthopexy) · negative vector: don't take fat
Also: lagophthalmos, dry eye, ectropion, scleral show, ptosis, diplopia (inferior oblique), milia
Retrobulbar haematoma: pain, proptosis, falling vision → open the wound, lateral canthotomy + cantholysis at the bedside (vision lost within ~90 minutes)

10Facelift & otoplasty

  • SMAS (platysma below, temporoparietal fascia above) · facial nerve deep to it · skin-only → plication → imbrication → deep plane → composite · neck: Dedo I–VI
  • Haematoma 3–15% (men, hypertension) · great auricular nerve = commonest nerve injury · skin necrosis in smokers · pixie ear
  • Prominent ear: weak antihelix ± deep concha · angle 20–30°, helix 15–20 mm off scalp · operate at five to six (85–90% grown) · newborn: moulding
  • Mustardé (antihelix) + Furnas (concha–mastoid) · haematoma → cauliflower ear · telephone ear = middle over-corrected

11Resurfacing & toxin

  • Peels: 10–25% TCA · 35% TCA · phenol → arrhythmias (ECG, one unit at a time) · dermabrasion to pinpoint bleeding · CO₂ vs Er:YAG · no resurfacing 6–12 months after isotretinoin
  • Botulinum toxin A cleaves SNAP-25 · works in 2–3 days, peaks 1–2 weeks, lasts 3–4 months · ENT: spasmodic dysphonia, synkinesis, Frey, drooling · lid ptosis → apraclonidine · avoid in myasthenia, aminoglycosides, pregnancy

12Fillers & scars

  • Hyaluronic acid 6–18 months, dissolves with hyaluronidase · danger zones: glabella, dorsum, nasolabial, temple
  • Z-plasty 30 / 45 / 60° lengthens ~25 / 50 / 75%, turns up to 90° · W-plasty for long scars · hair: Norwood, FUE, regrows at ~three months
Filler occlusion: blanching, pain, livedo → stop, flood with hyaluronidase, massage, warmth · visual loss = ophthalmic emergency

13Exam traps ✗

  • Hump off without osteotomies or spreaders
  • Aggressive cephalic trim → alar collapse
  • Blepharoplasty missing ptosis, dry eye, a lax lid
  • Filler without hyaluronidase to hand · operating on BDD
  • Brow lift dissection superficial laterally: frontal branch
