THE PINNA

congenital anomalies, haematoma and trauma ✎

1Cartilage and skin

  • One plate of elastic cartilage; skin tightly adherent laterally · cartilage depends on its perichondrium
  • No cartilage: the lobule and the incisura terminalis (tragus–helical crus) → endaural incision there avoids cartilage

Development

Six hillocks of His around the first branchial cleft · Dhingra: tragus from first arch, rest from second · Lee: first three hillocks first arch, last three second · adult shape by ~20 weeks' gestation, most adult size by ~nine years
Grafts from the pinna: tragal + conchal cartilage, perichondrium, lobule fat

3Treating anomalies

  • Bat ear: otoplasty, usually after age six, before school teasing
  • Shape anomalies (Stahl's etc.): moulding in the first six weeks while cartilage is soft, no surgery
  • Preauricular sinus: antibiotics for infection · repeated infection → excise the whole tract
  • Microtia: autologous rib cartilage from about eight to ten = standard · porous polyethylene: earlier, single stage, more exposure

4Haematoma: the boxer's ear

  • Blunt trauma (boxing, wrestling, rugby) shears perichondrium off cartilage
  • Tense, fluctuant, smooth lateral swelling, landmarks lost
drain early: aspirate (small, fresh) / incise→pressure bolster, concavities packed→antibiotics, recheck
Untreated or infected → cartilage necrosis + irregular new cartilage = cauliflower ear · draining without a pressure dressing invites reaccumulation

InjuryDo

6Evidence

Cochrane 2004: no trials of aspiration vs incision or of dressings · case series favour incision + bolster or through-and-through sutures (less reaccumulation) · neonatal moulding corrects many deformities

7On call: swollen pinna after a blow

aspirate / incise→bolster, concavities packed→antibiotics→review in a day or two
Lacerations: repair early, cover the cartilage

8Exam traps ✗

  • Leaving a haematoma to resolve
  • Draining without a pressure dressing
  • Excising a keloid without adjuvant treatment
  • Microtia without a hearing test
