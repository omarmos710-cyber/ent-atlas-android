3Button battery = emergency

alkali leak + current→liquefactive necrosis in hours→septal perforation
Remove immediately · no drops, no water, no irrigation: they speed the burn · check for septal necrosis after · paired magnets: same urgency

5Rhinolith

  • Ca + Mg salts laid down for years around a nucleus: foreign body (exogenous) or clot, dried secretion (endogenous)
  • Adults · one-sided block, foul bloody discharge, epistaxis, neuralgic pain
  • Grey-brown or black, irregular, stony-hard, brittle, between septum + turbinates, in granulation · may erode septum or lateral wall
  • X-ray / CT: calcified mass · remove under GA, break up large ones · rarely lateral rhinotomy

6Nasal myiasis

  • Flies (Chrysomya) drawn to the foul nose of atrophic rhinitis, syphilis, leprosy, rhinosporidiosis, infected wounds · India: August–October
  • About 200 eggs at a time, larvae within a day
  • First three or four days: intense irritation, sneezing, watering eyes, headache, thin bloody discharge (often "epistaxis")
  • Then lids + lips swell, maggots crawl out, foul smell · burrow into sinuses, face, palate, eye · fistulae, meningitis, death
pick out every maggot (they flee light)→chloroform water / turpentine / ivermectin→warm saline douches→oral ivermectin + antibiotics→isolate under a net→endoscopy + imaging · treat the cause

7Synechiae

  • Scar bands where two raw surfaces touch: septal, sinus, turbinate surgery, polypectomy, FB removal, fractures, cautery, packs
  • Block + blocked sinus drainage → sinusitis, headache
  • Divide + silastic splint one to three weeks · prevent: no opposing raw areas, splint after surgery

8Exam traps ✗

  • Antibiotics for one-sided discharge in a child without looking
  • Forceps on a bead: pushes it back
  • Drops into a nose with a button battery
  • Forgetting the other nostril and ears
