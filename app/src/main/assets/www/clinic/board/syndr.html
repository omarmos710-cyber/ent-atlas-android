THE SYNDROMAL CHILD

spot when a common problem is part of a syndrome · then expect the airway, the neck and the ears ✎

1Syndrome, sequence, association

  • Syndrome: pattern with one cause (Down, Treacher Collins)
  • Sequence: cascade from one malformation · Pierre Robin: small jaw → tongue back → palate can't fuse + airway blocks
  • Association: clustering, no single cause (VACTERL) · CHARGE became a syndrome once CHD7 was found
  • Congenital anomalies: almost half of paediatric admissions · learn the handful that recur

Viva essentials

  • Down: OME + aids, OSA + post-op care, subglottis, atlantoaxial
  • Treacher Collins vs Goldenhar · CHARGE + choanal atresia
  • SNHL syndromes · 22q11.2: VPI + medial carotid

3Before you operate

  • Difficult airway: small jaw (Treacher Collins, Robin, Goldenhar), big tongue (BWS, Down), MPS → senior anaesthetist + ENT
  • Cervical spine: Down, Goldenhar
  • Monitor after adenotonsillectomy for OSA: often HDU
  • Image carotids in 22q11.2 before pharyngeal surgery

4Down syndrome

  • ~1 in 700 · trisomy 21 ~95%, translocation 3–4% (only inheritable), mosaic · maternal age
  • Learning disability, hypotonia, heart (~40%, AVSD), hypothyroid, low IgA, leukaemia, early Alzheimer's
  • Ears: narrow wax-filled canals · OME almost universal, longer · tubes harder, shorter benefit, more otorrhoea → hearing aids often first · CSOM, cholesteatoma → long follow-up
  • OSA ~60%: midface, tongue, tone, obesity, adenotonsils · oximetry/PSG first · up to three-quarters need breathing support after · often not cured
  • Mild subglottic stenosis: tube half a size smaller
  • Atlantoaxial instability ~one in five · films don't predict → no hyperextension · Grisel linked to excess diathermy

6Treacher Collins

  • Mandibulofacial dysostosis · ~1 in 50,000 · AD, TCOF1 (treacle) · half new mutations
  • Bilateral, symmetrical: down-slanting eyes, lower lid colobomas, absent zygomas, small jaw, microtia + atresia
  • Conductive loss → bone-conduction devices · cleft palate ~a third
  • Airway at birth (± choanal atresia) → tracheostomy · later distraction · intubation always hard

7Goldenhar

  • Hemifacial microsomia · commonest arch anomaly, ~1 in 5,600–26,500, sporadic
  • Unilateral, asymmetrical (more often right): jaw, TMJ, muscles, orbit · tags, microtia/anotia, atresia, ossicles ± VII, inner ear
  • Epibulbar dermoid, upper lid coloboma · graded by OMENS+ (orbit, mandible, ear, nerves, soft tissue)
  • Cervical vertebral fusion → protect the neck · difficult airway from the small jaw

8Robin and Stickler

  • Robin: micrognathia, glossoptosis, U cleft · prone, nasopharyngeal airway, tongue–lip adhesion, distraction, tracheostomy · feeding support
  • Stickler: collagen, commonest under Robin · high myopia, retinal detachment, joints, SNHL or mixed → eye exam for every Robin child

10CHARGE

  • Coloboma, Heart, choanal Atresia, Retarded growth, Genital, Ear · CHD7
  • Short wide ears, small lobe · absent semicircular canals → late balance + motor milestones · VII palsy, swallowing
  • Bilateral choanal atresia may be the presenting emergency

12In clinic

  • History + look at face, eyes, ears, neck, hands, skin · listen to parents
  • Genetics · ECG, renal US, eyes, thyroid, imaging · team care
  • ACMG 2021: exome/genome early · AAP 2022: PSG for every Down child by age four

13Exam traps ✗

  • Hyperextending a Down child's neck at adenotonsillectomy
  • Day-case adenotonsillectomy for Down + severe OSA
  • Missing long QT in a profoundly deaf child
  • Pharyngeal surgery in 22q11.2 without carotid imaging
