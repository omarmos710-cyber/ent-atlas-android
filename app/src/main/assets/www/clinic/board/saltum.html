2Suspect malignancy

  • Rapid growth, pain, fixity, ulceration, facial weakness, trismus, nodes · many cancers still look like adenomas
  • US + FNA (~90% benign vs malignant, Milan categories; up to 30% indeterminate) · US core: 96% accurate, 87% specific (2012) · seeding very rare
  • No open biopsy: seeds, risks the nerve
  • MRI: extent, deep lobe, parapharyngeal, perineural · CT bone · PET-CT high grade · record VII function

3Pleomorphic adenoma

  • Commonest in adults + children · ~85% parotid (superficial lobe, tail) · women, third–fifth decades · slow, firm, mobile, lobulated · deep lobe pushes tonsil + palate medially
  • Mixed epithelial + myoepithelial, myxoid/chondroid stroma, incomplete capsule
  • Superficial parotidectomy with VII preserved · extracapsular dissection for small mobile superficial ones (2014: no more recurrence) · no enucleation, no spillage (multinodular recurrence) · deep lobe/parapharyngeal via neck
  • Carcinoma ex pleomorphic: several % at ten to fifteen years · sudden growth, pain, palsy

4Warthin, oncocytoma, children

  • Warthin: tail of parotid, older male smokers · up to a fifth multifocal, 5–10% bilateral · soft/cystic, may inflame · hot on pertechnetate · rarely malignant · partial parotidectomy or watch
  • Oncocytoma ~1%, elderly, < 5 cm, hot · basal cell adenoma mimics adenoid cystic
  • Children: haemangioma commonest (girls, involutes by five to seven, propranolol) · lymphangioma doesn't involute · solid: up to half malignant, mostly mucoepidermoid

6Treatment

  • Superficial, total or radical parotidectomy · submandibular: gland + triangle · minor: wide excision (palate: partial maxillectomy)
  • Keep a working nerve if it separates · paralysed/encased → resect + graft at once (great auricular, sural) ± mastoidectomy
  • Neck: therapeutic I–V · elective for high grade, T3–T4, extraglandular, VII involved
  • PORT: high grade, close/+ margins, perineural, extraglandular, nodes, recurrent
  • Systemic for metastases · HER2, AR, NTRK targets

7Parotidectomy

  • Modified Blair or facelift incision · flap above parotid fascia · keep great auricular posterior branch · off ear cartilage + SCM
  • Trunk: tragal pointer, tympanomastoid suture, posterior digastric → follow branches forward · monitoring (2015: less early, not permanent, weakness) · retrograde in revision
  • Submandibular: incision 3–4 cm below mandible · Hayes Martin manoeuvre protects marginal mandibular

8After parotidectomy

  • VII: temporary a fifth to a third (~22% monitored), marginal mandibular most · permanent ~1–2% superficial benign, ~7% overall
  • Numb ear lobe (great auricular)
  • Frey: parasympathetic fibres regrow into sympathetic sweat glands → sweating, flushing with meals · ~20% clinical · Minor starch–iodine · reassure, botox · prevent with SMAS, SCM, dermis, fat · tympanic neurectomy (old)
  • First bite pain (deep lobe, parapharyngeal) · sialocele, fistula, haematoma, hollow, recurrence

9Exam traps ✗

  • Enucleating a pleomorphic adenoma
  • Open biopsy of a parotid lump
  • Deep lobe parapharyngeal tumour through the mouth
  • Sacrificing a working nerve that could be freed
