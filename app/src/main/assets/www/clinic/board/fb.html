FOREIGN BODIES OF THE AIRWAY

a negative bronchoscopy is far better than a missed foreign body ✎

1Who, what, where

  • Children, over half under four · inhale while laughing, crying, running · no molars, weak cough
  • Adults: coma, sleep, drink, seizures, anaesthesia (teeth, dentures), neurological disease, elderly
  • Vegetable (peanuts, seeds, beans; watermelon seeds in Egypt): swell + oils → vegetal bronchitis
  • Non-vegetable: toys, beads, pins, coins, pen caps · inert ones stay silent · adult food bolus = "café coronary"
  • Large above the glottis · small → right main bronchus (wider, shorter, more vertical; less so in young children)

2Three stages

choke, cough, wheeze→silent interval→complications
The event is often unwitnessed or forgotten · the quiet stage lasts hours to weeks
SiteFeatures
Larynxcomplete: aphonia, no cough → rapid death · partial: hoarse, croupy, stridor
Tracheabiphasic stridor, wheeze at open mouth · mobile: audible slap, palpable thud
Bronchustriad: cough, wheeze, reduced air entry · later collapse, emphysema, pneumonia, bronchiectasis, abscess

3Four valves

  • Check: hyper-resonant, quiet, mediastinum shifts away · stop: dull, shifts towards
  • Bulla → pneumothorax · a mobile object can switch sides

4Think of it

New one-sided wheeze, no response to bronchodilators, after choking · recurrent pneumonia in one lobe · cough after nuts → bronchoscopy
  • A witnessed choking episode: the single best clue · ask the whole family
  • Examine rate, effort, stridor, voice, one-sided wheeze, air entry, slap

5Imaging

  • Neck films AP + lateral · coin in trachea edge-on on PA (glottis sagittal), oesophagus flat
  • Chest PA + lateral: object, collapse, one-sided hyperinflation, pneumothorax
  • Inspiratory + expiratory (decubitus in toddlers) or fluoroscopy: air trapping, mediastinum swings to the normal side on expiration
  • Low-dose CT, virtual bronchoscopy in doubtful stable cases · high negative predictive value
Most are radiolucent nuts + seeds · film often normal on day one · suggestive story → bronchoscope

6First aid

WhoDo
Infant < one yearfive back blows (prone, head down) → five chest thrusts · no abdominal thrusts
Child, adultfive back blows → five abdominal thrusts (Heimlich)
UnconsciousCPR · remove a visible object · no blind finger sweep
  • Failing: laryngoscopy + Magill forceps, needle cricothyroidotomy (small child), tracheostomy
  • Coughing well? Keep coughing, calm transfer · blows may make it complete
  • ERC 2021 kept this sequence

7Rigid bronchoscopy

  • Larynx: direct laryngoscopy (laryngofissure if impacted) · trach first if airway compromised
  • Rigid ventilating bronchoscope, GA, spontaneous ventilation, agreed plan
  • Scope of right size ± one either side, telescopes, optical forceps, two laryngoscopes, Dormia, Fogarty, suction · check all · rehearse on a duplicate
  • Urgent: obstruction, swelling vegetable · otherwise next planned list, not the middle of the night
  • Small: through scope · large: held at tip, all out together · sharp: sheathed or trailing
  • Steroids, physio, antibiotics · C-arm for peripheral metal · flexible in selected adults · thoracotomy if unreachable

8When it goes wrong

  • Subglottic oedema, bronchospasm, bleeding granulation, pneumothorax, nut fragments
  • Dropped in the glottis → push into one bronchus, ventilate the other, retrieve
  • Retained: recurrent pneumonia, bronchiectasis, abscess, stenosis
  • Prevent: no nuts, popcorn, hard sweets, whole grapes under four or five · no small parts

9Exam traps ✗

  • Excluding it on a normal chest film
  • Toddler's sudden one-sided wheeze called asthma
  • Back blows or Heimlich in someone coughing well
  • Starting without right-sized instruments checked
