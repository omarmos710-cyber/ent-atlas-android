2Benign pinna lumps

  • Keloid: after piercing, darker skin · excision alone recurs → + steroid injection, pressure clips, rarely RT (Dhingra: option; Lee: rarely)
  • Strawberry haemangioma: grows in year one, regresses by ~five years · port-wine stain doesn't · venolymphatic malformations may need excision
  • Keratoacanthoma: fast crater + keratin plug, may regress, looks malignant → usually excised
  • Sebaceous / dermoid cysts, papilloma, cutaneous horn, neurofibroma

  • SCC: ~1 cm margins if small · large, within 1 cm of canal, or nodes → partial / total amputation + parotidectomy + neck dissection
  • BCC: RT if superficial, no cartilage · deeper → excision (Mohs)
  • Melanoma: wedge if small on helix · wider + parotid / neck as staged · sentinel node if >0.8 mm
  • Ear = high-risk site: wider margins / Mohs; ear SCC spreads to nodes more

5Canal tumours

  • Ceruminous gland: firm skin-covered mass, outer canal · malignant (adenoid cystic, ceruminous adenocarcinoma) outnumber benign about two to one · wide excision ± RT
  • SCC of the canal: after long chronic discharge · bloody discharge, pain, granulation that won't heal · facial palsy = advanced

6Assess

Biopsy any non-healing lesion · CT + MRI for deep / canal tumours · pre / postauricular, parotid, upper neck nodes · DDx: chondrodermatitis nodularis (pressure relief), perichondritis, necrotising OE, chronic granulation

7Exam traps ✗

  • Creams for months on a non-healing helical ulcer
  • A gouge on exostoses
  • Missing canal carcinoma: chronic discharge + new pain
