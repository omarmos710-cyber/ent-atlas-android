APPROACH TO A LUMP IN THE THROAT

globus: a lump feeling, no trouble swallowing food · recognise the typical, look for the atypical, examine fully · then explain with confidence ✎

1Typical globus

  • Fluctuates · worse with stress, tiredness, throat clearing · often months · middle age, slightly more women
  • Common, benign · many new ENT referrals · patients fear cancer

2What lies behind it

  • Multifactorial: LPR, raised cricopharyngeal tone, postnasal drip, throat clearing, muscle tension, stress, anxiety, pharyngeal hypersensitivity
  • Occasionally: thyroid swelling, osteophytes, long uvula, tongue-base lymphoid hypertrophy

3Not globus if…

  • Pain, odynophagia, referred otalgia
  • True dysphagia, food sticking, regurgitation, weight loss
  • One-sided or lateralised, or progressive
  • Hoarseness, haemoptysis, neck lump
  • Smoker or heavy drinker over 50
Any of these → investigate for hypopharyngeal, laryngeal, tonsil cancer and dysphagia causes

4Examine, then stop

  • Mouth, oropharynx · palpate tongue base and tonsils · neck, thyroid
  • Flexible endoscopy: nose, nasopharynx, oropharynx, hypopharynx, larynx · piriform pooling, postcricoid
  • Reflux signs (interarytenoid thickening, oedema) common and non-specific
  • Typical + normal exam = no further tests · no routine barium swallow
  • Atypical: OGD/barium for dysphagia · US for thyroid · imaging/EUA if cancer suspected

5Explanation is treatment

  • Common, real, benign, not cancer after a full exam · many improve with that alone
  • Sip or swallow instead of clearing · stop smoking · hydrate
  • Speech therapy: clearing, muscle tension, voice
  • Reflux trial: diet, alginate after meals and bed, PPI if typical reflux · unproven for globus alone (trials disappointing)
  • Persistent: low-dose amitriptyline, help with anxiety

6Pathway

Typical? midline, between meals, eased by eating, painless → step 2 · red flag → endoscopy + imaging Full examination: abnormal → investigate it · normal → treat Treat: explain · less clearing · speech therapy · reflux trial · discharge with safety net
NICE suggested-cancer guidance does not list isolated globus as a red flag

7Exam traps ✗

  • Painful or one-sided symptom called globus
  • Repeated tests for typical globus feed the fear
  • Indefinite PPI without review
