ORAL & OROPHARYNGEAL CANCER

one visible and surgical, one hidden and often HPV · look, feel, and feel the neck ✎

1Two regions, one disease

  • Oral: lip, buccal mucosa, oral tongue (front two-thirds), floor, alveolus, retromolar trigone, hard palate
  • Oropharynx: tongue base, tonsil + pillars, soft palate + uvula, posterior + lateral walls
  • India: ~a fifth of male cancers, buccal + tongue lead · West: lip commonest · oropharynx rising (HPV)
  • Smoking ~6× · alcohol multiplies · areca, pan, chewed tobacco · reverse smoking → palate · HPV 16 · teeth, Plummer-Vinson, sun
  • Field cancerisation: ~15% get a second primary (head + neck, oesophagus, lung)

2Look, feel, the neck

  • Non-healing ulcer: raised, rolled, everted edge, shaggy base, induration
  • Pain referred to the ear (V3, IX) · hot-potato voice (tongue base) · loose teeth, bad denture fit · trismus · numb lip · neck lump · fetor
  • Light every subsite · palpate lesion + bimanual floor / tongue · feel the tongue base + tonsils · both necks level by level · V, VII, XII
Refer urgently: ulcer or white / red patch > 3 weeks · unexplained lump in mouth or neck · loose tooth · one-sided sore throat or earache with a normal ear

5Depth and the mandible

  • Thickness predicts nodes: occult spread rises beyond ~4 mm → depth of invasion in AJCC 8 T stage · elective neck if deeper than ~3–4 mm
  • Tongue T1–2: partial glossectomy ± graft / flap, or RT + brachytherapy · advanced: commando (glossectomy + floor + mandible + neck) · total glossectomy may need laryngectomy
Periosteum → marginal · medulla or atrophic edentulous jaw → segmental · enters at the alveolar crest (teeth) or occlusal surface (edentulous) · never irradiate an invaded mandible

7Oropharynx, site by site

  • Tongue base: silent, blamed on lingual tonsil · ~70% have nodes (occult primary site) · late otalgia, hot-potato voice, fixation · bigger to the finger than the mirror
  • Tonsil: necrotic ulcer or smooth big tonsil · ~half nodes, jugulodigastric first · lymphoma: smooth one-sided
  • Faucial arch: superficial, early, slow, like oral cancer · walls: submucosal, nodes ~60%, retropharyngeal
  • Benign: papilloma, haemangioma, palatal pleomorphic adenoma, vallecular cyst

9Treatment

  • Oral: surgery first · advanced: resect, reconstruct, neck → RT, or chemoRT for positive margins / ENE
  • Oropharynx: early: IMRT or TORS + neck (ORATOR 2019: similar swallowing) · advanced: cisplatin chemoRT
  • Cetuximab instead of cisplatin worse in HPV + (De-ESCALaTE 97.5% vs 89.4%, RTOG 1016) · pembrolizumab: recurrent / metastatic (KEYNOTE-048), around surgery (KEYNOTE-689)
  • N0: elective neck if risk > ~20% (I–III oral, II–IV oropharynx) · elective dissection beat waiting: 80% vs 67.5% (2015) · sentinel node in early oral
  • Flaps: radial forearm, ALT, fibula, pectoralis · worse with ENE, low nodes, margins

10Exam traps ✗

  • Mouthwashes for a persistent ulcer instead of a biopsy
  • Judging a tongue base cancer without feeling
  • Cystic adult neck lump excised as a branchial cyst
  • Irradiating an invaded mandible
