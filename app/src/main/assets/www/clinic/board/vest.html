VESTIBULAR TESTING

bedside and laboratory: which side, and peripheral or central? ✎

1The parts and the tests

  • Canals: angular acceleration · utricle + saccule: linear + tilt
  • Right labyrinth fails → eyes drift slowly right, flick left: nystagmus beats away from a dead labyrinth, towards an irritated one
  • Ewald: eyes move in the stimulated canal's plane · lateral canal: ampullopetal stimulates more; vertical canals the reverse

2Bedside: first, often enough

  • Nystagmus in primary + each gaze, with and without fixation (Frenzel) · peripheral: horizontal-torsional, suppressed by fixation
  • Alexander's degrees: 1st only looking to the fast phase · 2nd also straight ahead · 3rd every direction
  • Central: downbeat (craniocervical: Chiari, cerebellar degeneration) · upbeat, pure torsional (brainstem) · pendular (MS, congenital)
  • Head impulse: catch-up saccade = failed VOR · dynamic visual acuity > five lines lost = bilateral loss
  • Romberg, tandem, Unterberger, past-pointing: fall + past-point towards the slow phase · cerebellar hemisphere: dysmetria, dysdiadochokinesia, rebound · midline: wide gait, truncal ataxia

Jongkees

canal paresis = [(RW + RC) − (LW + LC)] ÷ (RW + RC + LW + LC) × 100
e.g. RW 30, RC 28, LW 12, LC 10 → (58 − 22) ÷ 80 × 100 = 45% left canal paresis (cut-off ~20–25%)
DP: right-beating (RW + LC) vs left-beating (LW + RC)
  • Paresis >~15–30% · DP >~25–30% (Lee: questionable value)
  • Very small total (<~6–8°/s each) = bilateral weakness → chair / vHIT
  • Fixation fails to suppress = central
  • Paresis + DP away → Ménière's? · DP towards same side → schwannoma?
  • Kobrak: iced water from 5 mL; no response = dead labyrinth
Perforation → air, never water
ENG / VNG central signs: direction-changing nystagmus, no fixation suppression, abnormal saccades + pursuit, hyperactive calorics

4vHIT, VEMP, chair

  • vHIT gain = eye ÷ head velocity · normal ≥ ~0.8 lateral, ~0.7 vertical · weak canal: low gain + overt (visible) / covert (video only) saccades
  • Ménière's: abnormal calorics, normal vHIT · normal vHIT in acute continuous vertigo = think central

5When to order

side / site unclear · before a destructive procedure (check other ear) · plan + track rehab · gentamicin (daily head impulse + visual acuity) · Bárány ICVD gives the definitions

6Exam traps ✗

  • Water calorics with a perforation
  • Normal tests taken as no vestibular disease
  • Eyes tested only with fixation → central signs missed
