ORAL ULCERS, WHITE PATCHES & SUBMUCOUS FIBROSIS

most sore mouths are harmless · not healed in three weeks once the cause is gone → biopsy ✎

1Sorting the ulcer

History: duration, recurrence, pain, tobacco, areca, alcohol, dentures, drugs, skin + eye + genital lesions · examine every subsite, palpate for induration, feel the neck
GroupExamples

Biopsy: ulcer > three weeks after the cause is removed · rolled or indurated edge · fixed · numbness · hard neck node
NICE NG12: urgent referral for oral ulcer > three weeks, persistent lump, red or red-and-white patch

2Viral

  • Herpangina (Coxsackie A, child): vesicles on pillars, tonsils, soft palate, uvula → 2–4 mm ulcers, yellow base, red areola · within a week
  • Primary herpes (child): fever, vesicles anywhere, swollen bleeding gums · one to two weeks
  • Recurrent herpes (adult): trigeminal ganglion · stress, fever, sunlight · labialis, hard palate, attached gingiva · early aciclovir
  • Hand, foot + mouth: palate, tongue, cheek ulcers + vesicles on hands, feet

3Bacterial

  • Vincent (ANUG): fusiform bacillus + Borrelia vincentii · interdental papillae → gum margins, grey slough, foul smell · tonsil = Vincent angina · smear · penicillin or erythromycin + metronidazole · malnourished child → noma
  • TB (painful undermined, tongue), syphilis, actinomycosis (sulphur granules): biopsy vs cancer

5Aphthous ulcers

  • Commonest non-traumatic · on movable mucosa · spare hard palate + attached gingiva
TypeSize · course

  • B12, folate, iron, stress, hormones, food, coeliac, Crohn's, NSAIDs, nicorandil

6Immune and skin

  • Behçet: punched-out oral ulcers + genital ulcers + uveitis · skin, joints, CNS
  • Erythema multiforme: herpes or drugs (sulphonamides, anticonvulsants) · haemorrhagic lip crusts, skin target lesions · severe → Stevens–Johnson · steroids if severe
  • Lichen planus (T-cell): reticular = lacy Wickham striae, both cheeks, no treatment · erosive = painful, keratotic edge, topical steroid · erosive + atrophic: small cancer risk → follow up

7Where the blister splits

  • Pemphigus vulgaris: anti-desmoglein 3 · fragile blisters → painful erosions, often before the skin · fifty to seventy · systemic steroids + immunosuppression
  • Mucous membrane pemphigoid: gums, cheek, palate · conjunctival scarring · steroids + dapsone

8Blood, drugs, trauma

  • Leukaemia swells + ulcerates gums · agranulocytosis, cyclic neutropenia · film, counts, marrow
  • Mucositis (RT, methotrexate, fluorouracil, bleomycin): from about the second week · supportive
  • Jagged tooth, denture, pencil, acid, hot fluid, aspirin held on a tooth · smooth or adjust, look again in two to three weeks · not healed → biopsy

9Submucous fibrosis

  • Joshi, India 1953 · areca (betel) nut essential · IARC group 1 carcinogen
  • T-cells + macrophages → more collagen, less collagenase · avascular, atrophic epithelium
  • Twenty to forty · chilli intolerance, burning, vesicles → blanched stiff palate + pillars, fibrous bands, progressive trismus
  • Cancer ~3–7.6% (Lee 7–30%, meta-analysis ~5%) · trismus hides it → biopsy anything suspicious
  • Stop areca + tobacco · intralesional dexamethasone + hyaluronidase twice a week, eight to ten weeks · jaw exercises · surgery: release + graft or flap, coronoidectomy · recurrence common

11Jaw cysts

  • Dentigerous: crown of unerupted 3rd molar or upper canine · unilocular · marsupialise large ones · ameloblastoma in wall
  • Radicular: apex of a dead tooth · lining left → residual cyst

12Exam traps ✗

  • Repeated mouthwashes for a persistent single ulcer, no biopsy
  • White patch that won't rub off treated as thrush
  • Ulcers on hard palate or attached gingiva called aphthous: think herpes
  • Missing early cancer behind submucous fibrosis + trismus
