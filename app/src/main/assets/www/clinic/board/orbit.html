PROPTOSIS & THE ORBIT

thin walls shared with three sinuses · direction, speed and side point to the cause ✎

1The eye pushed forward

  • Proptosis = eye forward · exophthalmos often for thyroid eye disease
  • Sinusitis, mucocele, fungal, polyps, angiofibroma, inverted papilloma, cancer invade it · ESS can injure it · the nose can decompress + drain it
  • Hertel: difference > 2 mm or reading above ~20 mm = abnormal · exclude pseudoproptosis (myopia, lid retraction, small other eye)
  • Four-sided pyramid: medial wall = lamina papyracea (ethmoids) · floor = antrum · roof = frontal + anterior fossa · apex beside sphenoid + optic canal
  • Periosteum + septum limit infection · valveless ophthalmic veins → cavernous sinus

9Sinusitis: drain or not?

Children: start medical · IV antibiotics for S. anginosus (milleri) + decongestant · small medial abscess, normal vision often settles · check vision, colour, pupils, movements every few hours

10Retrobulbar bleed & DCR

Pain, tense eye, hard globe, ophthalmoplegia, falling vision after injury or ESS → lateral canthotomy + cantholysis now, then ophthalmology · no waiting for a scan
DCR for epiphora (nasolacrimal duct block): window from the sac into the middle meatus through lacrimal bone + frontal process of maxilla · endoscopic or external ± silicone tubes

11Exam traps ✗

  • Waiting for a scan in retrobulbar haemorrhage
  • Missing a sinus cause of one-sided proptosis
  • Forgetting colour vision + pupils
