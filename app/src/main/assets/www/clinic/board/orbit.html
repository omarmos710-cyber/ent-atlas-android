PROPTOSIS & THE ORBIT

thin walls shared with three sinuses · direction, speed and side point to the cause ✎

1The eye pushed forward

  • Proptosis = eye forward · exophthalmos often for thyroid eye disease
  • Hertel: difference > 2 mm or reading above ~20 mm = abnormal · exclude pseudoproptosis (myopia, lid retraction, small other eye)
  • Pyramid: medial wall = lamina papyracea (ethmoids) · floor = antrum · roof = frontal + anterior fossa · apex beside sphenoid + optic canal
  • Periosteum + septum limit infection · valveless ophthalmic veins → cavernous sinus

3Examine together

  • Joint ENT + eye assessment · acuity, colour vision, pupils (RAPD = optic nerve), movements, diplopia, degree + direction, lids, cornea, fundus · nasal endoscopy
  • CT: bone, sinuses, fractures, muscles · MRI: soft tissue, optic nerve, apex, intracranial
  • Thyroid function + antibodies · vasculitis, lymphoma bloods · biopsy endoscopically or by orbitotomy

9Evidence

  • Chandler (1970) still standard · Garcia + Harris (2000), EPOS 2020: small medial abscess → medical first
  • Trauma optic neuropathy: IONTS 1999 no gain from steroids or decompression · CRASH 2004: high-dose steroids ↑ death → avoided
  • Thyroid: EUGOGO 2021 IV methylprednisolone, decompress if steroids fail · teprotumumab ↓ proptosis (2020)

10Retrobulbar haemorrhage

Tense eye, hard globe, ophthalmoplegia, falling vision after injury or ESS → lateral canthotomy + cantholysis now, then ophthalmology · no waiting for a scan

11Exam traps ✗

  • Waiting for a scan in retrobulbar haemorrhage
  • Missing a sinus cause of one-sided proptosis
  • Forgetting colour vision + pupils
