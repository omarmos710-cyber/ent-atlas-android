APPLIED ANATOMY OF THE ORAL CAVITY

a small room with many tenants · know each subsite and where its lymph goes ✎

1Where the mouth ends

  • From the vermilion border in front to the oropharyngeal isthmus behind
  • Isthmus: hard–soft palate junction above · circumvallate papillae below · anterior pillars + retromolar trigones at the sides
  • Behind the ring = oropharynx: soft palate, tonsils, base of tongue, valleculae · staged + treated differently
  • Functions: intake + chewing, oral phase of swallowing, speech, protection (saliva, taste, gag)

3The lips

  • Vermilion: non-keratinised, no hair or sebaceous glands · red from capillaries
  • All muscles VII · orbicularis oris = sphincter · modiolus at commissure: orbicularis, buccinator, levator + depressor anguli oris, zygomaticus major, risorius
  • Sensation: upper infraorbital (V2), lower mental (V3) · superior + inferior labial arteries (facial)
  • Lower lip lymph → submandibular, midline submental, may cross · upper → submandibular, preauricular, infraparotid

4Teeth and jaws

  • Enamel (ameloblasts) → dentine (odontoblasts, lifelong, sensitive) → pulp · cementum + periodontal ligament anchor the root
  • Twenty deciduous (no premolars) · thirty-two permanent: per quadrant two incisors, one canine, two premolars, three molars (third = wisdom)
  • Attached gingiva bound to bone · looser alveolar mucosa beyond

5Tongue surface

  • Sulcus terminalis + V of circumvallate papillae: oral tongue in front, base behind
  • Foramen caecum at the apex: thyroid descends from here → lingual thyroid, thyroglossal cyst
  • Lingual tonsil + valleculae behind = oropharynx

7Nerves of the tongue

  • Chorda tympani → geniculate ganglion → nucleus solitarius · sweet, sour, salty, bitter, umami · flavour = taste + smell
  • Referred otalgia: lingual + auriculotemporal both V3 · IX supplies the middle ear (Jacobson's) → tongue or tonsil cancer = earache, normal ear
  • Lingual artery (2nd branch of ECA) · lingual vein + vein with XII

8Palate and floor

  • Hard palate: palatine processes of maxillae + horizontal plates of palatine bones · mucoperiosteum bound to bone
  • Incisive foramen: nasopalatine · greater palatine (medial to 2nd/3rd molar): pedicle of palatal flaps · lesser palatine → soft palate
  • Sensation: nasopalatine + greater palatine (V2) · greater palatine artery
  • Mylohyoid = diaphragm · above: sublingual glands, Wharton duct, lingual nerve looping beneath the duct, XII · below: submandibular space

9Saliva

  • One to one and a half litres a day · rest: two-thirds submandibular · stimulated: over half parotid
  • Stensen: opposite upper 2nd molar · Wharton: beside frenulum · sublingual: many small ducts
  • Minor glands in lips, cheeks, palate, tongue → the palate's salivary tumours

11Viva essentials

  • Boundaries + subsites · tongue nerves: sensation + taste, front + back
  • Extrinsic muscles + the test of XII (protrude: deviation, wasting, fasciculation)
  • Lymph of lip, tongue, floor · referred otalgia

12Exam traps ✗

  • Calling base of tongue or tonsil "oral cavity"
  • Forgetting lateral tongue can skip to level IV
  • Inspecting the floor without palpating it
  • Forgetting the anterior floor + lower lip cross the midline
  • Palatoglossus is X, not XII · deviation is towards the lesion
