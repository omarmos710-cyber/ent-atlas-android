2Diagnostic endoscopy: three passes

For every chronic nasal complaint · decongestant + 4% lignocaine · 4 mm 0° or 30° scope
PassRoute · sees
1along the floor → nasopharynx: IT, meatus, ET, fossa of Rosenmüller
2between MT + IT → sphenoethmoidal recess, superior meatus
3under MT into middle meatus: uncinate, bulla, hiatus, accessory ostia

3Antral puncture

  • Chronic maxillary sinusitis, culture, cytology · not in acute
  • Lichtwitz trocar through the inferior meatus 1.5–2 cm behind the front of the IT, aimed at the same-side ear · warm saline until clear
  • Amber + cholesterol = cyst · mucopus blobs = hyperplastic · foul pus = suppuration · risks: cheek, orbit, posterior wall, air embolism (never blow air in)

4Caldwell-Luc

  • Inferior meatal antrostomy = obsolete: cilia carry mucus to the natural ostium, not the new hole
  • Caldwell-Luc: sublabial, anterior wall at the canine fossa + nasoantral window
  • Uses: roots, foreign bodies, dental cysts, oroantral fistula, antral biopsy, recurrent antrochoanal polyp, fractures · access to ethmoids (Horgan), PPF
  • Not under about 17: tooth buds in the anterior wall
  • Bleeding, numb cheek + teeth, nasolacrimal injury, sublabial fistula, osteomyelitis · now: anterior + lateral recess disease, combined approaches

6Oroantral fistula

  • Second premolar / molar extraction (apical abscess) · failed Caldwell-Luc wound · cancer, fracture, osteitis
  • Drinks come out of the nose, foul discharge, can't use a straw · probe passes into the antrum
  • Fresh, small: suture + antibiotics · chronic / large: treat the sinus, excise the track, buccal advancement or palatal rotation flap

7Endoscopic sinus surgery

Functional: clear the OMC, widen the natural ostia, keep mucosa → ventilation + clearance return
For: CRS after failed medicine, recurrent acute, complications (orbital abscess, mucocele), fungal, antrochoanal polyp · access: CSF leak, orbit / optic nerve decompression, DCR, SPA ligation, atresia, tumours, skull base

Read the CT first: CLOSE

Cribriform · Lamina papyracea · Onodi cell · Sphenoid + carotid · Ethmoidal artery
medialise MT→uncinectomy→antrostomy at the natural ostium→bullectomy→basal lamella → posterior ethmoids→frontal recess if diseased→sphenoidotomy ~1 cm above choana
Messerklinger-Stammberger = front to back · Wigand = sphenoid first, along the skull base forward (polyposis, revisions)

8The frontal sinus: Draf

width of the opening, coronal schematic · dashed = lamina papyracea
  • I recess cleared · IIa lamina → MT · IIb lamina → septum · III = modified Lothrop, both sinuses joined
  • Ostium: lateral to MT, medial to orbit, in front of the AEA, behind agger nasi · spare mucosa
  • External: trephine, Lynch-Howarth, osteoplastic flap + obliteration, cranialisation

10Who benefits

  • RAND minimum (2016): Lund-Mackay ≥ 1 + ≥ eight weeks nasal steroid + short oral steroid (polyps) or antibiotic (no polyps) + SNOT-22 ≥ 20 after it
  • Up to one in three gain little · preoperative SNOT-22 predicts benefit best
  • Revisions: residual uncinate, missed natural ostium (recirculation), anterior ethmoid cells · ~11% at three years, 19% at five, 17% at ten
  • MACRO 2025: surgery > placebo at six months · NAIROS 2024: septoplasty 20 SNOT-22 points better · balloon: no good evidence (Cochrane 2011)

11Exam traps ✗

  • Operating without reading the CT
  • A second maxillary hole that misses the natural ostium
  • Stripping mucosa in the frontal recess
