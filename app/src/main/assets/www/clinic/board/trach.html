TRACHEOSTOMY & AIRWAY ENDOSCOPY

orderly on an intubated patient, dangerous on a struggling one · most deaths come later, on the ward ✎

1What it does

  • Bypasses obstruction lips → stoma
  • Cuts dead space 30–50% (normal ~150 mL), lowers resistance
  • Protects (cuff) against secretions + blood · allows pharyngeal packing
  • Suction when cough fails or hurts
  • Prolonged ventilation beyond a week or two (older texts: 72 hours; TracMan: early didn't cut mortality) · anaesthesia when intubation impossible

2Which ring

  • High acceptable only before a likely laryngectomy (stoma excised)
  • Low: thyroid ima (≤ 5%), high innominate
  • Emergency · elective · permanent · percutaneous · mini / cricothyroidotomy

3The open operation

  • Intubate first if possible · shoulder roll (not with spine injury) · LA + adrenaline or GA
  • Vertical (emergency) or 4–5 cm horizontal two fingerbreadths above notch
  • Midline · anterior jugulars · split straps · retract or divide isthmus (keeping it is fine)
  • Lidocaine into trachea · cricoid hook · open rings 2–4: slit, window, Björk flap, stay sutures
  • Withdraw ETT under vision → insert, cuff up, capnography → then remove ETT
  • Tapes ± sutures · no tight skin closure, no packing (emphysema)

4Children and percutaneous

  • GA over tube or bronchoscope · soft trachea, easily lost · lateral: RLN, carotid
  • Don't hyperextend (pleura, innominate, thymus rise) · stay sutures either side · vertical slit, no cartilage removed · blade not deep · soft right-sized tube, chest film
  • PDT: wire + dilators between rings 2–3 under bronchoscopy · quicker, cheaper, fewer infections · not with obesity, neck mass, poor extension, coagulopathy, children · misplacement, bleeding, posterior wall

6Aftercare

  • Up to half of in-hospital hypoxic airway deaths · mostly preventable · NTSP algorithms at every bed, bedhead sign, trained staff
  • Constant observation, call bell, writing pad · suction while withdrawing · humidify · clean inner cannula · cuff pressure
  • Don't change the outer tube for three to five days (no tract) · spare tubes, dilator, obturator, suction, O₂ at the bed
Sudden distress: remove valve + inner cannula → suction catheter · won't pass → cuff down, remove tube → O₂ to stoma + face → intubate from above (not a laryngectomy)
  • Decannulate early: deflate → smaller uncuffed → cap 24 hours (longer in children) → out, tape · infants in hospital
  • Failure: original disease, suprastomal granuloma, subglottic stenosis, anterior wall collapse, malacia, dependence → endoscopy

7Complications

  • At operation: bleeding · apnoea after relieving chronic obstruction (CO₂ drive gone) · pneumothorax (children) · RLN, oesophagus · false passage
  • Early: displacement + blockage = commonest killers · emphysema, crusting, pneumonia
  • Tracheo-innominate fistula < 1%, kills > 80% · sentinel bleed → act · overinflate cuff or finger through stoma against sternum → theatre
  • Stenosis (high trach, cuff > 30 cmH₂O, intubation > ten days, big tubes) · TOF · granulation · fistula · scar

8Buying time

jaw thrust→oro/naso airway→LMA→intubate
  • LMA doesn't help obstruction at or below the glottis
  • CICO: scalpel cricothyroidotomy (stab, rotate, bougie, 6 mm cuffed tube) · convert within ~72 hours · not under ten to twelve
  • Needle + jet ventilation: small children · allow expiration (barotrauma)
  • Emergency trach: hazardous · intubate first if at all possible

9Laryngoscopy, bronchoscopy

  • DL: hidden areas (tongue base, valleculae, lower piriform, infrahyoid epiglottis, commissure, ventricles, subglottis) · biopsy, treat · not with spine disease or uncontrolled obstruction
  • Boyce (sniffing) position: head up 10–15 cm, neck flexed, head extended · right of tongue → midline · lift, don't lever
  • Rigid bronchoscopy: ventilates, big biopsies, bleeding · FB of choice · head left for right bronchus · ~twenty minutes max in children
  • Flexible: topical, bedside, segmental · poor for bleeding · not for children's FBs

10Evidence

  • NTSP algorithms (2012) · NCEPOD (2014): bedhead signs, capnography, trained staff
  • TracMan (909, 2013): ≤ four days vs ≥ ten: same 30-day mortality
  • PDT: fewer infections + scars (2016)

11Exam traps ✗

  • High tracheostomy through the first ring
  • Changing the tube on day one, no tract or stay sutures
  • Tight skin sutures round the tube
  • Leaving a cricothyroidotomy for weeks
