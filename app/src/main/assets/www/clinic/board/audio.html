3Pure-tone audiogram

250–8000 Hz, air (headphones) then bone (mastoid vibrator) · worse = lower on the chart · right red ○, left blue ✕ · bone < (R) > (L), [ ] masked · arrow = no response at max (~120 dB air, only 60–70 bone)
check chart, masking, reliability→air: ≤20 dB normal; degree; shape→bone = cochlea→compare ears→fits the clinic?
  • Bone = air: SN · bone better than air by >10 dB: conductive · both raised + gap: mixed
  • Gap max ~60 dB from the middle ear (discontinuity, intact drum) · bigger → wrong bone or mixed
  • Bone better than normal → third window (SCD): apparent conductive, normal tymp, reflexes present
  • Asymmetry ≥15 dB at two adjacent frequencies, or 20 dB at one → MRI IAM
  • Repeat agrees within ~5 dB → change ≥10 dB at two frequencies is real · grade by 500/1000/2000/4000 Hz better ear

4Masking

interaural attenuation 40–65 dB air (more with inserts), ~0 for bone · mask when ears differ ≥40 dB on air, and for bone whenever there's a gap · plateau (Hood): raise noise in 10 dB steps until the threshold stops moving · unmasked dead ear → shadow curve (40–60 dB below the good ear on air, near-level on bone) · masking dilemma: big bilateral conductive (atresia) → inserts, bone ABR

6Speech audiometry

  • SRT: half of spondees correct; within 10 dB of the PTA
  • Discrimination 30–40 dB above SRT: good conductive · ↓ cochlear · very poor retrocochlear
  • Curve: conductive shifted but reaches 100% · cochlear shifted, lower maximum · retrocochlear poor maximum + roll-over
  • Predicts aid benefit, selects implant candidates

9Objective tests

  • OAE (outer hair cells): lost >~30 dB (transient), >~50 dB (distortion product) · absent with fluid / debris · present in auditory neuropathy · pass / refer (≠ deaf: vernix, noise) · early noise / cisplatin damage
  • ABR: five to seven waves within 10 ms · E. COLI: I–II VIII nerve, III cochlear nucleus, IV superior olive, V lateral lemniscus + inferior colliculus · infant pass ~30–40 dB nHL · unaffected by sleep, sedation · misses small tumours
ABRFinding
normalV at ~5.5–6 ms · I–III ~2, III–V ~2, I–V ~4 ms (independent of middle ear)
conductiveall waves delayed, I–V normal
cochlearI small / absent; V latency falls steeply with loudness
retrocochlearI–V prolonged or later waves lost; V delayed vs other ear (Dhingra >0.2 ms, Lee 0.4–0.5)
  • ASSR: frequency-specific into the profound range (beyond ABR ~80 dB)
  • ECochG: raised SP/AP (cut-off ~0.3 to 0.4–0.5 by text) → hydrops; normal doesn't exclude
  • Auditory neuropathy: OAE + cochlear microphonic present, ABR absent / abnormal

11Exam traps ✗

  • False-negative Rinne called conductive · forgetting to mask
  • Flat tymp called fluid without the volume
  • A shadow curve taken as a real loss
  • Stapes surgery for a gap with present reflexes (third window?)
  • A "severe" loss in someone following quiet talk
