CANCER OF THE LARYNX

on the fold: hoarse early, no lymphatics, often cured · above or below: silent, nodes, late ✎

1Who and why

  • Second commonest upper aerodigestive cancer, ~1–5% of all · men four to eight times · sixth–seventh decades · UK ~2,400 a year, deprived areas
  • Glottic ~60% · supraglottic ~35–40% · subglottic 1–2%
  • Tobacco (ten- to twentyfold, falls after ten years off) · alcohol (supraglottic; with tobacco ~fifteenfold) · neck RT, papillomas, asbestos, nickel, wood dust, reflux
  • Dysplasia progresses: mild–moderate ~7–11%, severe 18–30% (≈ CIS) · excisional biopsy = diagnosis + treatment · lifelong follow-up
  • SCC 85–95% · verrucous 1–2%: pushing, no nodes → excise, not RT · others: spindle, basaloid, neuroendocrine, salivary, chondrosarcoma (cricoid), lymphoma

2Regions decide behaviour

  • Conus + quadrangular membrane resist · pre-epiglottic (via epiglottic pits) + paraglottic spaces carry it silently → transglottic
  • Broyles' ligament: no perichondrium at the anterior commissure → into cartilage

4See it, map it

  • Hoarse > two to three weeks at cancer age → laryngoscopy · NBI, office biopsy · stroboscopy: stiff segment
  • Neck: swelling over cricothyroid membrane, tender or widened cartilage, nodes
  • CT neck (spaces, cartilage, nodes) · MRI (cartilage, recurrence) · CT chest (nodule = second primary?) · PET-CT advanced + at 10–12 weeks after CRT · US-FNA
  • Image before biopsy if airway safe · SLT before decisions
  • DL: hidden areas (infrahyoid epiglottis, anterior commissure, ventricle, subglottis) · panendoscopy (synchronous 1–6%)

6Early cancer: one modality

  • Stage I–II: RT or surgery, similar control · choose by voice, swallow, access, fitness, preference
  • RT 60–70 Gy over six weeks · T1 glottic ≥ ~90%, T2 ~85% mobile, ~63–70% if impaired · mucositis, chondronecrosis, can't repeat
  • TLM (CO2) / TORS: cordectomy, supraglottic resection · a day, cheaper, no trach, RT kept in reserve · voice poorer after wide cordectomy · not if fixed, interarytenoid, poor lungs

7Total laryngectomy

  • Whole larynx + hyoid + pre-epiglottic space · pharynx closed · end stoma
  • T3–T4, cartilage invasion, both arytenoids, posterior commissure, transglottic + fixed
  • Failed RT or conservation · non-functioning larynx with aspiration
  • ± neck dissection, post-op RT · not with distant metastases

8Organ preservation

  • VA Larynx (1991): induction cisplatin + 5-FU → RT = survival of laryngectomy · two-thirds keep larynx
  • RTOG 91-11: concurrent cisplatin best preservation + control · no survival difference at ten years (2013)
  • CRT standard for many T3 + node-positive T2 · cetuximab or carboplatin if no cisplatin
  • But survival fell as practice left surgery (2006) · salvage laryngectomy after CRT: high complications
NICE NG36: offer T3 a choice · T4a: surgery first · also favour surgery: dead larynx, trach, patient wish

11Exam traps ✗

  • Antibiotics + voice rest for a smoker's hoarseness
  • No DL: commissure, ventricle, subglottis missed
  • One-sided neck in supraglottic cancer
  • Organ preservation for T4a through cartilage
