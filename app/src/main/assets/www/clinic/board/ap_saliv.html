2Red flags

  • Facial weakness + parotid lump
  • Pain, rapid growth, fixity, skin ulcer, trismus · neck nodes
  • Lump in submandibular, sublingual, minor glands
  • Tonsil pushed medially → deep lobe
  • Sjögren's + new persistent swelling → lymphoma

3History and exam

  • Duration, fluctuation, meals, pain, one or many · bad taste, pus · dry eyes/mouth, joints · B symptoms · alcohol, diabetes · anticholinergics, diuretics · RT · HIV · face and scalp skin cancers
  • Palpate; bimanual for submandibular + Wharton's duct stone
  • Stensen's opposite upper second molar · Wharton's beside frenulum · massage: clear, cloudy or pus
  • Every VII branch · oropharynx · neck · skin

4Smaller gland, worse odds

  • Most tumours in the parotid · pleomorphic adenoma → Warthin's (older male smokers, sometimes bilateral)
  • Malignant: mucoepidermoid (commonest), adenoid cystic (perineural), acinic, salivary duct, ca ex PA · parotid node metastases from cSCC, melanoma
  • Benign: superficial/partial parotidectomy or extracapsular dissection, VII preserved · malignant → MDT

5Investigate

  • Ultrasound first: stones, dilatation, sialectasis, inflammation, cysts, masses · guides FNA or core
  • Milan system grades malignancy risk · core if inconclusive or lymphoma
  • MRI: extent, deep lobe, perineural · CT: stones, bone · (MR) sialography · sialendoscopy diagnoses and treats
  • Bloods: inflammatory markers, anti-Ro/La, IgG4, ACE, HIV

6Stones and infection

  • 80–90% submandibular: alkaline, mucous saliva, duct runs uphill · meal-time syndrome
  • Near opening: transoral · up to about 4–5 mm: sialendoscope basket · larger: laser, lithotripsy, combined · gland excision for failure
  • Parotid: more often strictures → endoscopic dilatation
  • Bacterial (S. aureus): fluids, sialogogues, massage, hygiene, antibiotics, drain abscess · mumps: supportive · JRP settles by puberty

7Systemic

  • Sjögren's: dry eyes + mouth, parotid swelling, MALT lymphoma risk (ACR/EULAR 2016)
  • IgG4: firm bilateral submandibular (Küttner), steroids (criteria 2019)
  • Sarcoid: + uveitis, fever, VII palsy = Heerfordt's
  • HIV: lymphoepithelial cysts · sialosis: alcohol, diabetes, malnutrition, drugs

8Pathway

Meals → US, sialendoscopy · acute + pus → fluids, antibiotics, drain · bilateral → Sjögren's, IgG4, sarcoid, HIV bloods Discrete lump → US + FNA/core · MRI if malignant, deep, fixed Red flags → treat as malignant, MDT · benign → parotidectomy, ECD or observe Sjögren's + persistent swelling → biopsy for lymphoma

9Exam traps ✗

  • Open biopsy of a parotid lump (seeding, VII)
  • Skin primary missed behind a parotid node
  • Gland removed for a stone that could come out endoscopically
